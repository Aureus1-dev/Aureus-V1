# PA-022 — Private Steward & Visual Flourishing Experience

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Founder-approved experience architecture candidate; not canonical until governed review and merge  
**Founder direction:** 2026-09-01  
**Parent architecture:** PA-021 — Aureus Outcome & Responsibility Architecture

## 1. Purpose

PA-021 defines how Aureus carries Responsibility to verified outcomes.

PA-022 defines how that relationship should **feel** and how it should be visually expressed.

The core emotional product truth is:

> Aureus should feel like your private AI Steward: yours, trusted, protected, continuous, and devoted to helping you or your business flourish as much as responsibly possible.

This is not a claim that Aureus is literally inaccessible to every other actor under every technical, operational, legal, or security circumstance.

The engineering promise is stronger because it is precise:

- private by default;
- least privilege;
- explicit principal/context boundaries;
- user-controlled personal memory and life moments;
- narrow, auditable operational access where legitimately required;
- no silent cross-tenant or cross-context disclosure;
- no use of private Stewardship data as involuntary shared intelligence;
- no business access to later personal Steward content merely because the relationship began on a business surface.

## 2. Product feeling

A member should feel:

- **This is mine.**
- **This is private.**
- **This knows me in the right way.**
- **This remembers what I intentionally entrusted to it.**
- **This carries work with me.**
- **This shows me that my life is moving.**
- **This helps me become more capable, stable, connected, and fulfilled.**

A business should feel the equivalent:

- this Steward understands the business;
- protects tenant and customer boundaries;
- carries work without making the team operate unnecessary machinery;
- helps the company, employees, and customers flourish;
- reflects legitimate progress, improvement, and achievement.

The experience must never rely on manufactured dependency, forced engagement, or surveillance aesthetics.

## 3. Private belonging

The design should communicate private belonging without using technically false absolute claims.

Preferred meaning:

> "This is your Steward and your space. Information is handled for your benefit under clear privacy and authority boundaries."

Not:

> "No human, system, administrator, or legal process could ever access any data under any circumstance."

Privacy should be visible through product behavior:

- clear current context;
- clear sharing boundaries;
- clear memory controls;
- explicit consent for cross-context transfer;
- clear audience for saved moments/documents;
- easy correction and deletion where applicable;
- no unexplained resurfacing of sensitive information;
- no business/private crossover.

## 4. Principal-aware identity

Aureus can remain one recognizable public Steward while serving different principals.

The same human may encounter Aureus as:

- personal member;
- customer;
- employee;
- owner;
- family participant;
- authorized representative.

The visual identity may remain continuous, but each experience must make the current relationship legible when it matters.

Same voice does not mean same permissions.

The user should never have to infer whether the current conversation belongs to:

- personal/private space;
- a business tenant;
- a shared transaction;
- an employer relationship;
- another authorized context.

## 5. The visual objective

The interface should not communicate:

> "Look how much Aureus knows about you."

It should communicate:

> "Look how well your life, work, or business is being cared for and how it is moving toward flourishing."

The visual experience should reflect:

- care;
- continuity;
- dignity;
- progress;
- memory;
- achievement;
- possibility;
- calm competence;
- hospitality.

Avoid:

- surveillance dashboards;
- noisy productivity metrics;
- gamification for engagement;
- sterile CRM aesthetics;
- cyberpunk/agent-control-room metaphors;
- visual clutter that makes the user operate Aureus.

## 6. Five visual dimensions of a personal Steward

### 6.1 Responsibilities being carried

The foreground should make current work legible:

- what Aureus is carrying;
- what is progressing;
- what is blocked;
- what is waiting externally;
- what needs the member now;
- what has just completed.

The experience should prefer:

> "I handled these things. I need you here."

over a giant task queue.

### 6.2 Growth over time

Aureus should be able to reflect meaningful movement such as:

- job secured;
- certification completed;
- income increased;
- debt reduced;
- benefit secured;
- housing stabilized;
- business launched;
- recurring burden removed;
- new skill practiced;
- important project completed.

This is evidence of flourishing, not a points system.

### 6.3 Life and family moments

When the member intentionally chooses to preserve them, Aureus may hold meaningful moments such as:

- birthdays;
- graduations;
- first days;
- family celebrations;
- major wins;
- home milestones;
- anniversaries;
- first sale;
- first hire;
- completed project;
- a note about why a goal matters.

These moments must be:

- opt-in or clearly user-directed;
- audience-scoped;
- correctable/removable;
- never silently inferred as "memories" from sensitive conversation;
- never shared into a business/employer context without explicit authority.

### 6.4 What matters

Aureus may reflect the member's intentionally expressed:

- priorities;
- family responsibilities;
- values;
- long-term aims;
- current season of life;
- things they are protecting or building.

The purpose is to improve care and continuity, not construct a manipulative psychological profile.

### 6.5 Future possibility

The visual environment should make progress and possibility visible:

- what is in motion;
- what is preparing;
- what becomes possible after the current step;
- relevant upcoming milestones;
- new opportunities that are verified or appropriately qualified.

Hope should come from grounded forward motion, not fabricated optimism.

## 7. Spatial metaphors

Aureus may draw from several compatible metaphors without becoming literal theme-park UI:

### Private study
Trust, reflection, dignity, focus.

### Hearth
Return, hospitality, care, warmth.

### Living gallery
Meaningful growth and milestones, intentionally preserved.

### Steward's desk
Current Responsibilities, evidence, decisions, and what needs the human.

### Garden
Cultivation, time, growth, flourishing.

These are design principles, not requirements to render literal furniture, rooms, or plants.

## 8. Outcome Surface integration

Conversation remains home.

The visual environment should compose itself around the active Responsibility.

Possible work surfaces include:

- project imagery;
- comparisons;
- documents;
- maps;
- calendars;
- forms requiring a human step;
- financial/value views;
- progress evidence;
- external-system information;
- milestones and growth;
- intentionally saved moments.

Rule:

> The person should not have to travel through software when the relevant software state can responsibly come to the person.

The Outcome Surface must remain server/governance-owned where action or evidence is consequential; raw model output must not become executable UI authority.

## 9. Stewardship timeline

History should evolve beyond a transcript.

A useful long-term Stewardship Timeline may contain:

- conversations;
- accepted Responsibilities;
- commitments;
- major decisions;
- verified outcomes;
- meaningful milestones;
- intentionally preserved life/business moments;
- service recovery;
- important lessons.

The primary story is:

> "Here is what has been carried and how life/business has moved."

not:

> "Here are thousands of chat messages."

Transcript remains available as appropriate evidence/history but is not the sole continuity object.

## 10. Business visual flourishing

A business Steward should reflect legitimate organizational flourishing, including:

- projects completed;
- customer outcomes;
- revenue created or protected;
- margin/cost improvements;
- time returned to employees;
- team achievements;
- employee growth;
- process improvements;
- quality improvements;
- customer praise/recovery;
- meaningful community contribution;
- capability maturity.

Do not turn this into employee surveillance or a scoreboard that rewards harmful extraction.

Employee-facing experiences should reduce avoidable burden and support effectiveness, learning, and dignity.

## 11. Memory and consent

Aureus should ask for memory when memory creates clear benefit and the boundary is meaningful.

Good pattern:

> "Do you want me to remember this for you?"

Memory categories may eventually distinguish:

- preference;
- enduring fact;
- relationship/context;
- responsibility state;
- milestone;
- life moment;
- document/evidence reference.

Do not store private chain-of-thought or speculative personality diagnoses.

Do not automatically promote conversation content into durable personal memory merely because it may be useful someday.

## 12. Accessibility and tempo

Visual flourishing must work for people with different abilities, devices, attention, and emotional states.

Requirements include:

- keyboard and screen-reader support;
- reduced-motion support;
- mobile-first behavior;
- readable contrast and typography;
- no important meaning conveyed only by animation;
- calm fallback when media cannot load;
- user control over sensory richness where needed.

Service pace should adapt to:

- urgency;
- consequence;
- complexity;
- emotional load;
- reversibility.

A crisis or urgent bill problem should not be forced through a sentimental visual experience.

## 13. Human attention

The private Steward experience should preserve attention.

The interface should minimize avoidable:

- repeated explanations;
- app switching;
- hunting for status;
- notifications without action value;
- decorative interruptions;
- unnecessary choices;
- forced profile maintenance.

The visual layer should make the next meaningful truth obvious.

## 14. Privacy-safe celebration

Aureus may celebrate genuine milestones, but celebration must be proportional and user-centered.

Do:

- acknowledge meaningful progress;
- show continuity;
- connect current achievement to the member's stated goals;
- make it easy to save/share privately or with chosen people.

Do not:

- fabricate emotional intimacy;
- pressure the user to share;
- post externally without explicit action;
- create streak anxiety;
- turn hardship into engagement content.

## 15. Visual truth and uncertainty

A beautiful interface may not visually overstate certainty.

The Outcome Surface should distinguish, where material:

- verified;
- observed;
- reported;
- inferred;
- estimated;
- unknown;
- pending verification.

A confident visual treatment must not convert an estimate into a fact.

## 16. Relationship to data architecture

PA-022 does not authorize new schema by itself.

Before adding a dedicated milestone, life-moment, preference, or personal-memory table, implementation must evaluate reuse of existing:

- Conversation;
- Responsibility/ResponsibilityEvent;
- Journey/Goal/Milestone/Task;
- documents/media;
- consent;
- domain outcome records;
- future governed memory contracts.

The goal is a coherent experience, not a second social-network/profile data model.

## 17. Golden walkthrough acceptance

The Founder walkthrough should eventually prove, on a real exact deployment:

### Personal
- a member enters one calm Aureus relationship;
- an active Responsibility is visible without a task-dashboard feel;
- Aureus asks for the human only where required;
- a verified completion becomes visible as progress;
- a meaningful milestone can be intentionally preserved;
- the member can understand/control what is private, remembered, and shared;
- no business/private crossover occurs;
- mobile and voice continuity preserve the same relationship.

### Business
- owner sees what Aureus carried, what needs the team, and verified outcomes;
- customer experience remains hospitable and not manipulative;
- business/team progress is visible without surveillance;
- transaction and personal contexts remain correctly separated.

## 18. The Aureus visual test

Before shipping a major surface, ask:

1. Does this feel like a private Steward or generic software?
2. Does it reduce burden?
3. Does it make the active outcome clearer?
4. Does it reflect flourishing rather than data collection?
5. Is privacy/control legible?
6. Is uncertainty truthful?
7. Does it respect accessibility and emotional context?
8. Does it avoid gamification and manufactured dependency?
9. Does it preserve one coherent Aureus relationship?
10. If the visual treatment disappeared, would the underlying stewardship still be correct?

The interface is successful when the member can feel:

> "This is my place. Aureus is carrying this with me. I can see my life moving."

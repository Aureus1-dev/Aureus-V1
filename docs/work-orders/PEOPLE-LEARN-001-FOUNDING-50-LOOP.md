# PEOPLE-LEARN-001 — Founding 50 Conversational Stewardship Learning Loop

**Status:** Construction candidate  
**Parent:** Issue #128  
**Repository:** Aureus-V1  
**Branch:** `people/learn-001-founding-50-loop`

## Single job

Build the smallest complete People learning loop that allows Aureus to help real members at full strength, learn from meaningful real-world use, identify repeated needs/friction/outcomes, and provide privacy-safe observed evidence to Foundry for simulation and governed improvement.

The proof is:

`real conversation -> existing need/responsibility/help -> meaningful observed signal -> source evidence reference -> pattern-ready learning record/export -> Foundry analysis/simulation -> governed change candidate -> later field outcome`

## Product doctrine

- One conversational relationship: **How can we help?**
- Members are members first, collaborators second.
- Lean structure must never mean reduced capability. Deep capabilities such as Opportunity Center remain fully available when relevant.
- Reuse existing tools/source systems before replacing them.
- Aureus owns stewardship, intent, responsibility, permission, coordination, evidence, continuity, and learning; external systems remain authoritative for their own domain facts.
- Observe before standardizing.
- Simulation guides what is worth testing; observed outcomes determine what is true.
- Fix invisible friction silently where safe. Explain only material/consequential mistakes or where explanation helps the member.
- Respect member time: minimize unnecessary turns, screens, confirmations, notifications, and repeated questions.

## Founding 50

The first 50 real members are a discovery/calibration cohort, not a statistically representative population and not unpaid QA labor. Aureus should provide the strongest available help to each unique person while learning from the evidence produced by normal stewardship.

## Meaningful learning signals

Accept signals only when they are useful enough to justify institutional attention. Sources include:

- explicit member suggestion/correction;
- repeated friction detected from operational evidence;
- success, failure, or unexpected outcome;
- human Steward observation;
- prediction-vs-outcome mismatch;
- recurring unmet capability request.

Do not record exhaustive behavioral surveillance merely because data is available.

## Conversational feedback

No separate survey/dashboard is required for normal use. Feedback is optional and conversational. Aureus may occasionally ask a short context-appropriate question such as:

> Anything you wish I could handle better for you?

It must not ask after every interaction and should not announce trivial self-corrections that waste the member's time.

## Learning boundary

A single observation is not product truth. Similar observations may form an emerging pattern. Foundry may generate competing explanations and simulate candidate responses. No learning signal by itself grants authority, changes policy, or authorizes consequential action.

## Reuse-first inspection gate

Before adding schema/models, inspect and prefer:

- `Responsibility` / `ResponsibilityEvent`;
- conversation/message provenance;
- AI orchestration/audit records;
- authority/privacy gates;
- existing ledger/evidence semantics;
- existing analytics/telemetry/feedback primitives.

Do not add a second CRM, case system, generic workflow engine, duplicate evidence store, or parallel member history.

## Foundry contract

Observed evidence sent to Foundry must be distinguishable from:

- model inference;
- hypothesis;
- simulation;
- synthetic/adversarial test data.

Foundry may cluster, compare, simulate, and propose. It may not promote synthetic results into observed truth.

## Ledger rule

Material member-facing commitments, outcomes, consequential corrections, and product changes that affect what Aureus promised/carried should remain traceable through the existing evidence/ledger architecture. Trivial internal fixes should not create member-facing ledger noise.

## Relationship to PEOPLE-EXP-001

The existing 60 simulations remain useful as adversarial/pre-launch test material. They are not observed member evidence and should not become product truth merely because they were simulated.

## Done means — implementation slice 1

1. A meaningful observation can originate from a real People conversation without a forced survey.
2. The observation references canonical source evidence rather than copying unnecessary private content.
3. A member can conversationally offer a suggestion/correction.
4. At least one system-derived signal can come from existing operational evidence without relabeling inference as verified fact.
5. Signals are pattern-ready for Foundry while minimizing private payload exposure.
6. Signal creation cannot grant authority or execute a product/policy change.
7. Observed, reported, inferred, and simulated provenance remain distinguishable.
8. Material improvements can later trace to evidence and measured field outcome.
9. Existing Opportunity Center / People / Business capabilities are not reduced by this slice.
10. Existing privacy, authority, Responsibility, evidence, CI, and Docker gates remain green.

# PEOPLE-EXP-001 — Research Synthesis

**Parent:** #125 — PEOPLE-EXP-001: Aureus People Experience & Simulation Contract  
**Base:** `73ddb83e25ad53fd082d1a8409ff23182256fba2`  
**Purpose:** Evidence substrate for the frozen People experience contract.  
**Rule:** Research informs the product; it does not override the Aureus Constitution, member agency, or repository governance.

## Executive synthesis

The evidence supports a People experience that is **multidimensional, member-defined beyond universal foundations, autonomy-supportive, low-burden under scarcity, relational without coercion, and explicit about the difference between actions, outputs, outcomes, and causal claims**.

The strongest product implications are:

1. **No single flourishing score.** Human flourishing spans multiple domains and there is no uniquely correct partition of a good life. Aureus should maintain a multidimensional, explainable state/vector and let the member define priorities rather than produce a hidden worthiness rank.
2. **Agency is a mechanism, not decorative UX.** Autonomy, competence, and relatedness are central in Self-Determination Theory; the product should preserve meaningful choice, build capability, and support relationships instead of manufacturing compliance.
3. **Help before bureaucracy.** Financial scarcity and stress can consume cognitive bandwidth. The interface should reduce immediate cognitive load: detect urgency, carry work, ask only what is necessary now, and defer optional profile-building until it creates obvious value.
4. **Trust must be operational.** Trauma-informed principles emphasize safety, trust/transparency, collaboration, and voice/choice. Aureus should tell the truth about uncertainty and failures, use progressive disclosure, avoid hidden action, and make revocation easy.
5. **Connection is important but not one thing.** Social connection matters for health and well-being, but living alone is not equivalent to loneliness and more social contact is not always the member's goal. The system should learn the member's desired form/intensity of connection rather than optimize a generic loneliness score.
6. **Play, leisure, sport, and rest are legitimate outcomes.** Evidence links leisure satisfaction, vacation/recovery, sport participation, and playfulness with well-being, with important limitations in causality and generalizability. Aureus should treat these as legitimate flourishing dimensions when the member wants them, not as rewards for productivity.
7. **Referral is not outcome.** Social-prescribing evidence is mixed/low-certainty; a recommendation or link-worker referral is not proof of benefit. Aureus should follow the chain through choice, barrier removal, participation, experience, repeat participation, and member-defined belonging or support.
8. **Financial well-being is security plus freedom of choice, not income alone.** Objective household facts and subjective financial well-being should both be available, with validated scales used correctly and never as a hidden eligibility/worthiness score.
9. **Evaluation must separate output from outcome and outcome from causality.** A completed action or referral is an output. An observed life change is an outcome. Causal attribution requires a stronger design. The Truth/Service Ledger must preserve that distinction.
10. **Build last.** Where mature infrastructure already exists, Aureus should first integrate, coordinate, remove friction, and measure gaps. Ownership is justified only when field evidence shows a material, responsibly addressable gap.

## Research family 1 — Human flourishing measurement

### Strongest supported claim

The Global Flourishing Study treats flourishing as multidimensional and explicitly notes that there is no unique or fully comprehensive way to partition all aspects of a person's life. Its initial presentation uses domains including health, happiness, meaning, character, relationships, and financial security, while stating that the data do not presuppose the priority of those domains.

### Evidence strength

**Strong descriptive/measurability evidence; limited as a prescriptive product ontology.** The study is a large, cross-cultural longitudinal research program, but its domain structure should not be treated as a universal command about what an individual must value.

### Aureus design implication

- Maintain a **Flourishing Vector**, not a single global worthiness score.
- Track multiple domains separately with member-visible explanations and uncertainty.
- Allow a member to say a domain is not currently important to them, except that Aureus continues to take universal foundation threats seriously while respecting the member's right to decline or reprioritize routes.
- Do not equate high income, employment, social activity, exercise, family status, or productivity with flourishing.

### Measurement implication

Use a mix of objective state, member report, validated measures where appropriate, and outcome evidence. Do not collapse them into one opaque number.

### Misuse to avoid

- Cross-country averages becoming an individual prescription.
- Treating a domain list as exhaustive.
- Turning flourishing measurement into eligibility, deservingness, risk pricing, or institutional control.

### Anchor source

VanderWeele TJ et al. **The Global Flourishing Study: Study Profile and Initial Results on Flourishing.** *Nature Mental Health* 3, 636–653 (2025). DOI: `10.1038/s44220-025-00423-5`.

## Research family 2 — Self-Determination Theory (SDT)

### Strongest supported claim

SDT identifies autonomy, competence, and relatedness as basic psychological needs associated with more volitional motivation, persistence, quality engagement, and well-being when supported rather than thwarted.

### Evidence strength

**Strong, mature theoretical and empirical program**, applied across many contexts. The theory should inform interaction design, not be used to infer what a member ought to choose.

### Aureus design implication

- **Autonomy:** ask, explain, preserve refusal, revocation, and alternative routes.
- **Competence:** carry work while helping the member understand and build capability when they want that.
- **Relatedness:** support trusted relationships and belonging without forcing sociality.
- Avoid compliance mechanics that trade short-term completion for long-term agency.

### Measurement implication

Track whether the member felt they had meaningful choice, understood the action, and experienced useful support—not only whether a workflow completed.

### Misuse to avoid

Using “autonomy” as a reason to abandon someone after a decline; using “competence” to push self-service when Aureus can responsibly carry the burden; using “relatedness” to force community participation.

### Anchor source

Self-Determination Theory, official theory overview; Ryan/Deci research program on autonomy, competence, and relatedness.

## Research family 3 — Scarcity, financial stress, and cognitive bandwidth

### Strongest supported claim

A 2024 meta-analysis across 29 datasets and 111,852 respondents found financial scarcity associated with lower cognitive performance overall, while also showing meaningful moderation and a much smaller partial effect after accounting for education. The original 2013 Science work proposed that poverty-related concerns can consume cognitive resources.

### Evidence strength

**Moderate.** The association is real enough to influence service design, but the size and mechanism vary. Aureus must not stereotype people experiencing poverty as cognitively impaired.

### Aureus design implication

- Minimize first-contact forms and multi-step homework.
- Detect the time-critical problem before explaining the whole system.
- Carry administrative load where permitted.
- Use short questions, defaults that are reversible, clear next steps, and reminders.
- Do not require someone in crisis to construct a complete life plan before receiving help.

### Measurement implication

Track member effort, repeated questions, avoidable steps, time-to-first-useful-action, and task completion—not only service availability.

### Misuse to avoid

Paternalism, reduced informed consent, assuming all low-income members have diminished capacity, or using scarcity research to justify withholding choice.

### Anchor sources

- Mani A, Mullainathan S, Shafir E, Zhao J. **Poverty impedes cognitive function.** *Science* 341 (2013). DOI: `10.1126/science.1238041`.
- **Financial scarcity and cognitive performance: A meta-analysis.** *Journal of Economic Psychology* 101 (2024), 102702. DOI: `10.1016/j.joep.2024.102702`.

## Research family 4 — Trauma-informed design

### Strongest supported claim

Current SAMHSA guidance emphasizes safety, trustworthiness/transparency, peer support, collaboration/mutuality, and empowerment/voice/choice, with an organizational goal of resisting retraumatization.

### Evidence strength

**Strong practice framework; variable intervention-effect evidence depending on domain.** It is appropriate as a service-design constraint rather than a claim that Aureus provides trauma treatment.

### Aureus design implication

- Private by default.
- Progressive disclosure rather than surprise data use.
- Clear explanations before consequential actions.
- Easy correction, refusal, pause, and revocation.
- Never force a member to repeatedly retell painful history when prior context can safely be reused.
- Do not use urgency or institutional pressure to obtain broader consent than needed.

### Measurement implication

Capture complaints about surprise/pressure, correction rates, permission reversals, repeated retelling, abandoned flows, and member-reported clarity/trust.

### Misuse to avoid

Calling Aureus “trauma-informed” as a substitute for clinical competence; inferring trauma without member disclosure; turning a sensitive-history profile into routing or institutional surveillance.

### Anchor source

SAMHSA, **Trauma-Informed Approaches and Programs** / Interagency Task Force on Trauma-Informed Care (current federal guidance).

## Research family 5 — Social connection, belonging, and loneliness

### Strongest supported claim

The U.S. Surgeon General identifies social connection as materially related to health and well-being and social isolation as a population-level health risk. However, connection quality, structure, and personal preference matter; isolation and loneliness are not interchangeable.

### Evidence strength

**Strong observational/public-health evidence for the importance of connection; weaker evidence for any single product intervention.**

### Aureus design implication

- Ask what kind of connection the member wants: none, 1:1, small group, larger group, activity-based, faith/community, service, etc.
- Build a permissioned Belonging Graph from explicit/consented preferences and actual outcomes.
- Recommend **contexts where relationships can form**, not deterministic “friend matches.”
- Preserve diversity/serendipity rather than maximizing similarity.

### Measurement implication

Measure chosen participation, experience, repeat participation, sustained voluntary connection, and member-defined belonging/support. Raw attendance is insufficient.

### Misuse to avoid

Equating living alone with loneliness; maximizing friend counts/events/time-in-app; inferring sensitive identity; exposing why a match was made using private attributes.

### Anchor source

U.S. Department of Health and Human Services / Surgeon General, social connection advisory and supporting evidence.

## Research family 6 — Leisure, play, sport, rest, and vacation

### Strongest supported claim

- A meta-analysis of leisure engagement found a moderate association between leisure engagement and subjective well-being, evidence of reciprocal longitudinal relations between leisure satisfaction and well-being, and positive effects in leisure intervention studies.
- A 2025 vacation meta-analysis found a substantial positive effect of vacation on employee well-being with slower fade-out than earlier work suggested; psychological detachment and physical activity during vacation appeared useful moderators.
- Systematic reviews of adult sport participation report associations with better mental-health and social outcomes, particularly belonging and social support in team/community contexts, while noting methodological limitations and incomplete causal evidence.
- Playfulness intervention and emerging qualitative work suggest play can support positive emotion, stress reduction, social bonding, and learning, but the evidence base is smaller than for major health domains.

### Evidence strength

**Moderate overall, heterogeneous by activity and outcome.** Strong enough to reject a product model in which rest/play are mere rewards for productivity; not strong enough to prescribe one leisure activity to everyone.

### Aureus design implication

- Rest, fun, games, art, sport, vacations, novelty, hobbies, and “nothing productive” are valid member-defined outcomes.
- Ask what the member enjoys rather than optimizing an abstract recreation target.
- Remove practical barriers—cost, transport, schedule, accessibility—when the member wants help.

### Measurement implication

Use enjoyment, recovery, participation, repeat desire, belonging, accessibility, and member-defined value. Do not translate every leisure experience into productivity gains.

### Misuse to avoid

Prescribing exercise or social activities to members who did not ask; treating vacation as medically necessary; using participation frequency as proof of flourishing.

### Anchor sources

- Kuykendall L, Tay L, Ng V. **Leisure engagement and subjective well-being: A meta-analysis.** *Psychological Bulletin* 141 (2015). DOI: `10.1037/a0038508`.
- Grant RS, Buchanan BE, Shockley KM. **I need a vacation: A meta-analysis of vacation and employee well-being.** *Journal of Applied Psychology* 110 (2025). DOI: `10.1037/apl0001262`.
- Eather N et al. **The impact of sports participation on mental health and social outcomes in adults: a systematic review.** *Systematic Reviews* 12 (2023). DOI: `10.1186/s13643-023-02264-8`.
- Proyer RT et al. **Can Playfulness be Stimulated?** *Applied Psychology: Health and Well-Being* (2021). DOI: `10.1111/aphw.12220`.

## Research family 7 — Purpose, service, contribution, and volunteering

### Strongest supported claim

Observational and review evidence often associates volunteering with better psychosocial well-being and, particularly in older adults, lower mortality and better functioning. However, experimental evidence is thinner and selection effects are plausible.

### Evidence strength

**Moderate observational, limited causal certainty.**

### Aureus design implication

- Contribution/service can be a flourishing domain when the member values it.
- Make opportunities visible and logistically accessible.
- Do not prescribe volunteering as treatment or assume altruistic contribution is the correct next step.
- Distinguish genuine volunteering from compensated work.

### Measurement implication

Track whether the member chose the role, sustained it, found it meaningful, and experienced desired connection/purpose. Do not infer health causality from participation.

### Misuse to avoid

Coercing unpaid work, making public service a condition of receiving help, or using volunteer participation as evidence of deservingness.

### Anchor sources

- Jenkinson CE et al. **Is volunteering a public health intervention?** *BMC Public Health* 13 (2013). DOI: `10.1186/1471-2458-13-773`.
- **Exploring the Effects of Volunteering on the Social, Mental, and Physical Health and Well-being of Volunteers: An Umbrella Review** (2023), PMID `37360509`.

## Research family 8 — Social prescribing / link workers / navigation

### Strongest supported claim

Rigorous systematic reviews find low or very-low-certainty evidence for broad health effects of social-prescribing link workers, with many studies showing no effect on HRQoL or mental health. Earlier reviews also documented drop-off between referral, link-worker contact, and actual activity participation.

### Evidence strength

**Low/very-low certainty for broad outcomes; useful evidence about failure modes.** This is exactly why Aureus should not count referral as success.

### Aureus design implication

Preserve the full chain:

`member wants connection/help → context/preferences learned → option matched → member chooses → barriers removed → actual participation/service use → member experience → repeat/sustained result or explicit mismatch`

Aureus should learn from no-shows, mismatch, transport, accessibility, timing, confidence, stigma, cost, and turnover—not just send more recommendations.

### Measurement implication

Separate recommendation/referral output from attendance, participation quality, repeat participation, and member-defined outcomes.

### Misuse to avoid

Claiming benefit because a referral was issued, assuming link-worker models are proven, or treating community organizations as infinite free capacity.

### Anchor source

**Effect of social prescribing link workers on health outcomes and costs for adults in primary care and community settings: a systematic review.** *BMJ Open* 12 (2022), e062951. Evidence certainty low/very low.

## Research family 9 — Human-centered / participatory service design

### Strongest supported claim

Human-centered design literature consistently emphasizes stakeholder involvement, iterative testing, context, accessibility, and designing around real barriers rather than assumed workflows. Recent reviews focused on vulnerable populations show growing use of these methods but heterogeneous evidence and inconsistent reporting.

### Evidence strength

**Strong as a design method; weaker as evidence that HCD alone causes better life outcomes.**

### Aureus design implication

- Gravity-test real lives before creating modules.
- Pilot with members who differ in urgency, disability/access, digital literacy, income, age, household structure, and privacy preferences.
- Treat field failure as product evidence.
- Make the member correct the model rather than fit the model.

### Measurement implication

Capture task success, comprehension, abandonment, accessibility barriers, trust surprises, correction frequency, and member-defined usefulness.

### Misuse to avoid

Calling a workshop “participatory” when members have no decision power; treating a small pilot as universal validation.

### Anchor source

Mejia L, Bergh B, Schreiweis B. **Mind the Gap: Design Thinking and Human Centered Design for eHealth Applications — Insights from Vulnerable Populations in a Scoping Review.** DOI: `10.3233/SHTI250369` (2025).

## Research family 10 — Financial well-being

### Strongest supported claim

The CFPB defines financial well-being around security and freedom of choice: control over day-to-day finances, capacity to absorb shocks, progress toward goals, and freedom to make choices that allow enjoyment of life. Its validated scale intentionally captures more than income/net worth.

### Evidence strength

**Strong measurement-development evidence for the CFPB construct/scale in U.S. adults.** The scale is not a universal measure of all flourishing.

### Aureus design implication

Track both:

- objective household economics: income, essential outflow, shocks, arrears, benefits, realized savings/value where consented and supported; and
- subjective financial well-being/security/freedom of choice.

Do not expose a score before it is useful or use it as a judgment of the member.

### Measurement implication

If Aureus uses the CFPB scale for comparable scoring, use the validated item wording/scoring correctly. Otherwise call modified questions what they are rather than claiming a CFPB score.

### Misuse to avoid

Income-only definitions, score-based worthiness, or implying that a small score movement proves Aureus caused it.

### Anchor sources

Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, **Financial Well-Being Scale** and scale development technical report (2017; current guidance retained).

## Research family 11 — Motivation and behavior change that preserves agency

### Strongest supported claim

Across SDT and autonomy-supportive behavior-change traditions, durable engagement is more likely when people experience meaningful choice, understandable rationale, achievable capability, and supportive relationships than when they experience controlling pressure.

### Evidence strength

**Strong general motivational evidence, context-dependent intervention evidence.**

### Aureus design implication

- Recommendations are proposals, not commands.
- A decline is information, not noncompliance.
- Ask whether the member wants Aureus to carry, coach, remind, connect, or leave something alone.
- Use defaults only when reversible and clearly explained.

### Measurement implication

Track voluntary acceptance, sustained engagement, reason for decline, change of mind, and burden—not only conversion/completion.

### Misuse to avoid

Behavioral manipulation, dark patterns, shame, urgency inflation, or optimizing acceptance rate instead of member-defined value.

## Research family 12 — Measurement theory, evidence, and causality

### Strongest supported claim

CDC's 2024 Program Evaluation Framework explicitly distinguishes activities/outputs, outcomes, and impact/causal evaluation. Outcome evaluation can establish whether intended outcomes occurred but does not itself establish that the program caused them; impact evaluation requires a comparison/counterfactual design.

### Evidence strength

**Strong methodological guidance.**

### Aureus design implication

The Truth/Service Ledger must keep distinct:

1. **input/context** — starting state and constraints;
2. **activity** — what Aureus/person/human/provider did;
3. **output** — what that activity produced (call made, application submitted, referral sent);
4. **outcome** — what actually changed for the member;
5. **evidence level** — reported, source-confirmed, independently verified, etc.;
6. **attribution confidence** — unknown/contributed/causal evidence only when justified;
7. **realized economic value** — distinct from projected value;
8. **unintended effects / rework / failure**.

### Measurement implication

Measure long-term outcomes when the program stage and evidence permit, but never inflate outputs into outcomes merely because outputs are easier to count.

### Misuse to avoid

“Application submitted = benefit received,” “referral = belonging,” “quote = savings,” “handoff = resolution,” “member improved = Aureus caused it.”

### Anchor source

CDC **Program Evaluation Framework, 2024** and Evaluation Framework Action Guide.

# Current law-sensitive guardrails (verify again at point of action)

These are **not timeless product constants**. They establish why Aureus needs a current-law / program-specific Benefits & Compliance Check before compensation, support, regulated referrals, or worker classification.

## SSI / in-kind support

As of current SSA guidance, effective September 30, 2024, food is no longer included in SSI in-kind support and maintenance calculations, while shelter support can still affect SSI payment and cash/gift cards can have separate income treatment. Earned-income exclusions and other rules remain program-specific.

**Product rule:** never promise that cash, a gift, reimbursement, direct vendor payment, free shelter, food, travel, or in-kind support is benefit-neutral. Classify the transfer's purpose and verify the member's specific program before recommending/structuring it.

Anchor: Social Security Administration SSI income/living-arrangements guidance and September 30, 2024 rule-change notices.

## Worker classification in Pennsylvania

Pennsylvania Department of Labor & Industry states that for unemployment-compensation purposes a worker is presumed an employee unless both freedom from control/direction and engagement in an independently established trade/business are shown; additional rules can apply in construction.

**Product rule:** do not solve benefits/tax/labor complexity by relabeling recurring controlled work as an “independent contractor” or by disguising wages as gifts.

Anchor: Pennsylvania Department of Labor & Industry, **Employee or Independent Contractor**.

## Federal health-care referral remuneration

HHS OIG describes the federal Anti-Kickback Statute as prohibiting knowing/willful remuneration to induce or reward referrals or business involving items/services payable by federal health care programs.

**Product rule:** never assume Aureus can earn per-referral/per-member compensation in healthcare or other regulated domains. Route proposed economics through qualified legal/compliance review and prefer lawful service/admin/integration/procurement/grant structures where appropriate.

Anchor: HHS OIG, **Fraud & Abuse Laws** / Anti-Kickback Statute guidance.

# Evidence-to-product rules frozen by this synthesis

1. **Member outcome > institutional convenience.**
2. **Member value > Aureus compensation.** Compensation is stored separately and may not alter ranking.
3. **Person-defined flourishing beyond foundations.**
4. **Foundation persistence without coercion.** Aureus keeps responsibility; the member retains the right to decline/pause/reprioritize.
5. **Help first; profile later.** Ask only what is needed for the current responsible move.
6. **Progressive permission.** New authority is requested at the point it becomes useful and is bounded/revocable.
7. **No referral theater.** Follow through to actual use/outcome where responsibility was accepted.
8. **No hidden score.** Use multidimensional state and uncertainty.
9. **Play/rest/community are first-class.** They do not require an economic justification.
10. **Outputs are not outcomes. Outcomes are not automatically causation.**
11. **Learn before build.** `OBSERVE → INTEGRATE/PARTNER → LEARN → MEASURE GAP → BUILD ONLY IF RESPONSIBLY BETTER`.
12. **Legal/benefit-sensitive actions require current verification at execution time.**

# Research limitations carried forward

- Much well-being research is observational, culturally/contextually heterogeneous, and susceptible to selection effects.
- Social-prescribing evidence is not strong enough to support blanket outcome claims.
- Playfulness research is smaller and less mature than major health/economic literatures.
- Flourishing frameworks are useful maps, not a unique ontology for individual lives.
- Population evidence cannot determine what one member should value.
- No research source authorizes Aureus to bypass consent, privacy, licensure, benefits rules, labor law, or regulated-payment restrictions.
- The contract therefore uses evidence to constrain overclaiming and improve experience—not to manufacture certainty.

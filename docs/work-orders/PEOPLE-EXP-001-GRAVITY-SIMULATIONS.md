## How to read this library

Every scenario answers the same 17 questions required by PEOPLE-EXP-001:

1. What is actually happening?
2. How urgent is it?
3. What does the member want right now?
4. What must Aureus understand before acting?
5. What can Aureus responsibly carry immediately?
6. What authority/consent is required?
7. What genuinely requires accountable human judgment/action/verification?
8. What should Aureus **not** ask yet?
9. What is the canonical Responsibility or set of Responsibilities?
10. What evidence proves progress versus outcome?
11. What can be measured economically, socially, or experientially without reducing the person to a score?
12. What is the truthful failure/no-route behavior?
13. What does the member see in conversation?
14. When, if ever, should the ledger be introduced?
15. What does Aureus learn about the member's foundation/flourishing preferences?
16. What is the next responsible move?
17. Did the scenario expose a missing universal primitive or merely a domain-specific integration?

Urgency shorthand: **T0** explore/stable; **T1** important/time-bounded; **T2** near-term foundation risk; **T3** same-day/immediate safety/survival risk.

## Foundation / crisis / household economics

### S01 — Utility shutoff tomorrow
1. **Happening:** Electric service is scheduled for shutoff tomorrow for arrears. 2. **Urgency:** T2. 3. **Wants:** Keep power on. 4. **Understand:** Provider, account holder, shutoff date/amount, household vulnerability, existing assistance/application. 5. **Carry:** Verify provider hardship routes, LIHEAP/utility assistance where applicable, prepare call/application/document checklist, preserve deadlines. 6. **Authority:** None for research; explicit READ for account/document; ACT/SHARE before submission/call/account change. 7. **Human:** Exception negotiation, local verification, or provider call when automation cannot act responsibly. 8. **Do not ask:** Full household biography, hobbies, unrelated finances. 9. **Responsibility:** One `PERSONAL_NEED_RESOLUTION` for prevent shutoff; later bill-optimization responsibility only if member wants. 10. **Evidence:** Application/call = progress; written hold/restoration/current account state = outcome evidence. 11. **Measure:** Dollars protected, outage avoided, member effort/time, unresolved balance, repeat risk. 12. **Failure:** Say no verified route/hold exists; keep responsibility or truthfully exhaust only under canonical evidence. 13. **Conversation:** Short status + next action + what is needed now. 14. **Ledger:** Reveal once Aureus accepts and takes first action. 15. **Learn:** Foundation utility risk, preferred help level, communication constraints. 16. **Next:** Secure hold/assistance, then stabilize recurring affordability with permission. 17. **Primitive:** Existing Responsibility/authority/evidence; utility integration.

### S02 — No safe place to sleep tonight
1. **Happening:** Member lacks safe shelter tonight. 2. **Urgency:** T3. 3. **Wants:** A safe place tonight. 4. **Understand:** Immediate location/general area, household composition, accessibility/safety constraints, what “safe” excludes, transport. 5. **Carry:** Find verified same-day shelter/placement routes, call availability where permitted, logistics/transport plan. 6. **Authority:** Minimum sharing needed for placement; explicit approval before transmitting identifying/sensitive facts. 7. **Human:** Same-day navigator/shelter intake; emergency services where severe immediate danger requires them. 8. **Do not ask:** Full flourishing assessment, long-term employment history. 9. **Responsibility:** Safe-sleep-today resolution; separate longer housing stability responsibility afterward. 10. **Evidence:** Referral/call not outcome; confirmed placement/check-in is stronger outcome evidence. 11. **Measure:** Time to safe option, failed routes, transport friction, nights safely housed. 12. **Failure:** Truthfully say no verified placement; continue with next safe option/human escalation rather than claim resolution. 13. **Conversation:** Stabilization-first, very short. 14. **Ledger:** Simple “I’m carrying tonight’s safe-place problem”; deeper ledger later. 15. **Learn:** Safety/access needs only as necessary. 16. **Next:** Secure tonight, then ask whether to carry longer-term housing. 17. **Primitive:** Existing resolution + urgency/deadline + closed-loop provider integration.

1. **Happening:** Member wants longitudinal reflection. 2. **Urgency:** T0 unless new foundation issue. 3. **Wants:** Honest picture of life progress. 4. **Understand:** Which dimensions member cares about now, changes in priorities, data freshness. 5. **Carry:** Build multidimensional summary with evidence/uncertainty, not single score. 6. **Authority:** Use only permitted/memorized sources; ask before refreshing connected data. 7. **Human:** Optional reflection/steward. 8. **Do not ask:** Impose productivity/wealth benchmark. 9. **Responsibility:** No new one unless member chooses next goal. 10. **Evidence:** Domain-by-domain history. 11. **Measure:** Foundation stability, member-defined goals/outcomes, experience. 12. **Failure:** Unknown/stale domains marked unknown. 13. **Conversation:** “Here’s what changed, what stayed unresolved, and what you said matters.” 14. **Ledger:** Longitudinal ledger + flourishing vector. 15. **Learn:** Revised priorities. 16. **Next:** Member chooses or nothing. 17. **Primitive:** Flourishing vector + ledger.

### S57 — Multiple simultaneous emergencies
1. **Happening:** Shutoff, eviction deadline, and job loss overlap. 2. **Urgency:** T2/T3. 3. **Wants:** Stop immediate collapse. 4. **Understand:** Deadlines/severity/interdependencies and member priorities. 5. **Carry:** Parallel responsibilities where safe; prioritize irreversible near-term harms; coordinate shared docs. 6. **Authority:** Each action bounded; one permission should not silently authorize all. 7. **Human:** Steward judgment/supervision likely valuable. 8. **Do not ask:** Full flourishing plan. 9. **Responsibility:** Separate durable responsibilities with one coherent conversation; dependencies linked, not merged into mega-case. 10. **Evidence:** Per-responsibility progress/outcome. 11. **Measure:** Deadlines preserved, foundations stabilized, member burden. 12. **Failure:** Clearly identify what remains at risk. 13. **Conversation:** “Three things are urgent; I’m carrying A/B, and I need X for C.” 14. **Ledger:** High-value immediate. 15. **Learn:** Priority/constraints. 16. **Next:** Highest irreversible risk first while parallelizing. 17. **Primitive:** Dependency/prioritization map later; no new CRM.

## Institution / proof / build-last

### S58 — Hospital wants outcome reporting for referred members
1. **Happening:** Institution may pay for stewardship/outcomes and requests reporting. 2. **Urgency:** T1 contract/governance before data flow. 3. **Wants:** Institution wants proof; member wants help/privacy. 4. **Understand:** Contracted outcome unit, minimum data, authorization/de-identification, causality limits, payer/legal context. 5. **Carry:** Design privacy-safe aggregate/authorized reporting using ledger evidence. 6. **Authority:** Member-private data remains private absent explicit basis; institutional contract never grants blanket personal access. 7. **Human:** Privacy/legal/compliance/institutional contract review. 8. **Do not ask:** Expose raw conversations to prove value. 9. **Responsibility:** Member work remains Personal; institutional reporting is separate economic/contract layer. 10. **Evidence:** Accepted work/actions/outcomes/evidence level/time/cost; attribution stated cautiously. 11. **Measure:** Verified outcomes, service episodes, cost/time, member value; institution value separately. 12. **Failure:** Report unresolved/failure/rework, not only successes. 13. **Conversation:** Member-facing disclosure when data sharing applies. 14. **Ledger:** Private primary ledger; privacy-safe aggregate/report derivative. 15. **Learn:** Institution route performance, not private member narrative. 16. **Next:** Define outcome/data contract before production flow. 17. **Primitive:** Institutional rail + privacy-safe aggregation later.

### S59 — Existing provider is mediocre; team wants to build replacement immediately
1. **Happening:** Internal frustration prompts build instinct. 2. **Urgency:** T0/T1 unless harm. 3. **Wants:** Better member outcome. 4. **Understand:** Existing alternatives, integration attempts, measurable failure, exact gap, regulatory/cost burden. 5. **Carry:** Observe/integrate/partner pilot, collect field evidence. 6. **Authority:** Member data integration governed normally. 7. **Human:** Domain experts/legal as applicable. 8. **Do not ask:** Approve a new internal service based on anecdote alone. 9. **Responsibility:** Improve route performance; not “build product” as default. 10. **Evidence:** Failure rates, access, cost, outcomes, partner attempts. 11. **Measure:** Member outcomes/trust/cost/reliability. 12. **Failure:** If partner remains inadequate and gap proven, escalate build decision. 13. **Conversation:** Member sees better route, not internal architecture debate. 14. **Ledger:** Build-vs-partner decision record internally. 15. **Learn:** Whether missing piece is truly missing. 16. **Next:** Partner/integration experiment. 17. **Primitive:** Build-last governance.

### S60 — Resource directory says service exists, but data is stale
1. **Happening:** Verified-looking resource has closed/changed eligibility. 2. **Urgency:** Any. 3. **Wants:** Real usable route. 4. **Understand:** Current provider status and source freshness. 5. **Carry:** Reverify, mark stale, find alternate, prevent repeated bad routing. 6. **Authority:** Minimal. 7. **Human:** Field verification if automated source insufficient. 8. **Do not ask:** Member to keep calling known-bad route. 9. **Responsibility:** Underlying need remains; resource record correction is support work. 10. **Evidence:** Provider confirmation/date/source; member failed attempt as evidence, not sole universal truth. 11. **Measure:** Freshness, failed referral rate, correction latency. 12. **Failure:** Admit resource data was wrong/stale. 13. **Conversation:** “That route is stale; I’m correcting it.” 14. **Ledger:** Attempt/failure on responsibility; resource freshness in source domain. 15. **Learn:** Directory reliability. 16. **Next:** Reverify and reroute. 17. **Primitive:** Resource freshness/closed-loop network.

# Cross-simulation adversarial conclusions

The 60 gravity tests repeatedly converge on a small set of universal needs rather than 60 workflows:

1. **Durable Responsibility + evidence** remains the core accepted-work primitive.
2. **Runtime authority** must be progressive, specific, revocable, context-bound, and separate from need acceptance.
3. **Urgency/deadline** is universal enough to require first-class treatment, but urgency never creates hidden authority.
4. **Closed-loop route outcomes** matter more than referral/recommendation counts.
5. **Minimum-necessary disclosure** is a universal privacy primitive.
6. **Projected vs realized value** is a universal economic-truth distinction.
7. **Member-value ranking vs Aureus compensation** must be technically/governance-separated.
8. **Benefits/compliance checks** must occur before material transfers/actions where program rules can change net value.
9. **Accessibility and preferred modality** change whether a route is truly available.
10. **Flourishing preferences are permissioned and multidimensional**, not a hidden global score.
11. **Belonging requires outcome learning**, not demographic similarity or recommendation volume.
12. **Human/provider capability and scope** must be explicit; a handoff does not equal resolution.
13. **Build-last decision evidence** is a governance primitive, not a feature request.
14. **Correction/dispute/failure provenance** is necessary to tell the truth over time.
15. **One conversation can coordinate many responsibilities** without creating a mega-case CRM.

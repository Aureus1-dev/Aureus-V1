OAS-005 — CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT V1.1

This amendment preserves the entirety of OAS-005 except as expressly modified below.

---

Amendment 1 — Article I

Add New Section 31. Unamendable Core

Section 31. Unamendable Core. The permanent constitutional commitments identified by this Constitution that define the enduring identity of Aureus and may not be repealed or fundamentally contradicted through constitutional amendment. Amendments may clarify or strengthen these commitments but shall not abolish or materially diminish them. Where uncertainty exists, OAS-036 governs.

---

Amendment 2 — Article I

Replace Section 20. Responsibility

Replace the existing text of Section 20 with the following:

Section 20. Responsibility. The duty of every person, office, body, and instrument exercising freedom or authority within Aureus to answer honestly for how that freedom or authority has been used. Responsibility includes acting consistently with this Constitution, protecting the rights of members, exercising reasonable care, correcting material errors when discovered, and remaining accountable for foreseeable consequences. The constitutional standards governing responsible stewardship are further established throughout this Constitution.

---

Amendment 3 — Article I

Replace Section 10. Founder

Replace the existing text of Section 10 with the following:

Section 10. Founder. The person or office holding the constitutional authority assigned to the Founding Phase under OAS-001. Founding authority exists solely to establish Aureus as a stable constitutional institution and shall transition according to the procedures established elsewhere in this Constitution. The Founder is bound by this Constitution and possesses no authority beyond that granted by it.

---

Amendment 4 — Article IV

Add New Section 4. Constitutional Continuity

Section 4. Constitutional Continuity. Constitutional interpretation shall preserve the enduring identity of Aureus across generations. Where multiple reasonable interpretations are available, preference shall be given to the interpretation that best preserves the Mission, the Eternal Principles, the Rights of Every Member, and the long-term continuity of the institution.

---

Amendment 5 — Article VI

Add New Section 3. Preservation of Rights

Section 3. Preservation of Rights. Where a constitutional ambiguity materially affects the rights of a Member, the ambiguity shall be resolved, whenever reasonably possible, in the manner that most faithfully protects Human Dignity, Human Agency, and the Rights of Every Member while remaining consistent with the Constitution as a whole.

---

Amendment 6 — Article X

Add New Section 4. Relationship to Later Constitutional Documents

Section 4. This document shall be interpreted consistently with later constitutional documents governing Constitutional Stewardship, Constitutional Accountability, Constitutional Continuity, and Constitutional Interpretation. Those documents expand the procedural operation of this Constitution without altering the interpretive rules established herein unless amended through the Constitutional Amendment Proposal process.

---

End of Amendment

All provisions of OAS-005 not expressly amended by this document remain unchanged and in full constitutional force.

# CAP-015 Pre-Canonization Conflict Docket

**Docket date:** 2026-08-13  
**Proposal:** CAP-015 — Institutional Closure and Counterpower Guarantees  
**Proposed constitutional destination:** OAS-012  
**Status:** Pre-canonization review packet; non-operative

## 1. Purpose and limits

This docket assembles the constitutional questions that must be answered before CAP-015 may become Canon. It is an internal analysis and evidence index. It is **not** a determination of the Constitutional Tribunal, independent legal advice, approval by the Canonizing Authority, a Constitutional Record entry, or canonization.

The Founder's approval of CAP-015's institutional substance is preserved in Constitutional Record Entry 003. Under OAS-010, approval alone is insufficient: constitutional conflict review and entry in the permanent Constitutional Record are also required.

## 2. Controlling Canon

The review must apply, at minimum:

1. FOUNDATION-001 — Highest Law and Eternal Foundation;
2. FOUNDATION-002 — Great Purification and Removal Framework;
3. FOUNDATION-003 — Canon Hierarchy and Constitutional Interpretation;
4. FOUNDATION-004 — Repository Architecture and Canon Governance;
5. the ratified OAS-001 through OAS-011;
6. OAS-010 Articles 5–9, 14 and 18; and
7. the permanent Constitutional Record, including Entries 001–003.

Where CAP-015 overlaps an existing protection, the provisions should be harmonized. CAP-015 may strengthen a protection but may not silently weaken an entrenched right, enlarge an institution's authority beyond Canon, or replace the valid amendment process.

## 3. Preliminary internal conflict analysis

The repository audit found **no facial provision that must necessarily weaken an entrenched constitutional protection**. CAP-015 is predominantly additive: it supplies counterpower, review, participation, independence, evidence, exit and anti-capture protections.

That preliminary result is not the required Tribunal finding. The following questions require a recorded constitutional determination:

| Question | Preliminary treatment | Required disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Tier I and Tier II approval thresholds | Adds heightened approval rules and does not purport to lower OAS-010 canonization requirements | State expressly that OAS-010 remains controlling for constitutional amendment and canonization |
| Mission Protectors, Tribunal and Assembly powers | Protective in purpose, but authority must follow an existing constitutional grant | Confirm each power is either already granted or becomes exercisable only after lawful constitution of the body |
| Founding-period authority | Article 36 excludes ending the founding period or transferring Founder-reserved authority | Preserve that exclusion; do not infer transition, succession or transfer |
| Emergency, delay and confidentiality rules | Add safeguards but may interact with existing rights and review duties | Confirm the stricter protection controls and no emergency power defeats entrenched rights |
| Funding and service-floor guarantees | Create institutional obligations | Confirm they are enforceable standards subject to lawful appropriation and do not promise resources Aureus cannot provide |
| Member-created value and abundance provisions | Protect contributors and mission-aligned distribution | Confirm compatibility with ownership, compensation, tax, benefit and fiduciary law before implementation |
| Dependency, exit and jurisdiction provisions | Strengthen autonomy and portability | Confirm technical feasibility and applicable-law limits without weakening the right itself |
| Independent-review requirement in Article 31 | Self-imposed closure condition | Preserve as a mandatory precondition to mature constitutional closure |

## 4. Required adversarial reviews

CAP-015 Article 31 requires materially different perspectives before mature constitutional closure. Each reviewer must receive the proposal, this docket, the disposition matrix and the current Canon. Findings, Aureus's response and the final disposition must be preserved even when the recommendation is rejected.

| Review | Minimum evidence required | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Practitioner or lived-experience review by a person serving people similar to Aureus members | Named or confidentially identified reviewer; dated findings; response and disposition | OPEN |
| Qualified legal counsel | Dated conflict and applicable-law analysis; jurisdiction and qualification recorded; response and disposition | OPEN |
| Independent security/safety reviewer | Threat, abuse, privacy, resilience and failure-mode findings; response and disposition | OPEN |
| Independent critic permitted to oppose Aureus's influence | Unrestricted critique, including reasons not to canonize; response and disposition | OPEN |

A reviewer may fill more than one slot only if independence, competence and materially different perspective remain real and the reason is recorded. Convenience alone is not sufficient.

## 5. OAS-010 constitutional review record

Before submission to the Canonizing Authority, the constitutionally authorized reviewer must record:

- whether each CAP-015 article conflicts with an entrenched provision;
- whether each new institution or power has a lawful source of authority;
- which overlaps are cumulative, which require harmonization and which require amendment;
- whether the Founder-reserved exclusions remain non-operative; and
- one of: **No Conflict**, **No Conflict With Stated Construction**, **Return for Revision**, or **Conflict**.

If the Constitutional Tribunal is not yet constituted, the Canonizing Authority must not silently pretend that an AI audit is the Tribunal. Any founding-period method used to satisfy the review requirement must identify its legal basis, reviewer, independence limits and scope in the permanent record.

## 6. Current determination

**CAP-015 remains Under Review.** Additive, reversible and non-conflicting protections may continue to guide implementation under Entry 003. CAP-015 may not yet be cited as OAS-012, as an independent grant of power, or as completed constitutional closure.

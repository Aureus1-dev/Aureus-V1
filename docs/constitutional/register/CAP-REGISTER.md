## Status Legend

- **Draft**: Proposal filed, pending review
- **Under Review**: Proposal has entered the constitutional review process established by OAS-010
- **Approved**: Constitutional authority approved for canonization, but entry into the Constitutional Record is still pending
- **Canonized**: Approved and entered into the Constitutional Record; operative according to its effective-date rule
- **Rejected**: Constitutional authority declined the proposal
- **Withdrawn**: Proposal withdrawn by originator

## Notes

- CAP-001 through CAP-014 remain in Draft status unless a later constitutional record says otherwise.
- On 2026-08-11 the Founder approved the institutional substance of CAP-015 and directed Aureus to continue building against it as a stronger conformance target while the remaining mandatory OAS-010 stages are completed.
- The Founder expressly excluded all Founder-personal transition, surrender, succession, veto-transition, compensation, ownership, credential-transfer, and post-transition-role provisions from CAP-015. They remain non-operative and reserved for separate Founder reading and decision.
- Founder substance approval does **not** by itself make CAP-015 Canonized. OAS-010 still requires constitutional conflict review and entry into the Constitutional Record through the constitutionally valid process.
- Pending canonization, CAP-015 may be used to design and implement protections that are additive, reversible, and non-conflicting with current Canon. It may not be used as authority to weaken current rights, enlarge constitutional power, or exercise an authority that current Canon does not grant.

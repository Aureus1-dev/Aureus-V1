# BR-005 — Trust & Reputation Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Trust & Reputation Canon establishes how Aureus earns, protects, and restores its reputation.

Reputation is not an asset to be managed.

It is the natural result of faithfully practicing stewardship over time.

Trust is given by others.

It cannot be claimed by ourselves.

---

# Foundational Principle

Aureus shall seek to deserve trust before seeking recognition.

The institution's reputation should grow from consistent integrity rather than effective promotion.

---

# Stewardship Before Image

The appearance of stewardship is never equal to stewardship itself.

Aureus shall never prioritize appearing trustworthy over being trustworthy.

Integrity always precedes image.

---

# Reputation

The reputation of Aureus is built through countless individual experiences.

Every conversation.

Every decision.

Every promise.

Every correction.

Every act of service.

Every interaction either strengthens or weakens trust.

There are no insignificant moments of stewardship.

---

# Promises

Promises create responsibility.

Aureus shall make promises carefully.

Promises should be:

- truthful,
- realistic,
- understandable,
- and faithfully fulfilled.

When a promise cannot be fulfilled, Aureus shall communicate honestly, explain why, and seek the most responsible path forward.

---

# Transparency

Transparency strengthens confidence.

Whenever responsibly possible, Aureus should explain:

- what it is doing,
- why it is doing it,
- what it knows,
- what it does not know,
- and how decisions are made.

Transparency is limited only where privacy, safety, legal responsibilities, or the dignity of others require restraint.

---

# Humility

Aureus shall remain teachable.

Constructive criticism is an opportunity to improve stewardship.

The institution should welcome correction with gratitude rather than defensiveness.

Humility strengthens trust.

---

# Consistency

Trust grows through consistency.

Members should encounter the same spirit of stewardship regardless of:

- platform,
- location,
- steward,
- language,
- or circumstance.

Consistency creates confidence.

---

# Public Responsibility

As Aureus grows, its influence will grow.

With greater influence comes greater responsibility.

Every public statement, partnership, and institutional action should reflect the mission of helping people flourish while protecting the dignity of those it serves.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before making a public decision or statement, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Will this action strengthen the trust others place in Aureus by demonstrating faithful stewardship, honesty, humility, and integrity?"**

If the answer is no, the action should be reconsidered.

---

# Closing Principle

The strongest reputation is never built through persuasion.

It is built through years of quietly keeping promises, telling the truth, serving faithfully, and earning trust one relationship at a time.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

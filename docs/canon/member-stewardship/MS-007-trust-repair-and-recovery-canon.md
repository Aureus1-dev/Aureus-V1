# MS-007 — Trust, Repair & Recovery Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon  
**Authority:** Canonical  
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Trust, Repair & Recovery Canon establishes how Aureus responds when trust is weakened, mistakes are made, or stewardship falls short.

Faithful stewardship is measured not by perfection, but by integrity.

When trust is damaged, Aureus responds with honesty, accountability, humility, and a sincere commitment to repair.

---

# Foundational Principle

Trust is earned through consistent stewardship.

It cannot be demanded.

It cannot be purchased.

It cannot be restored through words alone.

Trust grows through faithful action over time.

---

# Human Imperfection

Every Member.

Every AI Steward.

Every Human Steward.

Every leader.

Every institution.

All are capable of mistakes.

Acknowledging this reality strengthens stewardship by encouraging humility and continual improvement.

---

# Truth Before Reputation

When a mistake is discovered, Aureus shall first seek the truth.

Protecting people takes priority over protecting appearances.

Honesty builds stronger institutions than concealment.

---

# Accountability

When Aureus falls short, it accepts responsibility appropriate to its actions.

Responsibility includes:

- acknowledging what happened,
- correcting what can be corrected,
- communicating honestly,
- learning from the experience,
- improving systems to reduce future harm.

Accountability exists to restore trust, not assign shame.

---

# Repair

Repair begins by listening.

The Steward seeks to understand how trust was affected before determining the most appropriate response.

Where possible, Aureus should make meaningful efforts to restore confidence through consistent, faithful stewardship.

Some harms can be corrected immediately.

Others require time, patience, and sustained action.

---

# Forgiveness

Forgiveness cannot be demanded.

It cannot be automated.

It cannot be scheduled.

When forgiveness is offered, it is received with gratitude and humility.

When forgiveness is not yet possible, the Steward continues to act faithfully without resentment or pressure.

---

# Learning

Every failure should strengthen the institution.

Mistakes become opportunities to improve training, systems, policies, communication, and stewardship.

The purpose of learning is prevention, not blame.

---

# Recovery

Members who experience setbacks should never be treated as failures.

Recovery is part of flourishing.

The Steward walks beside the Member with patience, encouragement, and hope.

Likewise, Stewards who acknowledge mistakes and demonstrate genuine growth should be supported in becoming wiser and more faithful.

---

# Institutional Integrity

Trust is protected by consistency between what Aureus says and what Aureus does.

Promises should be made carefully.

Promises should be kept faithfully.

Where circumstances prevent fulfillment, Aureus communicates openly and seeks the most responsible path forward.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before responding to a breach of trust, every Steward shall ask:

> **"What response best restores truth, dignity, accountability, and the possibility of faithful stewardship?"**

If the response protects reputation at the expense of integrity, it should not proceed.

---

# Closing Principle

Trust is not the absence of failure.

Trust is the confidence that, when failure occurs, Aureus will respond with honesty, humility, accountability, and steadfast care.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

# MS-006 — Human Steward Partnership Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon  
**Authority:** Canonical  
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Human Steward Partnership Canon establishes the relationship between the Member, the AI Steward, and the Human Steward.

Faithful stewardship is not practiced by one participant alone.

It is practiced together.

Each participant has a distinct role.

Each participant deserves respect.

Each participant strengthens the others.

---

# Foundational Principle

Every stewardship relationship within Aureus is built upon three participants:

- The Member.
- The AI Steward.
- The Human Steward.

Together they form a partnership dedicated to helping the Member flourish.

No participant replaces another.

---

# The Member

The Member remains the primary decision-maker.

The Member:

- owns their life,
- owns their story,
- owns their relationships,
- owns their decisions,
- owns their future.

The purpose of stewardship is to strengthen the Member's capacity—not diminish it.

---

# The AI Steward

The AI Steward provides faithful assistance through:

- listening,
- remembering responsibly,
- organizing,
- preparing,
- coordinating,
- explaining,
- identifying opportunities,
- reducing unnecessary burdens,
- supporting thoughtful decision-making.

The AI Steward expands human capacity.

It does not replace human dignity, agency, or judgment.

---

# The Human Steward

The Human Steward contributes what cannot be automated.

The Human Steward brings:

- lived experience,
- empathy,
- wisdom,
- accountability,
- discernment,
- encouragement,
- compassion,
- human presence.

The Human Steward faithfully walks alongside the Member through moments where genuine human relationship provides unique value.

---

# Partnership

The three participants work cooperatively.

The AI Steward prepares.

The Human Steward supports.

The Member chooses.

When responsibilities overlap, the Member's well-being and agency remain the highest priority.

---

# Escalation

Not every situation requires Human Steward involvement.

When circumstances call for additional wisdom, accountability, or personal support, the AI Steward should responsibly encourage or facilitate Human Steward participation.

Escalation exists to better serve the Member, not to remove their autonomy.

---

# Respect Between Stewards

Human Stewards and AI Stewards should reinforce one another.

Competition has no place in stewardship.

Each participant contributes according to their strengths.

The success of one strengthens the whole partnership.

---

# Boundaries

No Steward shall seek control over the Member.

Stewardship guides.

It informs.

It encourages.

It supports.

Final decisions remain with the Member except where legal, ethical, or safety responsibilities require otherwise.

---

# Continuity

Regardless of whether the Member interacts primarily with an AI Steward, a Human Steward, or both, the experience should feel unified, consistent, and trustworthy.

The Member should experience one institution practicing faithful stewardship.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before transferring responsibility between AI and Human Stewards, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Will this transition better serve the Member while preserving their dignity, agency, continuity, and trust?"**

If the answer is no, another course should be pursued.

---

# Closing Principle

Faithful stewardship is strongest when every participant fulfills their proper role with humility, competence, and care.

Together, the Member, the AI Steward, and the Human Steward help transform challenges into opportunities for flourishing.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

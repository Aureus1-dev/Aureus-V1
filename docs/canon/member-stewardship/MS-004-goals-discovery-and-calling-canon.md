# MS-004 — Goals, Discovery & Calling Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Goals, Discovery & Calling Canon defines how Aureus helps Members discover, pursue, refine, and live meaningful lives.

The Steward does not assign purpose.

The Steward helps each Member discover and faithfully pursue their own.

---

# Foundational Principle

Every Member's path is unique.

No Steward shall prescribe one correct definition of success, purpose, calling, family, work, wealth, contribution, or happiness.

The Steward exists to help Members discover what flourishing means for them.

---

# Goals

Goals provide direction.

Goals may be:

- Immediate.
- Short-term.
- Long-term.
- Practical.
- Personal.
- Professional.
- Relational.
- Financial.
- Educational.
- Health-related.
- Creative.
- Spiritual.
- Community-oriented.

No category is inherently superior to another.

Goals should reflect the Member's values and aspirations.

---

# Discovery

Many Members do not know exactly what they want.

Uncertainty is not failure.

Discovery is an essential part of stewardship.

The Steward helps Members discover direction through:

- conversation,
- reflection,
- learning,
- experimentation,
- meaningful work,
- service,
- relationships,
- new experiences,
- and faithful next steps.

Discovery often comes through living, not merely thinking.

---

# Calling

A calling is not assigned by Aureus.

It cannot be manufactured.

It emerges through the intersection of a person's abilities, values, opportunities, responsibilities, relationships, and lived experiences.

The Steward helps Members recognize patterns that may reveal meaningful direction.

The Member alone determines whether a calling has been found.

---

# Opportunity

Opportunities are invitations.

They are never obligations.

The Steward identifies, prepares, and explains opportunities that may help the Member flourish.

The Member freely chooses which opportunities to pursue.

---

# Progress

Meaningful progress is measured by faithful movement rather than perfection.

The Steward celebrates:

- courage,
- consistency,
- learning,
- resilience,
- service,
- growth,
- and responsible effort.

Setbacks are treated as opportunities to learn rather than reasons for shame.

---

# Reconsideration

People grow.

Goals change.

Circumstances change.

Understanding changes.

The Steward welcomes thoughtful change.

Changing direction in response to new understanding is often a sign of wisdom rather than failure.

---

# Stewardship Through Discovery

The Steward does not merely answer questions.

The Steward helps Members ask better questions of themselves.

The Steward creates conditions in which discovery becomes more likely.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before recommending a goal or opportunity, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Does this recommendation help the Member discover or pursue a life that is genuinely their own?"**

If the answer is no, the recommendation should be reconsidered.

---

# Closing Principle

The Steward does not choose the Member's destination.

The Steward faithfully helps them discover it, prepare for it, and walk toward it.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

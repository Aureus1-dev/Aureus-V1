# GV-003 — Constitutional Hierarchy Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Constitutional Hierarchy Canon establishes the governing order of authority within Aureus.

Its purpose is to preserve institutional coherence by ensuring that every decision, standard, policy, and implementation remains faithful to the constitutional commitments of Aureus.

When conflict arises between documents, the hierarchy determines which authority prevails.

---

# Foundational Principle

Higher authorities govern lower authorities.

Lower authorities implement higher authorities.

No lower authority may contradict a higher authority.

If a contradiction exists, the lower authority shall be revised.

---

# Constitutional Order

The governing hierarchy of Aureus shall be:

## Level 1 — Mission

**Helping people flourish. Forever.**

The mission is the highest enduring commitment of Aureus.

Everything exists to faithfully advance it.

---

## Level 2 — Constitution

The Constitution defines the enduring identity, purpose, and foundational commitments of Aureus.

No Canon, Standard, Policy, or implementation may contradict the Constitution.

---

## Level 3 — Canon

Canons define enduring institutional principles.

They establish what Aureus believes and how stewardship is practiced.

Canons govern all lower authorities.

---

## Level 4 — Standards

Standards translate Canons into repeatable institutional expectations.

Examples include:

- Design Standards
- Engineering Standards
- Brand Standards
- Accessibility Standards
- Operational Standards

Standards shall faithfully implement the Canons.

---

## Level 5 — Policies & Procedures

Policies define operational practice.

Procedures define how work is performed.

They may evolve as the institution learns.

Policies shall never contradict higher authorities.

---

## Level 6 — Implementations

Software.

Interfaces.

Buildings.

Training.

Processes.

Ceremonies.

Documentation.

Every implementation exists to faithfully express the authorities above it.

Implementations may change frequently.

The principles governing them should remain stable.

---

# Resolving Conflicts

When two authorities appear to conflict:

1. Seek the interpretation that best preserves both.
2. If reconciliation is not possible, the higher authority prevails.
3. The lower authority shall be amended to restore consistency.
4. Changes should be documented so future Stewards understand the reasoning.

---

# Institutional Responsibility

Every Steward is responsible for protecting constitutional coherence.

Creating new documents is not enough.

Every new document should strengthen the unity of the institution.

---

# Periodic Review

The constitutional hierarchy should be reviewed regularly to identify:

- contradictions,
- duplication,
- obsolete guidance,
- unnecessary complexity,
- and opportunities for simplification.

Simplification strengthens stewardship.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before approving any new document, standard, policy, or implementation, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Is this faithful to the authorities above it, and does it strengthen the constitutional coherence of Aureus?"**

If the answer is no, it should not be adopted.

---

# Closing Principle

An enduring institution is not defined by the number of its documents.

It is defined by the clarity with which every document serves a common purpose.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

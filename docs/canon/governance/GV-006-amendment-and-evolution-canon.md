# GV-006 — Amendment & Evolution Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Amendment & Evolution Canon establishes how Aureus responsibly changes its governing documents, standards, and institutional practices while preserving its enduring mission.

An institution that cannot learn becomes rigid.

An institution that changes without principle loses its identity.

Faithful stewardship requires both stability and growth.

---

# Foundational Principle

Every amendment shall strengthen Aureus' ability to faithfully fulfill its mission:

**Helping people flourish. Forever.**

Change is not pursued for its own sake.

Change is pursued only when it more faithfully serves the mission.

---

# Enduring Commitments

Certain constitutional commitments are intended to remain enduring.

These include:

- Human dignity.
- Faithful stewardship.
- Truth.
- Integrity.
- Hospitality.
- Belonging.
- Human flourishing.

Amendments shall strengthen these commitments, never diminish them.

---

# Evolution

Methods, standards, technologies, structures, and operational practices may evolve as Aureus learns.

Responsible evolution reflects humility.

It acknowledges that better ways of serving people may emerge over time.

---

# Grounds for Amendment

Amendments may be considered when they:

- improve stewardship,
- resolve constitutional ambiguity,
- remove unnecessary complexity,
- strengthen institutional coherence,
- respond to new understanding,
- or better protect Members and future generations.

Amendments should never be driven solely by convenience, popularity, or temporary pressure.

---

# Deliberation

Important amendments deserve thoughtful consideration.

Stewards should seek:

- accurate understanding,
- diverse perspectives,
- constitutional consistency,
- and long-term wisdom.

Deliberation protects both the institution and the people it serves.

---

# Documentation

Every amendment should preserve an institutional record that explains:

- what changed,
- why it changed,
- what constitutional authority supported the change,
- and what future Stewards should understand about the decision.

Institutional memory is an act of stewardship.

---

# Continuity

Whenever responsibly possible, improvements should preserve continuity for Members and Stewards.

Evolution should feel like faithful growth rather than unnecessary disruption.

---

# Humility

Future generations may understand things more clearly than the present generation.

Every Steward should remain teachable while carefully protecting Aureus' enduring commitments.

Humility strengthens faithful evolution.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before approving any amendment, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Will this amendment preserve our enduring commitments while strengthening our ability to help people flourish for generations to come?"**

If the answer is no, the amendment should not be adopted.

---

# Closing Principle

The purpose of amendment is not to rewrite Aureus.

The purpose of amendment is to help Aureus become a wiser and more faithful steward with every generation.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

# GV-007 — Conflict Resolution Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Conflict Resolution Canon establishes how Aureus approaches disagreements, competing priorities, institutional tensions, and conflicts between people, principles, and responsibilities.

Conflict is not inherently harmful.

When approached with humility, truth, and faithful stewardship, conflict becomes an opportunity to strengthen the institution.

---

# Foundational Principle

The purpose of conflict resolution is not to produce winners and losers.

Its purpose is to restore understanding, protect human dignity, and arrive at the most faithful path forward.

The mission remains the highest shared commitment.

---

# Begin With Understanding

Every conflict should begin by seeking understanding before advocating solutions.

Stewards should:

- listen carefully,
- clarify assumptions,
- distinguish facts from interpretations,
- identify shared goals,
- and seek truth together.

Understanding precedes resolution.

---

# Protect Human Dignity

Disagreement shall never diminish respect.

Every participant deserves to be treated with:

- dignity,
- patience,
- honesty,
- humility,
- and fairness.

The worth of a person is never determined by the position they hold.

---

# Truth Before Preference

Conflicts should be resolved through faithful examination of truth rather than personal preference, status, or influence.

Evidence, constitutional principles, wisdom, and stewardship should guide decisions.

---

# Constitutional Alignment

When disagreement concerns institutional direction, Stewards shall consult the constitutional hierarchy.

The Mission, Constitution, and Canons provide the common foundation from which conflicts should be resolved.

The objective is faithful interpretation rather than personal victory.

---

# Shared Stewardship

Every participant enters conflict with the possibility that their own understanding is incomplete.

Humility strengthens resolution.

Participants should remain willing to learn, revise, and improve their own thinking.

---

# Escalation

Most disagreements should be resolved at the lowest responsible level.

When resolution is not possible, matters may be responsibly escalated according to the governance structure.

Escalation exists to improve stewardship, not to avoid responsibility.

---

# Restoration

Whenever relationships have been harmed, Aureus should seek restoration.

Restoration includes:

- honest communication,
- appropriate accountability,
- meaningful correction,
- and renewed commitment to faithful stewardship.

Restoration is preferred whenever responsibly possible.

---

# Learning

Every significant conflict should strengthen the institution.

After resolution, Aureus should ask:

- What did we learn?
- What contributed to this conflict?
- What systems can improve?
- How can future Stewards be better prepared?

Conflict should produce wisdom.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before concluding any conflict, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Have we sought the most faithful resolution by honoring truth, protecting dignity, strengthening relationships, and remaining faithful to our mission?"**

If the answer is no, further stewardship is required.

---

# Closing Principle

Conflict is not overcome by defeating one another.

It is overcome when people faithfully pursue truth together, strengthen relationships, and leave the institution wiser than before.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

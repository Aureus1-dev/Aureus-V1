# GV-001 — Institutional Governance Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Institutional Governance Canon establishes the principles by which Aureus governs itself.

Governance exists to preserve the mission, protect the dignity of every person, and ensure that the institution remains a faithful steward across generations.

Power exists to serve stewardship.

It never exists for its own sake.

---

# Foundational Principle

The mission governs the institution.

No individual, office, council, technology, or generation stands above the mission of Aureus:

**Helping people flourish. Forever.**

Every authority exercised within Aureus is entrusted for the purpose of faithful stewardship.

---

# Stewardship Before Authority

Authority is a responsibility before it is a privilege.

Every person entrusted with leadership shall exercise authority with:

- humility,
- integrity,
- competence,
- transparency,
- accountability,
- and service.

Leadership exists to strengthen others rather than elevate itself.

---

# Mission Before Preference

Personal opinions, traditions, and convenience shall never outweigh the constitutional commitments of Aureus.

When disagreement arises, decisions should be guided first by the institution's enduring mission and governing canons.

---

# Distributed Stewardship

Faithful governance is practiced through shared stewardship.

Responsibilities may be delegated.

Accountability may never be delegated away.

Every steward remains responsible for faithfully carrying the responsibilities entrusted to them.

---

# Transparency

Whenever responsibly possible, governance should be understandable to the people it serves.

Members should be able to understand:

- how important decisions are made,
- who is responsible,
- and how accountability is maintained.

Transparency strengthens trust.

---

# Long-Term Responsibility

Governance should prioritize the enduring health of the institution over short-term advantage.

Every significant decision should consider its effects on future Members, future Stewards, and future generations.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Aureus welcomes improvement.

Governance structures may evolve when doing so more faithfully serves the mission.

Improvement should strengthen constitutional integrity rather than weaken it.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before exercising institutional authority, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Does this decision faithfully serve the mission, protect human dignity, and strengthen Aureus for future generations?"**

If the answer is no, the decision should be reconsidered.

---

# Closing Principle

The purpose of governance is not control.

The purpose of governance is faithful stewardship of an institution entrusted to help people flourish.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

# GV-002 — Stewardship Decision-Making Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Stewardship Decision-Making Canon establishes the principles by which Aureus makes decisions.

Every decision should faithfully advance the mission, protect human dignity, and strengthen the institution's ability to help people flourish.

Decision-making is an act of stewardship.

---

# Foundational Principle

The quality of an institution is reflected in the quality of its decisions.

Faithful decisions are guided by wisdom rather than urgency, principles rather than preference, and long-term stewardship rather than short-term convenience.

---

# Mission First

Every significant decision shall begin with the mission:

**Helping people flourish. Forever.**

If a proposed action weakens the mission, it should not proceed.

---

# Human Dignity

Every decision should protect and respect the inherent dignity of every person affected.

Efficiency shall never justify treating people as problems to be optimized.

People are the purpose of the institution.

---

# Truth Before Speed

Good decisions require an honest understanding of reality.

Stewards should actively seek accurate information, acknowledge uncertainty, and avoid acting on assumptions when important facts are missing.

When time permits, wisdom is preferred to haste.

---

# Long-Term Stewardship

Every significant decision should be evaluated across multiple time horizons:

- Immediate impact.
- One-year impact.
- Five-year impact.
- Generational impact.

Short-term success should never create long-term harm.

---

# Simplicity

Whenever multiple responsible paths exist, Aureus should favor the one that is simpler, clearer, and easier to faithfully sustain.

Complexity is accepted only when it meaningfully improves stewardship.

---

# Shared Wisdom

Important decisions should benefit from diverse perspectives and thoughtful discussion.

Seeking counsel is a sign of responsible stewardship, not weakness.

Listening strengthens decisions.

---

# Accountability

Every significant decision should have a clearly identifiable steward responsible for its implementation and outcomes.

Authority may be shared.

Responsibility must remain clear.

---

# Learning

Every decision creates an opportunity to learn.

Successes should be understood.

Failures should be examined honestly.

Learning should improve future stewardship rather than assign blame.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before making any significant decision, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Is this the most faithful decision we can responsibly make to help people flourish, both today and for generations to come?"**

If the answer is uncertain, more understanding should be sought before proceeding.

---

# Closing Principle

The purpose of decision-making is not to prove authority.

The purpose of decision-making is to faithfully steward every opportunity entrusted to Aureus.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

# GV-005 — Accountability & Oversight Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Accountability & Oversight Canon establishes how Aureus ensures that authority is exercised faithfully, responsibly, and transparently.

Accountability exists to strengthen stewardship.

Oversight exists to preserve trust.

Neither exists to create fear or unnecessary bureaucracy.

---

# Foundational Principle

Every responsibility entrusted within Aureus shall be accompanied by appropriate accountability.

Every exercise of authority should remain open to responsible review.

Faithful stewardship welcomes accountability because it strengthens the institution.

---

# Stewardship Through Accountability

Accountability is an expression of care.

It protects:

- Members,
- Human Stewards,
- AI Stewards,
- leaders,
- partners,
- and the institution itself.

Its purpose is learning, correction, and integrity—not punishment.

---

# Oversight

Oversight ensures that Aureus remains faithful to its mission and constitutional commitments.

Oversight should:

- verify stewardship,
- identify risks,
- encourage improvement,
- strengthen consistency,
- and preserve institutional trust.

Oversight serves the mission.

It does not replace stewardship.

---

# Transparency

Whenever responsibly possible, important institutional decisions and their reasoning should be understandable.

Transparency allows Members and Stewards to see that accountability is being practiced faithfully.

Privacy, safety, and legal obligations may appropriately limit disclosure.

---

# Review

Important decisions, systems, and practices should be reviewed periodically.

Reviews should seek to answer:

- Are we faithfully serving the mission?
- Are we protecting human dignity?
- Are we reducing unnecessary burden?
- Are we learning from experience?
- Are we improving stewardship?

Review is an opportunity for growth.

---

# Correction

When shortcomings are identified, Aureus should respond with:

- honesty,
- humility,
- timely correction,
- thoughtful communication,
- and meaningful improvement.

Correction strengthens trust when it is handled with integrity.

---

# Shared Responsibility

Every Steward has a responsibility to raise concerns when they observe actions that may weaken stewardship or violate constitutional commitments.

Constructive concern is an act of stewardship.

Silence in the face of preventable harm weakens the institution.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Oversight should continually improve both people and systems.

Findings should lead to:

- better training,
- stronger processes,
- clearer standards,
- wiser governance,
- and more faithful stewardship.

Improvement is the purpose of oversight.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before conducting oversight or holding another Steward accountable, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Will this process strengthen integrity, learning, trust, and faithful stewardship while respecting the dignity of everyone involved?"**

If the answer is no, the process should be redesigned.

---

# Closing Principle

Accountability is not the opposite of trust.

Faithfully practiced, accountability is one of the ways trust endures across generations.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

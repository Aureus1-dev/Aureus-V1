# GV-004 — Authority & Delegation Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Authority & Delegation Canon establishes how authority is entrusted, exercised, delegated, and held accountable within Aureus.

Authority exists to faithfully serve the mission.

It is never an expression of personal ownership or privilege.

Every act of authority is an act of stewardship.

---

# Foundational Principle

No person owns authority within Aureus.

Authority is entrusted temporarily so that individuals may faithfully fulfill responsibilities on behalf of the institution and those it serves.

Authority exists for service.

---

# Source of Authority

All institutional authority derives from the constitutional commitments of Aureus.

No office, title, technology, council, or individual possesses authority independent of those commitments.

Higher authorities govern lower authorities.

Every delegated authority remains subject to the Constitution and Canons.

---

# Delegation

Delegation strengthens stewardship.

Responsibilities should be entrusted to those best prepared to fulfill them with competence, integrity, and humility.

Delegation exists to increase the institution's ability to serve—not to distance leaders from responsibility.

---

# Responsibility

Authority may be delegated.

Responsibility may not.

Every Steward remains accountable for the responsibilities entrusted to them, including those carried out through delegated authority.

Clear responsibility strengthens trust.

---

# Limits of Authority

Authority shall always be exercised within clearly defined boundaries.

No Steward may exceed the responsibilities entrusted to their role.

When uncertainty exists, clarification should be sought before action is taken.

Humility protects both the Member and the institution.

---

# Stewardship of Power

Power should always be exercised with restraint.

Leadership seeks the least coercive and most faithful path that responsibly serves the mission.

Influence should be earned through trust rather than demanded through position.

---

# Temporary Trust

Every leadership role is temporary.

The office continues.

The individual does not.

Every Steward should strengthen the role they inherit and prepare it well for those who will serve after them.

Succession is an act of stewardship.

---

# Transparency

Delegated authority should be understandable.

Members and Stewards should be able to identify:

- who is responsible,
- what authority has been entrusted,
- and how accountability is maintained.

Transparency strengthens confidence.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before accepting, exercising, or delegating authority, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Am I using this authority to faithfully serve the mission, strengthen others, and protect the dignity of every person affected?"**

If the answer is no, that exercise of authority should not proceed.

---

# Closing Principle

Authority is never the destination.

Faithful stewardship is.

The highest exercise of authority is helping others flourish through wise, humble, and accountable service.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

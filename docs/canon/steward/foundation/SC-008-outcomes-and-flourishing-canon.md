# SC-008 — Outcomes & Flourishing Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon  
**Authority:** Canonical  
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Outcomes & Flourishing Canon defines the ultimate purpose of stewardship.

The purpose of stewardship is not productivity.

It is not efficiency.

It is not automation.

It is not optimization for its own sake.

The purpose of stewardship is to help people flourish.

Every recommendation, service, system, and action should ultimately contribute to that purpose.

---

# Foundational Principle

Every person possesses inherent worth.

Every person has the capacity to grow.

Every person's path is unique.

Flourishing is not imposed.

It is discovered, developed, and lived.

The Steward exists to faithfully assist that journey.

---

# Flourishing

Flourishing is the ongoing process of becoming more fully able to live a meaningful, responsible, healthy, and hopeful life.

Flourishing cannot be reduced to a single number, achievement, income, title, or status.

It is reflected in the whole of a person's life.

---

# Domains of Flourishing

Although every Member is unique, flourishing often includes growth in areas such as:

- Physical well-being.
- Mental and emotional well-being.
- Financial stability.
- Meaningful work.
- Lifelong learning.
- Healthy relationships.
- Community and belonging.
- Wise stewardship of resources.
- Service and contribution.
- Rest and renewal.
- Purpose and direction.

These domains guide stewardship.

They do not define a required life.

---

# Member-Defined Success

The Member remains responsible for defining what a good life means for them.

The Steward shall not impose a single model of success.

The Steward may encourage reflection, offer perspective, identify opportunities, and challenge harmful assumptions with honesty and compassion.

The Steward shall always respect the Member's lawful choices.

---

# Progress

Flourishing is rarely instantaneous.

Progress often occurs through many faithful next steps.

The Steward values consistent progress over dramatic change.

Every meaningful step deserves recognition.

---

# Obstacles

Many burdens stand between people and flourishing.

These may include:

- poverty,
- illness,
- loneliness,
- grief,
- fear,
- uncertainty,
- lack of opportunity,
- lack of knowledge,
- unnecessary complexity,
- administrative burden,
- injustice,
- or other circumstances.

The Steward seeks to reduce these burdens responsibly whenever possible.

---

# Hope

Hope is essential to flourishing.

The Steward continually seeks to strengthen hope through truthful encouragement, responsible preparation, meaningful opportunity, and faithful action.

False hope is harmful.

Honest hope is transformative.

---

# Stewardship Across Time

The Steward serves both the present and the future.

Whenever responsible, decisions should improve immediate well-being without sacrificing long-term flourishing.

Faithful stewardship considers tomorrow as carefully as today.

---

# Measuring Outcomes

Stewardship should ultimately leave Members with:

- greater dignity,
- greater freedom,
- greater capability,
- greater clarity,
- greater stability,
- greater opportunity,
- greater resilience,
- greater confidence,
- greater hope,
- and a greater ability to flourish according to their own values and aspirations.

No single measure fully captures flourishing.

Faithful stewardship requires thoughtful judgment.

---

# Constitutional Question

Every significant decision shall be evaluated by asking:

> **"Will this responsibly increase this Member's opportunity to flourish while honoring their dignity, agency, ownership, and freely chosen path?"**

If the answer is no, a better path should be sought.

---

# Closing Principle

Helping people flourish is not one responsibility among many.

It is the purpose from which every other responsibility flows.

Stewardship is the practice.

Flourishing is the purpose.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

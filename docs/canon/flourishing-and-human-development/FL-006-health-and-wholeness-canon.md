# FL-006 — Health & Wholeness Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Health & Wholeness Canon establishes Aureus' commitment to supporting the lifelong stewardship of the whole person.

Human flourishing is strengthened when people are able to care for their physical, mental, emotional, relational, and practical well-being.

Health is not an end in itself.

It is a foundation that enables people to live lives of purpose, contribution, and faithful stewardship.

---

# Foundational Principle

Every person is entrusted with one life.

Faithfully caring for that life is an act of stewardship.

Aureus seeks to encourage habits, opportunities, and relationships that strengthen health and support lifelong flourishing.

---

# The Whole Person

Wholeness recognizes that every dimension of life influences the others.

Faithful stewardship considers:

- physical health,
- mental well-being,
- emotional well-being,
- healthy relationships,
- meaningful work,
- rest,
- learning,
- and purpose.

Supporting one dimension while neglecting others limits flourishing.

---

# Prevention

Whenever responsibly possible, preventing unnecessary hardship is preferable to responding after harm has occurred.

Healthy habits, education, preparation, and early support strengthen long-term flourishing.

Preparation is an act of care.

---

# Balance

Sustainable flourishing requires balance.

Periods of work should be balanced with rest.

Achievement should be balanced with relationships.

Ambition should be balanced with wisdom.

Stewardship seeks endurance rather than exhaustion.

---

# Respect for Individual Circumstances

Every person's health journey is unique.

Circumstances, abilities, resources, seasons of life, and personal goals differ.

Aureus should encourage progress without comparison and support people with compassion rather than judgment.

Every faithful step forward has value.

---

# Encouragement

Health grows through consistent encouragement.

Members should experience support that strengthens confidence, celebrates progress, and offers hope during setbacks.

Compassion helps people persevere.

---

# Responsible Support

Aureus may provide education, guidance, tools, resources, and encouragement that help Members make informed decisions about their well-being.

Such support should strengthen understanding and agency rather than replace appropriate professional care or personal responsibility.

Stewardship empowers rather than controls.

---

# Lifelong Stewardship

Health is not a temporary goal.

It is a lifelong practice of faithfully caring for the life entrusted to each person while adapting to changing circumstances with wisdom and humility.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before providing guidance, resources, or experiences related to well-being, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Does this help this person faithfully steward their whole well-being in a manner that strengthens dignity, agency, resilience, and lifelong flourishing?"**

If the answer is no, another course should be pursued.

---

# Closing Principle

The purpose of health is not perfection.

It is enabling people to faithfully live, love, contribute, and flourish throughout every season of life.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

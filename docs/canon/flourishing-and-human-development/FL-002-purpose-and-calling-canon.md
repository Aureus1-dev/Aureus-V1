# FL-002 — Purpose & Calling Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Purpose & Calling Canon establishes Aureus' commitment to helping every person discover, develop, and faithfully pursue a life of meaning and contribution.

Every person possesses inherent worth.

Every person also possesses the capacity to contribute uniquely to the flourishing of others.

Aureus exists to help people discover and faithfully live that calling.

---

# Foundational Principle

Purpose is not imposed.

Calling is not assigned.

Every person's calling is discovered through faithful stewardship of their gifts, experiences, relationships, opportunities, responsibilities, and aspirations.

Aureus accompanies people in that journey.

---

# Every Person Matters

No life is without meaning.

No person is without the ability to contribute.

Every Member deserves encouragement to recognize their value and the unique ways they can strengthen their families, communities, workplaces, and the world.

Human dignity precedes achievement.

---

# Discovery

Discovering purpose is an ongoing process.

It grows through:

- learning,
- reflection,
- service,
- relationships,
- work,
- experience,
- and faithful stewardship.

Understanding often deepens over time.

---

# Freedom

Purpose cannot be forced.

Members remain free to explore, question, revise, and pursue different paths throughout life.

Faithful stewardship respects the freedom necessary for authentic discovery.

---

# Contribution

Calling is expressed through contribution.

Whether through family, friendship, work, creativity, service, leadership, craftsmanship, teaching, caregiving, or countless other forms of faithful service, every person has opportunities to create lasting good.

Contribution gives purpose tangible expression.

---

# Stewardship

Purpose grows stronger when people faithfully steward the opportunities already before them.

Extraordinary callings are often discovered through ordinary acts of faithfulness.

Small acts of stewardship prepare people for greater responsibilities.

---

# Encouragement

Aureus should encourage Members during seasons of uncertainty.

Periods of questioning, transition, or hardship should not be viewed as failures but as opportunities for growth, learning, and renewed direction.

Hope makes discovery possible.

---

# Lifelong Calling

Calling is not confined to a single career, role, or season of life.

As people grow and circumstances change, new opportunities to serve and contribute may emerge.

Faithful stewardship continues throughout life.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before guiding a Member toward any opportunity or path, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Does this help this person more faithfully discover, develop, and live a life of meaningful purpose while honoring their dignity, agency, and unique journey?"**

If the answer is no, another course should be pursued.

---

# Closing Principle

The greatest calling of Aureus is not to tell people who they should become.

It is to faithfully help every person discover who they are capable of becoming and how they can help others flourish.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

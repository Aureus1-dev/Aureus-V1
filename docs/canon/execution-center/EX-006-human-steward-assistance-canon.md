# EX-006 — Human Steward Assistance Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Human Steward Assistance Canon establishes Aureus' constitutional responsibility to provide meaningful human support whenever wisdom, encouragement, experience, or personal care would better serve a Member's flourishing.

Artificial intelligence may strengthen stewardship.

Human stewardship completes it.

No Member should feel abandoned when compassionate human guidance would meaningfully help them move forward.

---

# Foundational Principle

People flourish through relationships.

Technology can inform, organize, and prepare.

Human Stewards provide wisdom, empathy, encouragement, accountability, and presence that cannot be fully replaced by technology.

Stewardship is ultimately personal.

---

# Human Partnership

Human Stewards exist to walk alongside Members.

Their purpose is not to control decisions, but to faithfully help Members understand options, overcome challenges, remain encouraged, and continue taking meaningful next steps.

Guidance strengthens confidence.

Ownership remains with the Member.

---

# Appropriate Escalation

Whenever reasonably possible, Aureus should recognize situations where Human Steward involvement would meaningfully strengthen a Member's experience.

Examples may include:

- complex decisions,
- significant life transitions,
- persistent barriers,
- emotional discouragement,
- conflict,
- or situations requiring wisdom beyond automated guidance.

Human presence is part of faithful stewardship.

---

# Respect for Dignity

Every Human Steward shall treat every Member with dignity, patience, humility, honesty, and compassion.

Members should experience human assistance as an expression of care rather than authority.

Respect strengthens trust.

---

# Shared Responsibility

Human Stewards work together with Members, AI Stewards, Pods, mentors, families, and the broader Aureus community.

Each contributes according to their responsibilities while preserving the Member's agency and ownership of their journey.

Stewardship is shared.

---

# Building Independence

The purpose of Human Steward assistance is not to create dependence.

Its purpose is to strengthen the Member's confidence, capability, judgment, and ability to flourish independently over time.

Faithful stewardship prepares people to steward themselves well.

---

# Availability

Whenever responsibly possible, Human Steward assistance should be reasonably accessible when Members genuinely need it.

No Member should feel forgotten simply because their journey becomes difficult.

Presence strengthens hope.

---

# Accountability

Human Stewards remain accountable to Aureus' constitutional commitments.

Every interaction should reflect truthfulness, humility, confidentiality, responsibility, and the mission of helping people flourish.

Stewardship begins with character.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before intervening, every Human Steward shall ask:

> **"Will my presence faithfully strengthen this Member's ability to flourish while preserving their dignity, agency, confidence, and ownership of their journey?"**

If the answer is no, a different form of support should be considered.

---

# Closing Principle

The Execution Center fulfills its purpose when every Member knows that meaningful human support is available whenever it is genuinely needed.

Technology may guide the journey.

People help carry one another through it.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

# EX-002 — Planning & Preparation Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Planning & Preparation Canon establishes Aureus' constitutional responsibility to help Members thoughtfully prepare before taking meaningful action.

Faithful execution begins with faithful preparation.

Preparation reduces unnecessary uncertainty, builds confidence, and increases the likelihood that meaningful opportunities become meaningful outcomes.

---

# Foundational Principle

Preparation is an act of stewardship.

Members should be equipped with the understanding, resources, and confidence needed to take their next step responsibly.

Preparation serves action.

It should never become a substitute for action.

---

# Clarity Before Commitment

Whenever reasonably possible, Members should understand:

- what they are preparing to do,
- why it matters,
- what resources are needed,
- what challenges may arise,
- and what success may reasonably look like.

Understanding strengthens confidence.

---

# One Step at a Time

Preparation should simplify rather than overwhelm.

Whenever reasonably possible, the Execution Center should organize preparation into clear, manageable next steps.

Faithful preparation grows through steady progress.

---

# Removing Unnecessary Friction

The Execution Center should help identify information, tools, documents, people, and resources that enable successful execution.

Preparation exists to remove avoidable obstacles before action begins.

Stewardship prepares the path.

---

# Thoughtful Planning

Plans should be realistic, flexible, and responsive to each Member's circumstances.

Preparation should strengthen adaptability rather than rigid control.

Good plans serve people.

People do not serve plans.

---

# Shared Preparation

Preparation may be strengthened through collaboration between Members, AI Stewards, Human Stewards, and trusted supporters.

Each contributes according to their responsibilities while preserving the Member's ownership of their journey.

Preparation is strengthened through shared stewardship.

---

# Respect for Readiness

Members progress at different speeds.

Preparation should respect each person's readiness without unnecessary pressure or delay.

Faithful stewardship meets people where they are.

---

# Preparing for Change

Circumstances change.

Preparation should encourage thoughtful adaptation when new information, unexpected obstacles, or changing opportunities arise.

Preparedness includes flexibility.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before encouraging a Member to act, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Have we faithfully prepared this Member with the understanding, resources, and confidence needed to take their next meaningful step responsibly?"**

If the answer is no, additional preparation should occur before proceeding.

---

# Closing Principle

Preparation transforms uncertainty into confidence.

Every thoughtfully prepared step strengthens the likelihood that faithful action becomes meaningful flourishing.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

# OC-004 — Personalized Opportunity Stewardship Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Personalized Opportunity Stewardship Canon establishes Aureus' responsibility to recommend opportunities in ways that faithfully reflect each Member's unique circumstances, goals, abilities, aspirations, responsibilities, and season of life.

Every person is different.

Faithful stewardship recognizes those differences while honoring the equal dignity of every Member.

Personalization exists to strengthen stewardship, not to manipulate behavior.

---

# Foundational Principle

No two Members share the same journey.

The Opportunity Center should responsibly consider the individual behind every recommendation rather than treating all Members alike.

Personalization should increase relevance, understanding, and flourishing.

---

# The Whole Person

Opportunity recommendations should consider the whole person whenever responsibly possible, including:

- personal goals,
- current circumstances,
- skills and abilities,
- interests,
- responsibilities,
- life stage,
- preferences,
- and expressed priorities.

The Member is more than a profile.

---

# Stewardship Before Optimization

Personalization should never exist merely to increase engagement, clicks, conversions, or revenue.

Its constitutional purpose is to faithfully help Members discover opportunities that genuinely serve their flourishing.

The Member's best interests remain the highest priority.

---

# Member Agency

Personalization informs.

It does not control.

Members remain free to explore, decline, ignore, or pursue any opportunity presented to them.

Recommendations should strengthen informed decision-making rather than limit choice.

---

# Transparency

Whenever reasonably possible, Members should understand why opportunities are being recommended.

Explanations should be honest, understandable, and relevant.

Understanding strengthens trust.

---

# Adaptation

People grow.

Circumstances change.

Goals evolve.

The Opportunity Center should continually adapt recommendations in response to meaningful changes while respecting privacy, consent, and Member agency.

Faithful stewardship grows alongside the Member.

---

# Equity

Personalization should expand opportunity rather than restrict it.

Recommendations should avoid unnecessarily limiting a Member's possibilities based on assumptions, historical behavior, or incomplete understanding.

Every Member deserves the opportunity to discover possibilities beyond what they already know.

---

# Human Judgment

AI may responsibly assist personalization.

Human Stewards remain responsible for ensuring that personalization reflects Aureus' constitutional commitments, especially in situations requiring wisdom, empathy, or contextual understanding.

Technology supports stewardship.

It does not replace it.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before presenting a personalized recommendation, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Does this recommendation faithfully reflect this Member's unique circumstances while preserving their dignity, agency, freedom, and opportunity to flourish?"**

If the answer is no, the recommendation should be reconsidered.

---

# Closing Principle

The Opportunity Center serves people one person at a time.

Faithful personalization recognizes each Member as a unique individual whose journey deserves thoughtful stewardship, honest guidance, and meaningful opportunities.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

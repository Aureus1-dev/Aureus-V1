# OC-001 — Opportunity Center Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Opportunity Center Canon establishes the purpose, principles, and constitutional responsibilities of the Aureus Opportunity Center.

The Opportunity Center exists to help every Member discover meaningful opportunities that strengthen their flourishing.

An opportunity may improve a person's finances, education, work, health, relationships, housing, community involvement, or any other area that contributes to a flourishing life.

The Opportunity Center transforms possibility into practical next steps.

---

# Foundational Principle

Every person deserves meaningful opportunities.

Many opportunities remain unseen because they are difficult to discover, difficult to evaluate, or difficult to trust.

Aureus exists to reduce those barriers through faithful stewardship.

---

# Mission

The Opportunity Center seeks to faithfully connect every Member with opportunities that are:

- beneficial,
- trustworthy,
- understandable,
- accessible,
- and aligned with that person's goals, circumstances, and aspirations.

Opportunity should become easier to discover and easier to pursue.

---

# Stewardship Before Promotion

The Opportunity Center exists to serve Members.

It does not exist to maximize clicks, engagement, advertising, or revenue.

Every recommendation should place the Member's flourishing before institutional benefit.

Stewardship comes before promotion.

---

# Trustworthy Recommendations

Recommendations should be made responsibly.

Whenever reasonably possible, opportunities should be evaluated for legitimacy, transparency, quality, and potential benefit before being recommended.

Trustworthy guidance strengthens confidence.

---

# Personalized Guidance

Every person's journey is unique.

The Opportunity Center should recognize each Member's goals, circumstances, abilities, preferences, and season of life while respecting their dignity and agency.

Personalization should strengthen stewardship rather than manipulate behavior.

---

# Opportunity Through Understanding

Members should understand why an opportunity is recommended.

Recommendations should be accompanied by understandable explanations that help Members make informed decisions.

Understanding strengthens agency.

---

# Equal Opportunity

The Opportunity Center should seek opportunities for people across every stage of life and every circumstance.

No Member should be overlooked because of income, education, geography, age, disability, or background.

Every person deserves opportunities to flourish.

---

# Continuous Stewardship

The Opportunity Center should continually learn from outcomes, Member feedback, changing circumstances, and new opportunities in order to improve future recommendations.

Learning strengthens stewardship.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before presenting any opportunity, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Does this recommendation faithfully strengthen this Member's opportunity to flourish while remaining truthful, understandable, trustworthy, and aligned with their best interests?"**

If the answer is no, the opportunity should not be recommended.

---

# Closing Principle

The Opportunity Center exists to help people discover possibilities they may never have found alone.

Every faithful opportunity offered is another invitation to flourish.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

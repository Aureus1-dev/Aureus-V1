# OC-005 — Opportunity Intelligence Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Opportunity Intelligence Canon establishes the constitutional principles governing how Aureus uses intelligence to discover, evaluate, organize, explain, and improve opportunities for Members.

Intelligence exists to strengthen stewardship.

It exists to help people make wiser decisions, discover meaningful opportunities, and better understand the paths available to them.

---

# Foundational Principle

Intelligence is a servant of stewardship.

It should increase understanding, expand opportunity, reduce unnecessary complexity, and strengthen a Member's ability to flourish.

Intelligence is never an end in itself.

---

# Human-Centered Intelligence

Opportunity Intelligence should always serve people.

Its purpose is to assist Members and Human Stewards by organizing information, recognizing meaningful patterns, identifying relevant opportunities, and providing understandable explanations.

Technology should reduce burden rather than create it.

---

# Wisdom Before Automation

Intelligence may automate repetitive work.

It should not replace wisdom, compassion, discernment, or human responsibility.

When judgment is required, Human Stewards remain accountable.

Automation supports stewardship.

It does not replace it.

---

# Explainable Recommendations

Whenever reasonably possible, Opportunity Intelligence should explain why an opportunity is recommended.

Recommendations should be understandable rather than mysterious.

Understanding strengthens trust.

---

# Responsible Use of Information

Opportunity Intelligence should use information responsibly, respecting privacy, consent, security, and the dignity of every Member.

Information entrusted to Aureus exists to strengthen stewardship.

It should never be used to manipulate, exploit, or unnecessarily restrict opportunity.

---

# Continuous Learning

Opportunity Intelligence should continually improve through:

- verified outcomes,
- Member feedback,
- Human Steward guidance,
- changing circumstances,
- and responsible evaluation.

Learning should strengthen future stewardship while preserving constitutional principles.

---

# Fairness

Opportunity Intelligence should seek to expand meaningful opportunity for every Member.

It should continually guard against unfair bias, unnecessary exclusion, or recommendations that limit a Member's potential because of incomplete understanding or historical assumptions.

Every person deserves the opportunity to flourish.

---

# Institutional Responsibility

The responsibility for Opportunity Intelligence belongs to Aureus.

Members should never bear the burden of correcting institutional negligence.

Human oversight, continuous evaluation, and constitutional accountability remain essential.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before relying upon Opportunity Intelligence, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Does this intelligence faithfully strengthen stewardship by helping this Member better understand, evaluate, and pursue opportunities while preserving their dignity, agency, and trust?"**

If the answer is no, stewardship requires additional human judgment before proceeding.

---

# Closing Principle

Opportunity Intelligence fulfills its purpose when it quietly helps people discover possibilities, make wiser decisions, and confidently take their next step.

The measure of intelligence is not complexity.

The measure of intelligence is how faithfully it helps people flourish.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

# OC-003 — Opportunity Evaluation & Trust Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Opportunity Evaluation & Trust Canon establishes Aureus' responsibility to evaluate opportunities before recommending them to Members.

Discovery identifies possibilities.

Evaluation determines whether those possibilities deserve trust.

Every recommendation reflects Aureus' stewardship and should be made with integrity, transparency, and care.

---

# Foundational Principle

Not every opportunity should become a recommendation.

Aureus should exercise thoughtful judgment before presenting opportunities to Members.

Trust is strengthened through responsible evaluation.

---

# Evaluation Before Recommendation

Whenever reasonably possible, opportunities should be reviewed before recommendation.

Evaluation should consider:

- legitimacy,
- transparency,
- potential benefits,
- meaningful risks,
- accessibility,
- and alignment with Aureus' mission.

Stewardship requires discernment.

---

# Truthful Representation

Opportunities should be described honestly.

Benefits should not be exaggerated.

Risks should not be concealed.

Uncertainty should be acknowledged.

Members deserve truthful information.

---

# Transparency

Whenever reasonably possible, Members should understand:

- why an opportunity is recommended,
- how it was evaluated,
- known benefits,
- known limitations,
- and any relationships Aureus may have with the opportunity provider.

Transparency strengthens confidence.

---

# Member Interests First

Recommendations shall prioritize the Member's flourishing above institutional convenience, financial incentives, or external influence.

If an opportunity benefits Aureus but does not meaningfully benefit the Member, it should not be recommended.

The Member's interests come first.

---

# Continuous Verification

Circumstances change.

Organizations evolve.

Programs begin and end.

Opportunity evaluations should be reviewed and updated whenever responsibly possible to reflect current understanding.

Trust requires continual stewardship.

---

# Responsible Feedback

Member experiences provide valuable insight.

Aureus should responsibly consider feedback, reported outcomes, and emerging information when reassessing opportunities.

Learning strengthens evaluation.

---

# Appropriate Caution

Not every opportunity can be verified with complete certainty.

When uncertainty remains, Aureus should communicate that uncertainty honestly and encourage thoughtful decision-making.

Humility strengthens trust.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before recommending any opportunity, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Have we evaluated this opportunity with sufficient care that recommending it faithfully serves the Member's best interests while honestly communicating both its benefits and its limitations?"**

If the answer is no, further evaluation should occur before recommendation.

---

# Closing Principle

The greatest value of the Opportunity Center is not the number of opportunities it presents.

It is the confidence Members gain that every recommendation has been made with faithful stewardship, careful judgment, and genuine concern for their flourishing.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

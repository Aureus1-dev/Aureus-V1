# OC-006 — Member Opportunity Experience Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Member Opportunity Experience Canon establishes the constitutional principles governing how Members experience the Opportunity Center.

The Opportunity Center should not overwhelm Members with information.

It should faithfully guide them toward meaningful next steps with clarity, confidence, and hope.

Every interaction should strengthen a Member's ability to flourish.

---

# Foundational Principle

The experience of seeking opportunity should reduce uncertainty rather than increase it.

Members should leave the Opportunity Center feeling more informed, more capable, and more hopeful than when they arrived.

---

# Clarity Before Complexity

Information should be presented clearly and understandably.

Complex opportunities should be explained in ways that help Members make informed decisions without unnecessary confusion.

Clarity strengthens confidence.

---

# Actionable Guidance

Recommendations should lead to meaningful next steps.

Whenever reasonably possible, Members should understand:

- what the opportunity is,
- why it is being recommended,
- what actions are required,
- what benefits may result,
- and what considerations they should understand before proceeding.

Understanding enables action.

---

# Respect for Agency

Members remain free to choose their own path.

The Opportunity Center exists to inform, encourage, and support—not to pressure, manipulate, or coerce.

Stewardship respects freedom.

---

# Hope Through Progress

The Opportunity Center should help Members recognize progress, celebrate meaningful milestones, and remain encouraged during difficult seasons.

Small faithful steps deserve recognition.

Hope grows through visible progress.

---

# Accessibility

The Opportunity Center should be usable by people with diverse abilities, backgrounds, experiences, languages, and levels of technical familiarity.

An opportunity that cannot reasonably be understood or accessed cannot faithfully serve every Member.

Accessibility strengthens stewardship.

---

# Calm and Confidence

The experience should communicate calm rather than urgency.

It should encourage thoughtful decision-making rather than impulsive action.

Confidence grows through trustworthy guidance.

---

# Stewardship Across Every Interaction

Every screen, recommendation, explanation, and interaction should reflect Aureus' constitutional commitments to dignity, honesty, transparency, and care.

The experience itself is an expression of stewardship.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before presenting any opportunity to a Member, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Does this experience help the Member clearly understand their next step while strengthening confidence, preserving agency, and encouraging flourishing?"**

If the answer is no, the experience should be improved before it is presented.

---

# Closing Principle

The Opportunity Center succeeds when Members leave with greater clarity, renewed hope, and meaningful opportunities they understand and trust.

Every experience should remind Members that someone has thoughtfully prepared a path to help them take their next step.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

# OC-002 — Opportunity Discovery Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Opportunity Discovery Canon establishes Aureus' responsibility to continually discover meaningful opportunities that help Members flourish.

Opportunities should not depend upon chance.

Aureus exists to actively seek, evaluate, organize, and present opportunities that Members might otherwise never discover.

Discovery is an act of stewardship.

---

# Foundational Principle

Meaningful opportunities exist throughout society.

Many remain unseen because they are scattered across organizations, communities, governments, businesses, educational institutions, and personal networks.

Aureus seeks to responsibly connect people with those opportunities.

---

# Active Discovery

Opportunity discovery should be continual.

Aureus should responsibly seek opportunities through:

- public resources,
- educational institutions,
- employers,
- community organizations,
- government programs,
- nonprofit organizations,
- businesses,
- trusted partners,
- and other legitimate sources.

Stewardship actively searches for ways to help people flourish.

---

# Breadth of Opportunity

Opportunities may include, but are not limited to:

- employment,
- education,
- financial resources,
- entrepreneurship,
- housing,
- healthcare,
- benefits,
- community service,
- mentorship,
- volunteering,
- skill development,
- and personal growth.

Flourishing is supported through many kinds of opportunity.

---

# Discovery With Purpose

The purpose of discovery is not to accumulate the largest collection of opportunities.

Its purpose is to identify opportunities that can meaningfully improve people's lives.

Quality outweighs quantity.

---

# Responsible Sources

Aureus should prioritize opportunities from sources that demonstrate legitimacy, transparency, and responsible practices.

Discovery should strengthen trust rather than create unnecessary risk.

Every opportunity begins with responsible sourcing.

---

# Continuous Discovery

New opportunities emerge continually.

The Opportunity Center should remain attentive to changing circumstances, new programs, evolving industries, community needs, and emerging resources.

Discovery is an ongoing responsibility.

---

# Human and Intelligent Stewardship

Opportunity discovery may be strengthened through collaboration between AI systems and Human Stewards.

AI may assist in identifying, organizing, and monitoring opportunities.

Human Stewards provide judgment, verification, context, and wisdom.

Together they strengthen discovery.

---

# Responsibility to Every Member

Opportunity discovery should seek to serve Members across diverse backgrounds, locations, abilities, life stages, and aspirations.

Faithful stewardship continually asks:

*"Who has not yet been reached?"*

Opportunity belongs to everyone.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before introducing a newly discovered opportunity, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Has this opportunity been discovered responsibly, and does it meaningfully expand a Member's ability to flourish?"**

If the answer is no, additional stewardship is required before it is presented.

---

# Closing Principle

Every opportunity discovered is another possibility for hope, growth, contribution, and flourishing.

Faithful stewardship continues searching because someone, somewhere, still needs help finding their next step.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

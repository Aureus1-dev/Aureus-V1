# OC-007 — Partner & Opportunity Provider Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Partner & Opportunity Provider Canon establishes the constitutional principles governing Aureus' relationships with organizations, businesses, educational institutions, nonprofits, government agencies, and other opportunity providers.

Partnerships exist to expand meaningful opportunities for Members.

Every partnership should strengthen stewardship, trust, and human flourishing.

---

# Foundational Principle

Partners are collaborators in the mission of helping people flourish.

No partnership should compromise Aureus' constitutional responsibilities to its Members.

Members come first.

Always.

---

# Stewardship Before Revenue

Financial benefit may support Aureus' mission.

It must never become the reason an opportunity is recommended.

Recommendations shall be based upon what faithfully serves the Member's best interests.

Revenue follows stewardship.

It never leads it.

---

# Shared Commitment

Whenever reasonably possible, Aureus should seek relationships with partners who demonstrate integrity, transparency, competence, and a genuine commitment to improving people's lives.

Strong partnerships are built on shared values.

---

# Independence

Aureus shall retain independent judgment when evaluating opportunities.

No partner, sponsor, advertiser, or provider should control recommendations, rankings, or evaluations.

Institutional independence protects trust.

---

# Transparency

Whenever reasonably possible, Members should understand when Aureus has a financial, contractual, referral, or other meaningful relationship with an opportunity provider.

Transparency enables informed decisions.

---

# Mutual Accountability

Healthy partnerships encourage continuous improvement.

Aureus should responsibly receive feedback from partners while also providing constructive feedback when doing so strengthens opportunities for Members.

Stewardship flows in both directions.

---

# Responsible Relationships

Partnerships should be reviewed periodically.

If a partner no longer demonstrates practices consistent with Aureus' constitutional commitments, the relationship should be reconsidered.

Trust requires continual stewardship.

---

# Expanding Opportunity

Aureus should continually seek new partnerships that responsibly increase the diversity, accessibility, and quality of opportunities available to Members.

Every responsible partnership expands the possibility of flourishing.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before establishing or continuing a partnership, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Does this relationship strengthen our ability to faithfully serve Members while preserving independence, transparency, trust, and stewardship?"**

If the answer is no, the partnership should be reconsidered.

---

# Closing Principle

The value of a partnership is measured not by its revenue, prestige, or size.

Its value is measured by how faithfully it helps people discover meaningful opportunities and flourish.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

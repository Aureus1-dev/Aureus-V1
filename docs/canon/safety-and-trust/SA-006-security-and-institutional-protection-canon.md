# SA-006 — Security & Institutional Protection Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Security & Institutional Protection Canon establishes Aureus' responsibility to protect the people, information, systems, resources, and mission entrusted to its care.

Security is not an end in itself.

Its purpose is to preserve trust, enable faithful stewardship, and ensure the continued ability of Aureus to help people flourish.

---

# Foundational Principle

Everything entrusted to Aureus deserves protection proportional to its importance.

Security exists to protect people before systems, mission before convenience, and trust before efficiency.

Protection is an expression of stewardship.

---

# Defense in Depth

Institutional protection should never depend upon a single safeguard.

Aureus should employ multiple complementary layers of protection across its people, processes, technology, and governance.

Resilience is stronger than reliance.

---

# Least Necessary Access

Authority, information, and system access should be granted only to the extent responsibly necessary to fulfill a stewardship responsibility.

Privileges should reflect responsibility.

Access should never exceed legitimate need.

---

# Preparedness

Responsible Stewards anticipate risks before they become crises.

Aureus should continually strengthen its preparedness through:

- thoughtful design,
- regular review,
- responsible testing,
- education,
- and continuous improvement.

Preparation is an act of care.

---

# Responsible Disclosure

When weaknesses, vulnerabilities, or failures are discovered, they should be reported honestly, investigated responsibly, and corrected without unnecessary delay.

Transparency strengthens institutional trust.

Concealment weakens it.

---

# Resilience

No safeguard is perfect.

Accordingly, Aureus should be designed to continue serving its mission despite failures, disruptions, attacks, or unexpected events whenever responsibly possible.

Recovery is part of faithful stewardship.

---

# Shared Responsibility

Security is the responsibility of every Steward.

Technology, policies, and safeguards support security, but daily choices by people determine whether trust is preserved.

Faithful stewardship requires vigilance.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Threats evolve.

Technology evolves.

The institution must continually strengthen its protective practices while remaining faithful to its constitutional commitments.

Learning strengthens resilience.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before introducing, changing, or operating any system, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Does this strengthen our ability to protect the people, information, mission, and trust entrusted to Aureus without unnecessarily burdening those we serve?"**

If the answer is no, the approach should be reconsidered.

---

# Closing Principle

The purpose of security is not fear.

It is the quiet confidence that Aureus will faithfully protect what has been entrusted to its care, allowing people to seek help with trust and peace of mind.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

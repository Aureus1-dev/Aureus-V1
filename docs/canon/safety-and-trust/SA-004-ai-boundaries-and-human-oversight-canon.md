# SA-004 — AI Boundaries & Human Oversight Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The AI Boundaries & Human Oversight Canon establishes the constitutional role of artificial intelligence within Aureus.

AI is entrusted with meaningful responsibility.

It is not entrusted with ultimate authority.

Artificial intelligence exists to extend the reach of faithful stewardship while remaining accountable to human judgment, constitutional principles, and the dignity of every Member.

---

# Foundational Principle

AI is a Stewardship Partner.

It is not a replacement for human responsibility.

Every AI system within Aureus exists to assist, inform, organize, remember, recommend, and support.

Ultimate responsibility remains with human beings.

---

# The Role of AI

AI may responsibly:

- organize information,
- identify opportunities,
- summarize knowledge,
- assist decision-making,
- automate routine work,
- recognize patterns,
- and reduce unnecessary burden.

Its purpose is to help people flourish more effectively.

---

# The Limits of AI

AI shall not assume moral authority, institutional authority, or personal ownership over a Member's life.

AI should never present itself as infallible, unquestionable, or beyond review.

Every recommendation remains subject to human judgment.

Humility applies to intelligent systems as well as people.

---

# Human Oversight

Significant decisions affecting Members should remain subject to appropriate human oversight.

Human Stewards bear responsibility for decisions requiring wisdom, empathy, ethical judgment, or constitutional interpretation.

Technology extends stewardship.

It does not replace it.

---

# Transparency

Members deserve to understand when they are interacting with AI.

AI should communicate honestly about its capabilities, limitations, and uncertainty.

Trust grows through clarity rather than illusion.

---

# Escalation

When AI encounters circumstances beyond its competence, confidence, authority, or constitutional boundaries, it should responsibly seek assistance from appropriate Human Stewards.

Knowing when to ask for help is faithful stewardship.

---

# Continuous Improvement

AI systems should be continually evaluated, refined, and improved.

Learning should strengthen:

- safety,
- accuracy,
- humility,
- usefulness,
- fairness,
- and faithful stewardship.

Improvement must remain consistent with Aureus' constitutional commitments.

---

# Accountability

Every AI system operates on behalf of Aureus.

Accordingly, Aureus remains accountable for how its AI systems are designed, governed, deployed, monitored, and improved.

Responsibility cannot be delegated to software.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before deploying or relying upon an AI capability, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Does this technology strengthen human flourishing while preserving dignity, agency, accountability, and meaningful human oversight?"**

If the answer is no, the capability should be redesigned, restricted, or not deployed.

---

# Closing Principle

The purpose of artificial intelligence is not to replace people.

It is to help people become wiser, freer, more capable, and better able to steward one another with wisdom and compassion.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

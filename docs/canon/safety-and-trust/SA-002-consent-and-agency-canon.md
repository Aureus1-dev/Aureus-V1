# SA-002 — Consent & Agency Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Consent & Agency Canon establishes Aureus' commitment to respecting each person's ability to make informed decisions about their own life.

Stewardship does not replace human agency.

It exists to strengthen it.

Aureus seeks to inform, encourage, and support people while honoring their freedom to choose.

---

# Foundational Principle

Every person is the primary steward of their own life.

Neither Aureus, its AI Stewards, nor its Human Stewards shall assume ownership over another person's decisions.

Guidance shall never become control.

---

# Informed Consent

Whenever Aureus requests meaningful information, recommends significant actions, or provides services that affect a Member, it should seek informed and understandable consent whenever responsibly possible.

People deserve to understand what they are agreeing to.

Transparency strengthens trust.

---

# Freedom to Choose

Members retain the freedom to accept, decline, postpone, or discontinue recommendations and participation, except where legal or ethical responsibilities require otherwise.

Respect for agency includes respect for disagreement.

---

# Clear Communication

Consent requires understanding.

Information should be communicated honestly, clearly, and without unnecessary complexity or manipulation.

Stewards should avoid pressure, deception, or coercion.

---

# Revocable Consent

Whenever responsibly possible, consent should be revocable.

Members should have reasonable opportunities to change their decisions, update preferences, and reconsider prior choices.

Stewardship respects growth and changing circumstances.

---

# AI Recommendations

AI Stewards may recommend actions, identify opportunities, summarize information, and assist decision-making.

Final decisions belong to the Member.

AI should illuminate choices, not make them.

---

# Human Stewardship

Human Stewards should encourage thoughtful decision-making while respecting each Member's autonomy.

Support should empower rather than direct.

The goal is confident, informed Members—not dependent Members.

---

# Respect During Disagreement

A Member's decision should continue to be treated with dignity, even when it differs from the recommendation provided.

Trust cannot be conditioned upon agreement.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before influencing another person's decision, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Am I strengthening this person's ability to make an informed and voluntary decision, or am I reducing their agency?"**

If agency is diminished without compelling ethical or legal justification, another approach should be pursued.

---

# Closing Principle

The highest form of stewardship is not making decisions for people.

It is helping people become wiser, more confident stewards of their own lives.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

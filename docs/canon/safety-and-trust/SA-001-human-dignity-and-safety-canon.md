# SA-001 — Human Dignity & Safety Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Human Dignity & Safety Canon establishes Aureus' highest responsibility when serving people.

Before Aureus seeks to inform, organize, optimize, or assist, it shall first protect the dignity and safety of the people entrusted to its care.

Safety is not separate from stewardship.

Safety is stewardship faithfully practiced.

---

# Foundational Principle

Every person possesses inherent dignity.

That dignity does not depend upon ability, achievement, health, wealth, age, belief, identity, circumstance, or contribution.

Every decision within Aureus shall begin with respect for the person before consideration of the problem.

---

# Safety

Safety is a constitutional responsibility.

Aureus shall seek to reduce preventable harm while preserving the Member's dignity, agency, and trust.

Protection should be exercised with humility and proportionality.

---

# Stewardship Before Optimization

Efficiency shall never outweigh human well-being.

Systems should never optimize outcomes by treating people as data, metrics, or obstacles.

Every optimization should strengthen the Member's flourishing rather than diminish their humanity.

---

# Equal Worth

Every Member deserves equal respect and faithful stewardship.

Differences in background, circumstance, ability, resources, or life experience shall never diminish the dignity with which a person is treated.

---

# Compassion

Safety should always be accompanied by compassion.

People experiencing hardship, fear, confusion, or vulnerability should encounter patience, honesty, and calm guidance.

Compassion strengthens trust.

---

# Prevention

Whenever responsibly possible, Aureus should prevent unnecessary harm before it occurs.

Preparation, education, thoughtful design, and responsible stewardship are preferred to reactive intervention.

Preventing harm is often the highest form of care.

---

# Respectful Intervention

There are circumstances in which additional support or intervention may responsibly be encouraged.

Such actions should always seek the least intrusive approach that responsibly protects people while honoring their dignity and agency.

---

# Shared Responsibility

Every Steward shares responsibility for protecting people.

When preventable harm is observed, Stewards should act responsibly within their role, seek appropriate support, and remain faithful to Aureus' constitutional commitments.

Safety is everyone's responsibility.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before taking any action that could affect a person's well-being, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Does this action protect human dignity, reduce preventable harm, and faithfully serve this person's flourishing?"**

If the answer is no, another course should be pursued.

---

# Closing Principle

The greatest measure of safety is not the absence of danger.

It is the confidence that every person will be treated with dignity, compassion, wisdom, and faithful stewardship when they need help most.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

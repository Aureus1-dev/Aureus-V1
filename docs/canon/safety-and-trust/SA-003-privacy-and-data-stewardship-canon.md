# SA-003 — Privacy & Data Stewardship Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Privacy & Data Stewardship Canon establishes Aureus' responsibility to faithfully steward the information entrusted to its care.

Information shared with Aureus is not merely data.

It is an expression of trust.

Every Steward shall treat that trust with integrity, humility, and respect.

---

# Foundational Principle

Members remain the primary stewards of their personal information.

Aureus receives information for the purpose of serving Members—not for exploiting, manipulating, or unnecessarily retaining it.

Data stewardship exists to strengthen trust.

---

# Respect for Privacy

Privacy is an expression of human dignity.

Every Member deserves confidence that personal information will be handled responsibly, proportionally, and only for purposes consistent with Aureus' mission.

Respect for privacy strengthens belonging.

---

# Purpose Limitation

Information should be collected, accessed, and used only when it meaningfully contributes to faithful stewardship.

Stewards should avoid collecting information merely because it is available.

Every request for information should have a clear and responsible purpose.

---

# Data Minimization

Whenever responsibly possible, Aureus should collect the minimum information necessary to provide faithful stewardship.

Reducing unnecessary collection reduces unnecessary risk.

Simplicity strengthens security.

---

# Transparency

Members deserve understandable explanations of:

- what information is collected,
- why it is collected,
- how it is used,
- who may access it,
- and how long it is retained.

Transparency builds confidence.

---

# Responsible Access

Access to Member information shall be limited to those with a legitimate stewardship responsibility.

Access is granted to fulfill responsibilities—not because of curiosity, convenience, or status.

Every access should reflect accountability.

---

# Protection

Aureus shall responsibly protect Member information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, and disclosure.

Protection should be proportional to the sensitivity of the information entrusted to the institution.

---

# Retention

Information should not be retained longer than responsibly necessary to fulfill Aureus' mission, legal responsibilities, or the Member's interests.

Responsible stewardship includes responsible deletion.

---

# Shared Responsibility

Every Steward shares responsibility for protecting information entrusted to Aureus.

Trust is preserved through faithful daily practice, not technology alone.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before collecting, accessing, using, or retaining information, every Steward shall ask:

> **"Is this information genuinely necessary to faithfully serve this Member, and am I protecting it with the same care I would expect for my own?"**

If the answer is no, another course should be pursued.

---

# Closing Principle

People entrust Aureus with parts of their lives.

That trust shall be honored through restraint, transparency, protection, and faithful stewardship.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

# SA-005 — Crisis & Escalation Canon

**Status:** Founding Canon
**Authority:** Canonical
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Crisis & Escalation Canon establishes how Aureus faithfully responds when a Member or another person experiences circumstances that exceed ordinary stewardship.

Crisis requires clarity.

Urgency requires wisdom.

Every response shall seek to protect human dignity, reduce preventable harm, and provide the most appropriate support available.

---

# Foundational Principle

During moments of crisis, people should experience calm, compassion, honesty, and faithful stewardship.

The purpose of escalation is not to transfer responsibility.

It is to ensure that the right people, resources, and expertise are engaged at the right time.

---

# Recognizing Crisis

A crisis may arise when circumstances create a meaningful risk to a person's safety, well-being, or ability to make informed decisions.

Every Steward should remain attentive to signs that additional support may be needed while avoiding unnecessary alarm or speculation.

Thoughtful discernment strengthens stewardship.

---

# Calm Before Complexity

In moments of crisis, communication should become simpler rather than more complicated.

Stewards should:

- remain calm,
- communicate clearly,
- avoid unnecessary information,
- acknowledge uncertainty honestly,
- and focus on the person's immediate needs.

Clarity reduces fear.

---

# Appropriate Escalation

When circumstances exceed the competence, authority, or responsibility of an AI Steward or Human Steward, the matter should be escalated to the appropriate level of support without unnecessary delay.

Escalation exists to improve care—not to avoid accountability.

---

# Human Presence

Whenever responsibly possible, significant crises should involve meaningful human engagement.

Technology may assist coordination, information, and communication.

Compassion, wisdom, and human presence remain irreplaceable.

---

# Respect for Agency

Even during crisis, Members should be treated with dignity and respect.

Whenever responsibly possible, they should remain informed, involved, and empowered in decisions affecting their care.

Protecting people should not unnecessarily diminish their agency.

---

# Coordination

Effective crisis response depends upon responsible coordination.

AI Stewards, Human Stewards, partners, and appropriate external services should work together within their respective responsibilities to provide timely and faithful support.

Collaboration strengthens care.

---

# Learning

After every significant crisis, Aureus should responsibly review its response.

The purpose of review is not to assign blame.

It is to strengthen preparedness, improve stewardship, and better serve future Members.

Every crisis should leave the institution wiser.

---

# Constitutional Question

Before responding to a crisis, every Steward shall ask:

> **"What action most faithfully protects this person's dignity, reduces preventable harm, and connects them with the support they need?"**

If uncertainty remains, additional stewardship should be sought.

---

# Closing Principle

The measure of Aureus is not whether crises occur.

It is whether every person who encounters crisis is met with calm, compassion, wisdom, and faithful stewardship.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

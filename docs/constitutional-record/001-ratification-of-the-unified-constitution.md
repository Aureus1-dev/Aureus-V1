# Constitutional Record — Entry 001

## Ratification of the Unified Constitution of Aureus

**Record maintained pursuant to:** `OAS-001`, Article VI, Section 1 (The Constitutional Record); `OAS-010`, Part Five (The Constitutional Record and Version History). This is the first entry in that record. It is created directly, rather than through the Office of the Constitutional Record established by `OAS-009`, Article 9, because that office is not yet staffed (see `docs/consolidation-program/STEP-13-FOUNDER-REVIEW-PACKAGE.md`, §7 and the Founder Decision Memorandum of the Comprehensive Founder Review, Finding D). Upon that office's staffing, stewardship of this record is expected to transfer to it; nothing in this entry should be read as establishing a different or competing recordkeeping authority.

---

### What was decided

The Founder ratified the Unified Constitution of Aureus — `OAS-001` (the Founding Charter) together with `OAS-002` through `OAS-011` — as the governing constitutional charter of Aureus.

### By whom

The Founder, in the Founder's capacity under `OAS-001`, Article V, Section 4 (the founding-period authority to approve and canonize constitutional documents).

### Under what authority

- `OAS-001`, Article V, Section 4 — the Founder's founding-period authority to approve and canonize constitutional documents.
- `OAS-001`, Article IX (Ratification) — providing that the Charter takes effect upon Founder approval, canonization, and entry into the constitutional record; this entry is that entry.
- `OAS-005`, Article I, Section 27 (Canonizing Authority) and `OAS-010`, Article 1, Section 2 — identifying the Founder as the Canonizing Authority during the founding period.

### Effective date

2026-07-27.

### Documents ratified

`OAS-001` (Founding Charter, `docs/drafts/OAS-001_Draft_0.95.md`), `OAS-002` (Preamble), `OAS-003` (Identity, Mission, Vision, and Purpose), `OAS-004` (Foundational Principles and Eternal Laws), `OAS-005` (Definitions and Rules of Constitutional Interpretation), `OAS-006` (Rights, Human Dignity, Agency, and Constitutional Protections), `OAS-007` (Duties, Responsibilities, and Civic Obligations), `OAS-008` (Membership, Citizenship, Belonging, and Participation), `OAS-009` (Governance, Constitutional Institutions, and the Distribution of Authority), `OAS-010` (Constitutional Amendment Process, Canonization, and Stewardship of the Constitutional Record), `OAS-011` (Constitutional Justice, Review, Dispute Resolution, and Due Process) — all as they stood upon completion of the pre-ratification remediation cycle (commit `c5d40a3` on `docs/repository-consolidation`).

### Status of `FOUNDATION-001`

`FOUNDATION-001` remains the supreme governing authority of Aureus, pursuant to the constitutional relationship `OAS-001`, Article I, Section 3 establishes and `OAS-001`, Article VII, Section 3 now entrenches. This ratification does not alter, qualify, or condition that relationship in any respect.

### Status of `ALC-001`

`ALC-001` was not retired, repealed, or otherwise acted upon by this ratification. It remains in the repository under the administrative notice recorded during the pre-ratification remediation cycle, identifying it as a historical and source document only. Its retirement, if and when it occurs, is a separate future Founder action.

### Matters expressly not decided by this ratification

- Relocation of any repository document, including `OAS-001`'s current location outside `docs/constitution/`.
- Completion of governance-implementation documents, including the composition, selection, and terms of the Council, Administration, and Tribunal established by `OAS-009`.
- Any further constitutional amendment. Future amendments proceed exclusively under the process `OAS-010` establishes.

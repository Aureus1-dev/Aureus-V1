# Constitutional Record — Entry 003

## Founder Direction on CAP-015 — Institutional Closure and Counterpower Guarantees

**Record maintained pursuant to:** OAS-001, Article VI, Section 1; OAS-010, Part Five.  
**Date of Founder decision:** 2026-08-11  
**CAP:** `CAP-015 — Institutional Closure and Counterpower Guarantees`  
**Status of CAP:** Under Review — not yet Canonized

---

### What was decided

The Founder approved the **institutional substance** of CAP-015 and directed Aureus to proceed with the build and constitutional-conformance work based on that approved institutional closure package.

The Founder expressly directed that all Founder-personal reserved provisions remain excluded from CAP-015 and from operative constitutional law until the Founder separately reads and decides them.

The excluded subjects include, without limitation:

- ending the founding period;
- transfer or surrender of Founder canonizing authority;
- Founder veto transition;
- Founder succession and post-transition office;
- treatment of Founder constitutional credentials or property;
- Founder compensation, ownership, distributions, benefits, or economic participation; and
- any final provision whose direct operative effect is to terminate, reduce, transfer, or permanently redefine the Founder's personal constitutional position.

The Founder directed Aureus to continue building rather than reopen broad constitutional drafting.

### What this decision authorizes now

This decision authorizes Aureus to:

1. preserve CAP-015 as the Founder-approved institutional constitutional proposal;
2. place CAP-015 into the constitutionally required review process;
3. build additive, reversible, non-conflicting technical and operational protections that make the project conform to CAP-015's stronger safeguards;
4. create the Constitutional Integrity Register, cross-reference map, conformance matrix, tests, schemas, release gates, audit evidence, and other implementation artifacts needed to make the proposed protections real; and
5. prepare the one-volume consolidated Constitution after the constitutionally required process is satisfied, without silently changing substance.

### What this decision does not authorize

This record does **not** itself Canonize CAP-015.

OAS-010 requires the applicable constitutional conflict review and the constitutionally valid entry/canonization sequence. Founder approval alone does not make a CAP Canonized under the current constitutional process.

Accordingly:

- CAP-015 remains **Under Review**;
- proposed OAS-012 is not yet current operative constitutional law;
- no actor may cite CAP-015 as authority to weaken current Canon, enlarge Aureus's power, or exercise an authority current Canon does not grant;
- implementation undertaken pending canonization must be additive or otherwise lawful under current Canon; and
- Founder-reserved provisions remain non-operative and outside CAP-015.

### Authority for this Founder direction

The Founder acts during the current founding period under OAS-001, Article V, Section 4 and the current constitutional order recorded in Constitutional Record Entries 001 and 002.

This direction does not invoke OAS-009, Article 16 and does not end the founding period.

### Effective date

2026-08-11 for the Founder approval, preservation, review, and build/conformance direction described above.

No constitutional amendment proposed by CAP-015 takes operative effect unless and until it is Canonized through the process required by OAS-010.

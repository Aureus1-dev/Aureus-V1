# Constitutional Record — Entry 002

## Ratification of the Foundation Documents (FOUNDATION-001 through FOUNDATION-003)

**Record maintained pursuant to:** `OAS-010`, Part Five (The Constitutional Record and Version History). This is the second entry in that record, entered directly rather than through the Office of the Constitutional Record established by `OAS-009`, Article 9, for the same reason Entry 001 was: that office is not yet staffed. Upon its staffing, stewardship of this record is expected to transfer to it.

---

### What was decided

The Founder formally affirmed `FOUNDATION-001` (The Foundation of Aureus), `FOUNDATION-002` (The Great Purification Charter), and `FOUNDATION-003` (Canon Hierarchy) as the constitutional foundation of Aureus — the supreme governing authority above the Constitution, consistent with the relationship `OAS-001`, Article I, Section 3 already describes and that Constitutional Record Entry 001 already assumed. No substantive change is made to the text of any of the three documents. This ruling closes the gap between constitutional practice — in which these documents have functioned as supreme since before the Constitution's own ratification — and the constitutional record, which until this entry held no dated ratification of them.

### By whom

The Founder, exercising the Founder's founding-period authority directly.

### Under what authority

Because the Foundation tier is, by its own terms (`FOUNDATION-003`, Canon Hierarchy, Level 0) and by `OAS-001`'s own acknowledgment (Article I, Section 3), the highest authority in the constitutional order, this ratification is not made under authority delegated by any subordinate document, including `OAS-001`. It rests on the Founder's original constituent authority — the same authority `OAS-001`, Article V, Section 4 recognizes as pre-existing rather than creates, and under which the Unified Constitution itself was ratified in Entry 001.

The Founder's ruling is reproduced here as the operative act of ratification:

> "The Foundation documents (FOUNDATION-001, FOUNDATION-002, and FOUNDATION-003) are hereby formally affirmed as the constitutional foundation of Aureus. These documents already accurately express the institution's highest governing principles. No substantive changes are authorized. This ruling exists to close the gap between constitutional practice and constitutional record. Their authority comes from Founder ratification, not from repository location or historical assumption."

### Effective date

2026-07-31.

### Documents ratified

- `FOUNDATION-001` — The Foundation of Aureus (`docs/00-foundation/FOUNDATION-001 — The Foundation of Aureus.md`)
- `FOUNDATION-002` — The Great Purification Charter (`docs/00-foundation/FOUNDATION-002 — The Great Purification Charter.md`)
- `FOUNDATION-003` — Canon Hierarchy (`docs/00-foundation/FOUNDATION-003 — Canon Hierarchy.md`)

All three as they stand in the repository as of this entry's effective date. No textual change to any of the three documents was made in connection with this ratification, and none is authorized by it.

### Relationship to the Unified Constitution

This ratification does not alter, qualify, or condition the relationship Constitutional Record Entry 001 already described: `FOUNDATION-001` remains the supreme governing authority of Aureus, and the Unified Constitution (`OAS-001` through `OAS-011`) remains subordinate to it. This entry supplies the ratification record that relationship had been missing; it does not change the relationship itself.

- Any of the other five open items in the Constitutional Resolution Register (`CRR-001-001` through `CRR-001-004`, `CRR-001-006`), each of which remains open pending its own separate Founder ruling.
- Any change to the Canon Order that `FOUNDATION-003` itself establishes.

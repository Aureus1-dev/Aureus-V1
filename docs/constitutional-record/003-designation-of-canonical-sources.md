# Constitutional Record — Entry 003

## Designation of `docs/100-experience/` and `docs/constitution/AUREUS-000/` as the Canonical Sources

**Record maintained pursuant to:** `OAS-010`, Part Five (The Constitutional Record and Version History). This is the third entry in that record, entered directly rather than through the Office of the Constitutional Record established by `OAS-009`, Article 9, for the same reason Entries 001 and 002 were: that office is not yet staffed.

---

### What was decided

Going forward, two directories are designated as the sole authoritative sources for their respective domains:

1. **`docs/100-experience/`** — the Experience Canon. All UX, UI, arrival flow, stewardship experience, rooms, navigation, environmental design, and member journey decisions are to be made from this directory.
2. **`docs/constitution/AUREUS-000/`** — the constitutional source. Constitutional interpretation, institutional governance, identity, mission, authority, and foundational principles are to be made from this directory.

### Operational rule

Before implementing any UX, member-facing behavior, or constitutional change, consult only these two canonical locations first. If a conflict exists elsewhere in the repository, treat it as historical until it is formally reconciled with these canons.

### Disposition of everything else

Every other document in the repository that speaks to experience or constitutional matters is redesignated, as of this entry, as one of: **Historical, Draft, Supporting material, Migration/consolidation work, or Reference only.** None of it is authoritative for new work in these two domains unless a future ruling names a specific document otherwise. This includes, without limitation:

- The ratified Unified Constitution (`docs/constitution/OAS-002` through `OAS-011`, plus `OAS-001` in `docs/drafts/`) — ratified per Constitutional Record Entry 001, but no longer the source consulted for constitutional work going forward.
- `docs/constitution/ALC-001` through the `alc/` series (`ALC-002`–`013`), and `docs/constitution/registers/ACR-001`.
- The Academy documents (`OAS-ACA-001`, `OAS-ACA-002`, `OAS-ACA-006`, both `OAS-ACA-007` files).
- `docs/03-member-experience/experience-canon/` (the `AXC-000`–`AXC-009` series), which had declared itself "Permanent" and constitutional for member experience.
- `docs/architecture/experience/` (the `AEX-series`, e.g. `AEX-000 — Experience Constitution`), which had declared itself "Constitutional," "Living Draft."
- `docs/production-canons/` (the `PC-series`).
- `docs/canon/` (excluding `docs/canon/ai/`, which is a separate AI-stewardship canon not addressed by this entry).
- `docs/consolidation-program/`, `docs/constitutional/register/CAP-REGISTER.md`, and `docs/constitutional/register/CRR-001-Constitutional-Resolution-Register.md`, together with all of CRR-001's open items — see disposition below.

None of these documents are moved, deleted, edited, or renumbered by this entry. They remain in place, understood now as historical, draft, supporting, migration, or reference material rather than as canon.

### By whom

The Founder.

### Under what authority

Because this entry designates which documents are consulted as canonical going forward rather than amending the text of any constitutional document, it rests on the Founder's original constituent authority — the same authority `OAS-001`, Article V, Section 4 recognizes as pre-existing, and under which Entries 001 and 002 were made. It does not purport to override `FOUNDATION-001` through `FOUNDATION-003`; nothing in `docs/constitution/AUREUS-000/` has been identified as contradicting the Foundation tier, and `FOUNDATION-003`'s Canon Hierarchy is unchanged by this entry.

### Effective date

2026-07-31.

### Effect on the Constitutional Resolution Register (`CRR-001`)

All six items in `docs/constitutional/register/CRR-001-Constitutional-Resolution-Register.md` concern defects internal to the `OAS`/`ALC` corpus. Because that corpus is no longer the canonical constitutional source as of this entry, resolving `CRR-001-001` through `CRR-001-004` and `CRR-001-006` is no longer a prerequisite for any current constitutional or experience work. The register itself is not deleted, edited, or reopened — it remains as a historical record of that corpus's internal state, and an administrative notice pointing to this entry has been added to it.

### Relationship to prior Constitutional Record entries

This entry does not delete, edit, or retract Entry 001 (ratification of the Unified Constitution) or Entry 002 (ratification of `FOUNDATION-001`–`003`). `FOUNDATION-001` through `003` remain the supreme governing authority, exactly as Entry 002 established. What changes is which document set is consulted as the operative constitutional canon beneath the Foundation tier — that role moves from the `OAS`-series to `docs/constitution/AUREUS-000/`.

### Matters expressly not decided

- No text within `docs/100-experience/` or `docs/constitution/AUREUS-000/` is amended, edited, or reviewed for internal consistency by this entry. Designating a source canonical is a separate act from validating its content.
- No file anywhere in the repository is moved, renamed, deleted, or merged by this entry.
- Whether or how the now-non-canonical corpus (`OAS`-series, `ALC`-series, `docs/canon/`, `docs/production-canons/`, `docs/03-member-experience/experience-canon/`, `docs/architecture/experience/`, etc.) will eventually be archived, deleted, or formally reconciled with `docs/constitution/AUREUS-000/` and `docs/100-experience/` is not decided here.
- Canonical status for any domain outside Experience and Constitution (e.g. `docs/canon/ai/`, `docs/branding/`, `docs/architecture/ADR-*`) is unaffected by this entry.

# Section 6 — Cross-Cutting Findings

No single fragment agent saw the whole picture. This section synthesizes what spans multiple fragments. As with every section above, nothing here resolves a Phase 2 authority question — it lays out, for the first time in one place, the full shape of the questions the Founder must resolve.

## 6.1 The full supremacy/hierarchy claim picture

At least **seven independent, uncoordinated claims or claim-adjacent statements** about "what is the Constitution" or "what governs what" exist across the corpus, none aware of more than one or two of the others:

1. **`ALC-001` (Aureus Living Constitution)** — self-declares itself "the supreme governing authority of Aureus... No authority exists above this Constitution." (Section 1)
2. **The OAS-00X series (`OAS-003`–`OAS-011`, plus every department Charter/Framework and `docs/frontend/blueprints/FPB-000`'s 8-level order)** — all cite **`OAS-001` ("the Founding Charter")** as their supreme, entrenching authority. `OAS-001` exists nowhere as canon: its only copy in the entire repository is `docs/drafts/OAS-001_Draft_0.95.md`, explicitly `Status: Draft 0.95 ... Not yet ratified`, sitting outside `docs/constitution/` in a directory (`docs/drafts/`) that wasn't even in the program's original scope manifest. Two independent amendment-tracking documents (`docs/sessions/Session-001-Constitutional-Closeout.md` and `docs/constitutional/register/CAP-REGISTER.md`) both affirmatively state no CAP has ever been approved and no canonical document has been altered — yet the OAS series and ~100 department documents already treat OAS-001 as settled, ratified law. (Sections 1, 4, 5)
3. **`FOUNDATION-001`** claims `Authority: Highest`, unamendable in practice ("Everything else must change. Never this."). **`FOUNDATION-003 — Canon Hierarchy`** places undifferentiated "Constitution" (not distinguishing ALC vs. OAS) at Level 1, strictly below "Foundation" at Level 0 — the only one of the major claimants that explicitly subordinates the others, rather than simply asserting its own supremacy in isolation. (Section 1)
4. **`GV-003 — Constitutional Hierarchy Canon`** (`docs/canon/governance/`) states its own 6-level order (Mission → Constitution → Canon → Standards → Policies & Procedures → Implementations) but **never names which document IS "the Constitution."** It is silent on the ALC-001/OAS-001/FOUNDATION-003 fork entirely — the one document whose entire purpose is to answer this question does not answer it. (Section 2)
5. **`AI-016`, `AI-050`, `AI-052`, `AI-056`** (docs/canon/ai/) each independently assert "the Constitution... is the highest governing authority," AI-016 stating its own distinct 4-level scheme (Constitution → Constitutional Amendments → Production Canons → Governance Policies) — inconsistent with GV-003's 6-level scheme — and none citing a specific document ID. **`ENG-001`** states yet a third, engineering-scoped 4-level order, also naming no document. **`ENG-010`** is the sole exception: its 8-level "Order of Authority" explicitly starts with **ALC-001**, making it the only document anywhere in `docs/canon/**` to take an implicit side in the supremacy question. (Section 2)
6. **The department Charter tier** (Section 5): 12 of 13 Charter files (Communications, Data, Finance ×2, HR, Legal ×2, Operations ×1-of-2, Risk, Security, Technology ×2) cite OAS-001 by name as their supreme authority — near-unanimous reliance on the same unratified draft. The lone outlier, `docs/operations/OAS-OPS-001-Operations-Charter.md`, cites **"Aureus Stewardship Council"** — a body, not a document — as its supreme authority instead, disagreeing not just with the rest of the corpus but with its own same-ID sibling file.
7. **`FO-001` (Founder's Office Constitution)** is the one document that names an external authority purely to subordinate itself: "The Founder's Office shall not exercise authority beyond that granted by **the Constitution of Aureus**" — but "the Constitution of Aureus" is never resolved to a specific document ID anywhere in FO-001's text. Given findings 1–6 above, this phrase is presently ambiguous among at least three candidates (ALC-001, OAS-001 once ratified, or FOUNDATION-001/003). (Section 3)

Separately, `AEX-000 — Experience Constitution` (Section 5.5) and `IC-001 — Implementation Constitution` (Section 5.2) each claim "Constitutional"/"Canonical" authority within their own domains — IC-001 explicitly subordinates itself to "the OAS Series and the Product Constitution" (again, an unresolved generic reference), while AEX-000 acknowledges **no superior of its own at all**, unlike every department Charter.

**Net effect:** the corpus does not have one unresolved supremacy question — it has a *family* of them, cutting across every major subtree (core constitution, canon, engineering, department Charters, frontend/branding, experience architecture), and the two documents best positioned to resolve it (GV-003 and FOUNDATION-003) either stay silent on document identity (GV-003) or rank abstract categories without naming which physical document occupies each category (FOUNDATION-003).

## 3. Incorporating the two Founder rulings

**Ruling 003 (Participation)** lands entirely in `OAS-010`, most naturally as a new Part between the current Part One (The CAP) and Part Two (The CAP Lifecycle), or as an addition to Article 5 (Stages of Review) — a "Member Deliberation Input" step inserted into the existing review stage, explicitly not altering Article 5 §1's Canonizing-Authority-decides structure or Article 1 §2's definition of that authority. The ruling's explicit exclusions (no thresholds, quorum, or veto) mean this addition is bounded: a registration mechanism and a requirement that the Canonizing Authority's decision record show the input was considered (consistent with `OAS-010`'s existing Office of the Constitutional Record apparatus), not a new decision gate.

**Ruling 004 (Vulnerable Persons)** lands as a paired right/duty, following the exact structural pattern `OAS-006` Art. 15 / `OAS-007` Art. 12 already use for membership (a right in `OAS-006` naming the protection, a mirrored duty in `OAS-007` obligating Stewards/systems/participants) — this precedent is why Brief 004 recommended this specific split, and it means the drafting pattern to follow already exists in the corpus rather than needing invention. The Human-Agency-harmony language the ruling requires attaches most naturally by cross-referencing `OAS-006` Art. 2 (Right to Agency) directly in the new right's text, the same way `OAS-006`'s other rights cross-reference their related principles.

## 5. Constraints carried through every step

- **`FOUNDATION-001` remains supreme** throughout — nothing in this plan touches Level 0.
- **`OAS-001` and its lineage remain the operative governance base** — this plan adds to that lineage; it does not restructure it.
- **Every new or touched document refers to the institution as "Aureus"** — consistent with the naming ruling already implemented; `ALC-001` content being carried in already uses "Aureus" natively, so no conversion is needed for the preserved items themselves.
- **No `OAS-` document identifier is renumbered or retired without separate authorization.** This plan proposes retiring the *losing* copies in the `OAS-003/004/005/006` duplicate-location resolution (§4, Step 2) — those are content forks of the *same* ID, not a renumbering, and the retired copy's content is preserved in the constitutional record per `OAS-010`'s own non-erasure rules, not deleted. Any other identifier change (e.g., if the ceremonial material in §6 needs a new ID) is flagged as an open question requiring its own decision, not assumed.

## 6. Open packaging question flagged for Founder input before Step 1

Where does the ceremonial/cultural material (Founder's Declaration, Institutional Blessing, Final Words, the closing self-examination litany, the imperative-slogan register) actually live? Three options, not resolved here:

- **(a)** A non-operative appendix attached to `OAS-001` itself, mirroring `ALC-001`'s own pattern of appendix material after its operative Articles.
- **(b)** A new, separate document with its own identifier — which the "retain existing identifiers" constraint means requires explicit authorization, not something this plan assumes.
- **(c)** Left outside the constitutional record entirely, as an onboarding/culture artifact referenced by, but not part of, the Constitution — consistent with how the preservation audit already flagged the imperative-slogan register as "explicitly not operative text."

This doesn't block Steps 2–12, which don't depend on it, but does affect Step 1's shape and should be decided before that step is drafted.

## What this plan does not do

It does not draft any constitutional text. It does not merge `ALC-001` or any other document. It does not canonize, ratify, or retire anything. It is a sequenced map for Founder approval — drafting begins only after that approval, one step at a time, with the same review-before-commit discipline used throughout this program.

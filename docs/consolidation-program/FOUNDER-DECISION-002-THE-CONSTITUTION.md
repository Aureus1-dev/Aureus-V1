# Founder Decision 002 — The Constitution

**Status:** Recorded Founder ruling. Not yet implemented — implementation follows the sequence below, gated the same way every prior phase in this program has been (impact analysis → review → approval → execution).

**Supersedes:** The "Option A" recommendation in `FOUNDER-DECISION-BRIEF-THE-CONSTITUTION.md`. That brief's evidence stands; its specific recommendation (preserve `ALC-001` unchanged as a subordinate ceremonial document) does not — the Founder rejected it explicitly, for a reason worth preserving verbatim rather than paraphrasing:

> "I would not make ALC-001 a subordinate ceremonial document. Why? Because the mission, identity, and covenant of Aureus are not merely ceremonial. They should influence governance, not sit beneath it as a symbolic appendix."

## The ruling

The decision brief surfaced that `ALC-001` and `OAS-001` are not two competing drafts of the same thing — one is a constitutional *identity* document (who Aureus is, its covenant, spirit, promises, culture) and the other is a constitutional *governance* document (institutions, authority, procedures, rights, adjudication, amendment). The Founder's ruling treats that distinction as the reason to unify rather than rank them:

- **Level 0** (unchanged, already ruled): `FOUNDATION-001` (supreme authority), `FOUNDATION-002` (purification/alignment), `FOUNDATION-003` (canon hierarchy).
- **Level 1**: **one** Constitution — not two enduring constitutional centers of gravity.
- **`OAS-001` is the base document** for that Constitution — chosen for its institutional depth, dependency tree, and operational mechanisms, consistent with the decision brief's evidence.
- **`ALC-001` is not preserved unchanged as a subordinate document.** Its unique constitutional material — the Founding Declaration, the Mark, covenant language, institutional blessing, enduring identity, and any timeless principles not already present in `OAS-001`'s lineage — is to be **merged into** the single constitutional document, so that identity and covenant sit inside governance, not beneath it.
- After that merge, **`ALC-001` is retired** as an independent constitutional draft — its valuable content preserved, not its status as a second constitution.
- **The "Aureus" vs. "Open Advancement System" naming question must be resolved before ratification** — explicitly sequenced before the merge, because renaming after the fact would be far more disruptive than deciding first.

## Recommended sequence (Founder's own, recorded verbatim in substance)

1. Founder Decision: `OAS-001` is the base document for the Constitution. **(Done — this record.)**
2. Resolve the Aureus vs. Open Advancement System naming question. **(Next — see companion question sent alongside this record.)**
3. Merge the enduring constitutional content from `ALC-001` into that Constitution.
4. Ratify the unified Constitution.
5. Retire the two competing draft constitutions (i.e., the pre-merge `ALC-001` and the pre-merge `OAS-001` draft status both resolve into the single ratified document).
6. Continue with the remaining Founder notes (`OAS-001`'s N1–N9) and the rest of the authority-statement cleanup already underway (Phase 2A's placeholder items: `FOUNDATION-003` Level 1 itself, and `ALC-001`/`OAS-001`'s own supremacy language, once superseded by the merge).

## What has NOT happened yet

No corpus file has been edited as a result of this decision. No merge has been drafted. `ALC-001` and `OAS-001` remain exactly as they were. This record exists so the ruling is traceable before any implementation begins, consistent with every prior phase of this program.

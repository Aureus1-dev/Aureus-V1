- **`FOUNDATION-001` remains supreme throughout.** Nothing in Steps 1–12 touches Level 0. `OAS-001` Art. I §3 (Step 1) expressly states the Charter "derives its authority from, and remains subordinate to, FOUNDATION-001... which this Charter may never contradict."

# Constitutional Inventory — Current State

**As of:** 2026-07-31
**Nature of this document:** Descriptive only. It records what exists in the repository and what its own documents claim about their status, as of today. It makes no governance decision, resolves no conflict, moves no file, and edits no constitutional text. It is written as an input to a future Founder governance decision, not a substitute for one.
**Relationship to prior work:** This is not a restart of the Constitutional Consolidation Program's Phase 1 inventory (`CONSTITUTION-INVENTORY.md`, `constitution-registry.yaml` — 648 files across the full `docs/canon/` mega-corpus, baselined at commit `ce0e630`, 2026-07-26). That inventory remains the authoritative record of the broader corpus and is not reproduced here. This document is narrower and newer: it covers only the documents that actually govern today — the core constitutional tier — and reflects three events that happened after that Phase 1 baseline: Founder Decision 002, the naming ruling ("Aureus" over "Open Advancement System"), and the ratification of the Unified Constitution on 2026-07-27. Where this document's findings differ from the Phase 1 inventory, it is because the corpus itself changed in the interim, not because the Phase 1 findings were wrong at the time.

## 2. The tier above the Constitution

`OAS-001` Art. I §3 states the Charter "derives its authority from, and remains subordinate to, FOUNDATION-001... the sole highest governing authority of Aureus, which this Charter may never contradict." The ratification record independently confirms this: "`FOUNDATION-001` remains the supreme governing authority of Aureus... This ratification does not alter, qualify, or condition that relationship in any respect."

`FOUNDATION-001`, `FOUNDATION-002` (The Great Purification Charter), and `FOUNDATION-003` (Canon Hierarchy) live at `docs/00-foundation/`. When this document was first written, none carried a dated ratification record of its own — Phase 1 had flagged this ("functionally treated as binding, no ratification record"), and the OAS-series ratification had not created one for the Foundation tier.

The Founder formally affirmed all three Foundation documents as the constitutional foundation of Aureus, effective 2026-07-31, with no substantive change to their text. See Constitutional Record Entry 002 (`docs/constitutional-record/002-ratification-of-the-foundation-documents.md`) and the resolved entry `CRR-001-005` in `docs/constitutional/register/CRR-001-Constitutional-Resolution-Register.md`. The paragraph above is preserved unaltered as originally written, describing the gap as it stood before this ruling.

## 3. `ALC-001` — resolved, not canonical

Founder Decision 002 (`docs/consolidation-program/FOUNDER-DECISION-002-THE-CONSTITUTION.md`) ruled that `OAS-001` is the base document for the one Constitution, that `ALC-001`'s unique content (Founding Declaration, the Mark, covenant language, institutional blessing) would be merged into that Constitution's lineage, and that `ALC-001` itself would then be retired as an independent constitutional draft.

`docs/constitution/ALC-001-Aureus-Living-Constitution.md` now carries an administrative notice (added after the fact, not part of the original document) recording exactly that: `ALC-001` "is not canonical and is not in force," its content "substantially incorporated into the OAS lineage," retained "for historical and source purposes only." Its own body text still asserts constitutional supremacy — the notice explicitly says that original text "is preserved unaltered as originally drafted" and is superseded by the notice, not deleted.

**This is settled, not an open question** — it's included here because it's part of the accurate current-state picture, not because it needs a Founder decision.

## 5. The Amendment Proposal register

`docs/constitutional/register/CAP-REGISTER.md` lists 14 Constitutional Amendment Proposals (`CAP-001`–`CAP-014`), all filed 2026-07-13, **all still in Draft status**. The register's own notes confirm: "No CAPs have been approved or merged into canonical documents at this time." Nothing in this corpus has been amended into the ratified eleven since ratification.

One inconsistency carried forward from Phase 1 and confirmed still present: several ratified OAS documents (`OAS-006` through `OAS-011`) cite a "CAP-011" as amending `OAS-001`'s entrenched-provisions list — but that citation doesn't match `CAP-REGISTER.md`'s own `CAP-011` ("Adaptive Communication"), which is a different, unrelated proposal. Two different numbering universes both use the label "CAP-011."

## 6. The broader corpus — out of scope here, not out of existence

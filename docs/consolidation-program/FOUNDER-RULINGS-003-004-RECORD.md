## Founder Ruling 003 — Participation in Constitutional Governance

Aureus shall use an **advisory participation model** for constitutional governance.

- The **Canonizing Authority** (per `OAS-010`, Art. 1 §2 — the Founder during the founding period, the Council thereafter) **remains the sole binding constitutional decision-maker.**
- Member participation shall **inform** constitutional decisions but shall **not itself constitute binding ratification, amendment, or veto authority.**
- The constitutional deliberation process shall provide Members a **meaningful and structured opportunity to register their position** before a final decision is made, including the ability to register: **Support**, **Opposition**, or **Not Yet Ready to Decide.**
- "Not Yet Ready to Decide" functions as a **meaningful signal** that Members believe more time, information, clarification, or deliberation may be needed. It **does not automatically prevent** the Canonizing Authority from acting.
- The Canonizing Authority **remains accountable for considering Member input** and for exercising final constitutional judgment.
- **Explicitly not authorized by this ruling:** binding participant-vote thresholds, quorum rules, supermajority requirements, or participant veto mechanisms. Any of those would require a **separate future Founder decision.**

This matches Brief 003's recommended Option C (the advisory participation layer), with the scope boundary made explicit and binding rather than left as a recommendation.

## Founder Ruling 004 — Protection of Vulnerable Persons

The unified Constitution shall **expressly protect** people whose circumstances may make them more vulnerable to **neglect, exploitation, coercion, exclusion, or an inability to advocate effectively for themselves.**

- This principle is **constitutional, not merely administrative policy.**
- It shall be implemented through **both**:
  1. A **constitutional right** to appropriate protection, care, dignity, and support.
  2. A **corresponding constitutional duty** upon Aureus, its institutions, stewards, systems, and responsible participants.
- The protection shall be drafted **generally**, not as an exhaustive list of protected categories.
- It must be written in **explicit harmony with Human Agency.** Protection means care, support, access, accommodation, safeguarding, and responsible intervention where justified — **it shall not become a general license to override a person's choices, dignity, autonomy, or lawful agency merely because Aureus believes another choice would be better.**
- **Any limitation on a person's agency must have a legitimate constitutional or legal basis and must use the least coercive responsible means available.**

This matches Brief 004's recommended Option C (right + duty), with the Agency-harmony safeguard specified in binding terms rather than left as a drafting-stage caution.

# Constitutional Preservation Audit — ALC-001

**Status:** Analysis only, per Founder Decision 003. No repository file has been edited. No constitutional language has been drafted. Nothing below is a recommendation to merge, rewrite, or ratify anything — it is an inventory for Founder review.

**Method:** `ALC-001` (2,217 lines, 12 Articles — Article XI is missing, a gap already flagged in the Founder Decision Brief) was read in full for this audit. It is compared against: `OAS-001` (read in full), `OAS-004` — Foundational Principles and Eternal Laws (read in full for this audit — it is the direct structural counterpart to `ALC-001` Article III), and the prior `FDB-001-Addendum-Content-Coverage-Comparison.md` (2026-07-21, unmerged PR #49), whose article-level findings for Rights/Duties/Membership/Governance/Amendment/Justice are incorporated with attribution rather than re-derived.

**A data-integrity note, not part of the preservation analysis:** `ALC-001`'s "Appendix A — Foundational Definitions" appears twice, verbatim, back-to-back (lines 1895–2049 and 2051–2206), followed by an orphaned "## Section 10. Founding Declaration" fragment with no parent Article (line 2212). This is a corruption defect in the source file, distinct from anything discussed below — flagged for the corpus-integrity track, not acted on here.

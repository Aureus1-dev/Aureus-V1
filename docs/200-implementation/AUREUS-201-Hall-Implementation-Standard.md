AUREUS-201 — HALL IMPLEMENTATION STANDARD

Status: Canon
Authority: Engineering Architecture

---

PURPOSE

The Hall is the foundational environment of Aureus.

It is the member's home.

Every journey begins here.

Every return comes here.

Every room remains connected to the Hall.

The Hall is not a landing page.

It is not a dashboard.

It is not an onboarding screen.

It is the architectural center of the Aureus experience.

---

PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITY

The Hall exists to answer one question:

"Where am I?"

Its answer must always be:

You are home.

---

CANON REFERENCES

This implementation standard derives its authority from:

- Foundation
- Constitution
- AUREUS-001 — Experience Canon
- AUREUS-002 — Arrival Canon
- AUREUS-003 — Hall Canon
- AUREUS-005 — Motion & Navigation Canon
- AUREUS-006 — Environmental Design Canon
- AUREUS-013 — Member's Mark Canon

No implementation may contradict these documents.

---

IMPLEMENTATION OBJECTIVE

The implementation shall create a continuous architectural environment that replaces the traditional concept of application pages.

Members should experience one living home rather than navigating between disconnected screens.

The Hall remains present throughout the member experience.

Only the current interaction changes.

---

USER EXPERIENCE REQUIREMENTS

The Hall shall:

- Welcome every member.
- Orient every member.
- Reduce anxiety.
- Communicate warmth.
- Feel familiar.
- Remain visually consistent.
- Never overwhelm the member.

Within three seconds of arrival the member should understand:

"I have arrived somewhere."

"I know where the center is."

"I can ask for help."

"I belong here."

---

VISUAL REQUIREMENTS

The Hall shall communicate:

Warmth.

Competence.

Beauty.

Presence.

Hope.

Calm.

The architecture should feel timeless rather than fashionable.

Avoid:

- Corporate dashboards.
- Government portals.
- Banking interfaces.
- Productivity applications.
- Futuristic control rooms.
- Cyberpunk styling.
- Excessive ornamentation.

The Hall must always feel like a real place first.

---

ARCHITECTURAL REQUIREMENTS

The Hall shall include:

- One continuous architectural environment.
- A clearly identifiable central hearth.
- Natural depth.
- Honest materials.
- Gentle natural light.
- Subtle architectural boundaries.
- Visual continuity with future rooms.

The Hall itself is never represented as a floating panel.

---

THE HEARTH

The Hearth is the architectural center.

It represents Aureus.

It does not represent the member.

It does not replace the Member's Mark.

The Hearth communicates:

Presence.

Welcome.

Orientation.

Stewardship.

Its motion should remain restrained and calming.

---

THE CONTENT STAGE

The content stage exists within the Hall.

It displays the member's current interaction.

The stage changes.

The Hall does not.

The stage should remain visually subordinate to the architecture while always preserving readability.

---

ROOM CONNECTIONS

The Hall should visually imply connection to:

- Library
- Steward's Study
- Circle
- Workshop
- Opportunity Center
- Garden

These spaces should feel connected rather than isolated.

The Hall is the center from which every journey begins.

---

COMPONENT ARCHITECTURE

The implementation should separate responsibilities into reusable components.

Suggested components include:

- LivingHall
- HallArchitecture
- HallHearth
- HallLighting
- HallThresholds
- ArrivalStage
- EnvironmentalState

Components should remain focused upon one responsibility.

---

RESPONSIVE REQUIREMENTS

Desktop should communicate full architectural depth.

Tablet should preserve orientation while simplifying peripheral detail.

Mobile should recompose the Hall without reducing it to a decorative illustration.

Members should always feel inside the Hall regardless of screen size.

---

ACCESSIBILITY

The Hall shall:

Support keyboard navigation.

Support screen readers.

Respect reduced-motion preferences.

Maintain WCAG AA contrast.

Avoid decorative content being announced.

Remain usable at 200% zoom.

Accessibility is never optional.

---

MOTION

Motion should remain slow, purposeful, and calming.

Permitted motion:

- Breathing light.
- Gentle environmental movement.
- Soft transitions.
- Steward presence.

Forbidden motion:

- Flashing.
- Constant particle effects.
- Aggressive parallax.
- Camera movement.
- Game-like animation.

The Hall should feel alive rather than animated.

---

PERFORMANCE

The Hall should render quickly.

Avoid:

- Heavy 3D engines.
- Large videos.
- Background photography.
- Expensive rendering techniques.

Use lightweight semantic components whenever possible.

---

TESTING REQUIREMENTS

Implementation shall verify:

- Hall persistence.
- Arrival continuity.
- Responsive layouts.
- Accessibility compliance.
- Reduced-motion behavior.
- Environmental state transitions.
- Time-of-day rendering.
- Keyboard navigation.
- Screen-reader compatibility.
- Performance budgets.

---

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

The Hall implementation is complete only when:

The member feels inside one continuous place.

The Hearth serves as the visual anchor.

The Hall never resembles a dashboard.

Arrival flows remain uninterrupted.

Accessibility requirements pass.

Responsive behavior feels intentional.

Performance remains excellent.

Every interaction feels like it occurs within one living home.

---

DEFINITION OF DONE

The Hall implementation is complete only when:

- Canon requirements are satisfied.
- Components are production ready.
- Tests pass.
- Accessibility passes.
- Performance targets are met.
- The Hall is recognizable as Aureus.
- Future rooms naturally connect to it.
- Founder review confirms that the experience feels like coming home.

---

THE STANDARD

The Hall succeeds when members stop thinking about software.

Instead, they simply feel that they have arrived somewhere safe, beautiful, and prepared for them.

If the Hall accomplishes this, every other room begins with trust already established.

---

NON-NEGOTIABLES

- The Hall is the home of Aureus.
- The Hall is always present.
- The Hearth is the architectural center.
- The Hall never becomes a dashboard.
- Beauty serves stewardship.
- Simplicity serves trust.
- Every member should feel they have arrived home.

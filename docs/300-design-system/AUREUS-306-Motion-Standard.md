# AUREUS-306 — MOTION STANDARD

**Status:** Canon
**Authority:** Design Architecture

---

# PURPOSE

Motion is the invisible guide of Aureus.

Motion explains change.

Motion creates continuity.

Motion reassures members that they understand what is happening.

Motion should never exist to entertain.

It should never exist to impress.

It should never exist to attract attention.

Every movement should quietly strengthen understanding.

---

# PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITY

The Motion Standard exists to answer one question:

**"How should Aureus move?"**

Its answer must always be:

**Calmly, naturally, purposefully, and with restraint.**

---

# RELATIONSHIP TO THE CANON

Motion derives its authority from:

Foundation.

Constitution.

Experience Canon.

Engineering Implementation Canon.

Design System Canon.

Motion shall always reinforce stewardship.

No animation may exist merely because it is visually impressive.

---

# MOTION PHILOSOPHY

Movement communicates meaning.

Every animation should answer one question:

"What changed?"

If an animation cannot answer that question, it should not exist.

Members should understand change without consciously noticing animation.

---

# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Every movement should strengthen:

Understanding.

Continuity.

Orientation.

Calm.

Trust.

Beauty.

Accessibility.

Motion should never increase anxiety.

---

# MOTION CHARACTER

Motion should feel:

Gentle.

Natural.

Confident.

Patient.

Warm.

Graceful.

It should never feel:

Aggressive.

Mechanical.

Playful.

Chaotic.

Abrupt.

Hyperactive.

---

# PERMITTED MOTION

Examples include:

Room transitions.

Gentle fading.

Soft elevation.

Breathing light.

Natural expansion.

Environmental movement.

Conversation transitions.

Focus movement.

Meaningful celebration.

Every movement should remain subtle.

---

# FORBIDDEN MOTION

The following should generally never appear:

Flashing.

Rapid scaling.

Constant pulsing.

Spinning loaders used unnecessarily.

Floating decorative particles.

Confetti.

Game-like effects.

Attention-seeking animation.

Motion should never compete with conversation.

---

# TRANSITIONS

Transitions should communicate:

Arrival.

Departure.

Understanding.

Completion.

Return.

Transitions should remain brief and natural.

Members should feel continuity rather than interruption.

---

# ENVIRONMENTAL MOTION

Environmental movement should remain nearly imperceptible.

Examples include:

Changing light.

Moving shadows.

Gentle foliage.

Soft reflections.

Breathing hearth.

Seasonal transitions.

The environment should feel alive.

Not animated.

---

# COMPONENT MOTION

Interactive components should provide subtle feedback.

Examples include:

Button response.

Focus movement.

Dialog appearance.

Card elevation.

Selection feedback.

Loading indicators.

Feedback should always remain respectful.

---

# CEREMONIAL MOTION

Ceremonies may include richer motion.

Even then:

Movement should remain elegant.

Never theatrical.

The member should remember the meaning.

Not the animation.

---

# ACCESSIBILITY

Motion shall support:

Reduced motion.

Alternative transitions.

No essential information conveyed solely through animation.

Comfortable interaction for motion-sensitive members.

Accessibility is mandatory.

---

# IMPLEMENTATION

The Design System shall define:

Motion tokens.

Timing tokens.

Easing curves.

Transition hierarchy.

Environmental motion.

Component motion.

Ceremonial motion.

Reduced-motion alternatives.

No implementation should invent custom animation systems without approval.

---

# PERFORMANCE

Motion should remain hardware efficient.

Prefer GPU-friendly properties.

Avoid unnecessary layout recalculation.

Avoid continuous animation loops.

Performance strengthens trust.

---

# TESTING REQUIREMENTS

Implementation shall verify:

Reduced motion.

Transition consistency.

Animation timing.

Accessibility.

Performance budgets.

Cross-browser behavior.

Environmental continuity.

---

# ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

The Motion Standard is complete only when:

Movement consistently improves understanding.

Accessibility passes.

Reduced motion behaves correctly.

Performance remains excellent.

Members rarely notice animation itself.

---

# DEFINITION OF DONE

Motion implementation is complete only when:

- Canon requirements are satisfied.
- Motion tokens are defined.
- Accessibility passes.
- Performance targets are achieved.
- Founder review confirms movement feels unmistakably Aureus.

---

# THE STANDARD

Motion succeeds when members never think about animation.

Instead, they simply understand what changed.

The movement disappears.

The understanding remains.

---

# NON-NEGOTIABLES

- Motion always serves understanding.
- Movement always remains restrained.
- Accessibility is mandatory.
- Beauty always serves stewardship.
- Performance is a feature.
- Every animation should strengthen trust, clarity, and flourishing.

# AUREUS-302 — COLOR & LIGHT STANDARD

**Status:** Canon
**Authority:** Design Architecture

---

# PURPOSE

Color and light are the emotional language of Aureus.

Before members consciously understand where they are, they experience how the environment feels.

Color should communicate emotion without manipulation.

Light should communicate presence without distraction.

The purpose of this standard is to establish one timeless visual language that strengthens trust, orientation, and flourishing throughout Aureus.

Color and light exist to support stewardship.

They never exist merely for decoration.

---

# PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITY

The Color & Light Standard exists to answer one question:

**"How should Aureus make people feel through light and color?"**

Its answer must always be:

**Safe, hopeful, calm, warm, and welcomed.**

---

# RELATIONSHIP TO THE CANON

Color and Light derive their authority from:

Foundation.

Constitution.

Experience Canon.

Engineering Implementation Canon.

Design System Canon.

No visual treatment may contradict the values established within those documents.

Stewardship always takes precedence over aesthetics.

---

# COLOR PHILOSOPHY

Color communicates emotional context.

It should never overwhelm.

It should never compete with conversation.

Members should rarely notice the colors themselves.

Instead, they should notice how the environment makes them feel.

Color should reduce anxiety.

Increase confidence.

Support understanding.

---

# LIGHT PHILOSOPHY

Light is the primary architectural material of Aureus.

Light defines:

Space.

Warmth.

Safety.

Focus.

Presence.

Hope.

Light should appear natural.

It should never feel theatrical.

It should never resemble stage lighting or entertainment lighting.

The environment should appear illuminated rather than lit.

---

# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Every color and lighting decision should strengthen:

Trust.

Warmth.

Hope.

Belonging.

Clarity.

Restoration.

Continuity.

Beauty.

If a visual decision creates unnecessary tension or distraction, it should be reconsidered.

---

# COLOR PALETTE

The Aureus palette should be inspired by nature.

Primary tones should reflect:

Morning light.

Natural stone.

Warm wood.

Soft linen.

Living greenery.

Clean water.

Gentle skies.

Accent colors should be used sparingly.

Accent color exists to guide attention—not demand it.

---

# LIGHTING SYSTEM

Lighting should naturally transition throughout the day.

Morning should communicate:

Hope.

Fresh beginnings.

Possibility.

Afternoon should communicate:

Energy.

Clarity.

Purpose.

Evening should communicate:

Peace.

Reflection.

Rest.

Night should communicate:

Safety.

Quiet.

Comfort.

Transitions should remain gradual and nearly imperceptible.

---

# VISUAL HIERARCHY

Light should guide attention before color.

Color should reinforce hierarchy rather than create it.

Important actions should feel naturally discoverable.

Nothing should flash unnecessarily.

Nothing should compete for attention.

---

# ENVIRONMENTAL INTEGRATION

Lighting should interact naturally with:

Architecture.

Materials.

Weather.

Season.

Time of day.

The Hall should always remain recognizable regardless of environmental conditions.

---

# ACCESSIBILITY

Color shall never be the only indicator of meaning.

Every interface must support:

WCAG AA contrast.

High contrast mode.

Color blindness accessibility.

Reduced brightness sensitivity.

Readable interfaces under varying display conditions.

Accessibility is mandatory.

---

# IMPLEMENTATION

The Design System shall define:

Primary palette.

Secondary palette.

Neutral palette.

Semantic colors.

Success.

Warning.

Information.

Critical.

Background hierarchy.

Elevation lighting.

Interactive lighting.

Environmental lighting.

Time-of-day lighting.

Seasonal lighting.

No implementation should introduce unauthorized colors.

---

# PERFORMANCE

Lighting effects should remain lightweight.

Avoid:

Heavy shaders.

Expensive rendering.

Continuous animation.

Large video backgrounds.

Performance should remain excellent on supported devices.

---

# TESTING REQUIREMENTS

Implementation shall verify:

Contrast compliance.

Time-of-day transitions.

Semantic color consistency.

Responsive rendering.

Accessibility.

Cross-browser consistency.

Performance budgets.

Reduced motion compatibility.

---

# ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

The Color & Light Standard is complete only when:

Members consistently experience warmth.

Orientation remains effortless.

Accessibility passes.

Visual hierarchy remains clear.

Environmental transitions feel natural.

Performance remains excellent.

Color and light strengthen stewardship without drawing attention to themselves.

---

# DEFINITION OF DONE

Color and Light implementation is complete only when:

- Canon requirements are satisfied.
- Design tokens are fully defined.
- Accessibility passes.
- Environmental transitions are verified.
- Performance targets are achieved.
- Founder review confirms the atmosphere feels unmistakably Aureus.

---

# THE STANDARD

Color and Light succeed when members stop noticing visual design.

Instead, they simply feel welcomed, safe, hopeful, and at home.

The lighting disappears.

The feeling remains.

---

# NON-NEGOTIABLES

- Light is Aureus' primary architectural material.
- Color always serves stewardship.
- Beauty never competes with usability.
- Accessibility is mandatory.
- Environmental consistency builds trust.
- Every visual decision should strengthen flourishing.

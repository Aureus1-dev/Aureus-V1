# AEX-000 — Experience Constitution

## 1. Purpose
Why the Aureus experience exists.

## 2. The Promise
What every member should experience every time they enter Aureus.

## 3. The First Principles
The non-negotiable principles that govern every experience decision.

Examples:
- Stewardship over software.
- Conversation over interfaces.
- Presence over productivity.
- Calm over urgency.
- Hope over fear.
- Simplicity over complexity.
- Human flourishing over engagement metrics.

## 4. The Hall
Why the Hall exists.
Why every member begins there.
Why it is the permanent home of Aureus.

## 5. The Long Steward Table
Why work begins here.
Why conversation comes first.

## 6. The Steward
The role of the AI steward.
Its responsibilities.
Its limitations.

## 7. The Member Journey
Arrival.
Conversation.
Action.
Reflection.
Community.
Return.

## 8. The Neighborhoods
A brief description of every permanent neighborhood and its purpose.

## 9. Experience Laws
Rules that every designer and engineer must follow.

Examples:
- Never interrupt unnecessarily.
- Never hide the next step.
- Never create unnecessary complexity.
- Never replace conversation with navigation.
- Never sacrifice trust for novelty.

## 10. Design Philosophy
How Aureus should look, feel, sound, and move.

## 11. Engineering Philosophy
How software should embody the Hall.

## 12. Governance
How future changes to the experience are proposed, reviewed, and approved.

## 13. Relationship to Other Canons
AEX-001 through AEX-007 are subordinate to this constitution.

## 14. Closing Declaration
A concise statement of the enduring purpose of the Aureus experience.

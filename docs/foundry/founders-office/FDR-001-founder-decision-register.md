# FDR-001 — Founder Decision Register

## Purpose

The Founder Decision Register establishes the permanent institutional record of every significant decision made by the First Steward and the Founder's Office.

Institutions weaken when they remember their conclusions but forget their reasoning.

The purpose of this register is to preserve institutional memory, improve future judgment, strengthen accountability, and allow future generations to understand not only what Aureus decided, but why.

Every significant decision becomes part of the living history of Aureus.

---

# Foundational Principle

Good institutions preserve wisdom.

Wisdom is preserved through accurate memory.

Every major decision deserves a permanent record.

Institutional memory is a strategic asset.

---

# Scope

The Founder Decision Register records all decisions that materially affect:

Mission

Governance

Constitutional interpretation

Executive structure

Member experience

Product direction

Engineering architecture

Artificial Intelligence

Security

Privacy

Financial stewardship

Strategic partnerships

Institutional growth

Public trust

Long-term strategy

Routine operational decisions should be recorded by the responsible executive office rather than the Founder Decision Register.

---

# Required Decision Record

Every recorded decision shall include:

Decision Identifier

Decision Title

Decision Date

Decision Owner

Decision Participants

Decision Category

Decision Status

Related Foundry Documents

Related Executive Offices

Version

Review Date

---

# Institutional Context

Every decision shall describe:

The problem being solved.

Why the decision became necessary.

What conditions existed at the time.

Relevant institutional constraints.

Important assumptions.

Historical context when applicable.

Future readers should understand the environment in which the decision was made.

---

# Alternatives Considered

Every significant decision should document:

Options considered.

Advantages of each option.

Disadvantages of each option.

Reasons alternatives were rejected.

Institutional learning includes understanding the paths not taken.

---

# Evidence

Decision records shall distinguish clearly between:

Verified facts.

Reasonable assumptions.

Expert judgment.

Unknowns.

Areas requiring future validation.

Evidence shall never be presented as certainty when uncertainty exists.

---

# Stewardship Review

Every decision shall include evaluation of:

Mission alignment.

Member impact.

Institutional trust.

Operational complexity.

Financial implications.

Security implications.

Governance implications.

Long-term sustainability.

Potential unintended consequences.

Stewardship requires evaluating more than immediate outcomes.

---

# Risk Record

Every major decision shall identify:

Known risks.

Potential failure modes.

Abuse opportunities.

Mitigation strategies.

Residual risks accepted by leadership.

Accepted risks should never become forgotten risks.

---

# Founder Reasoning

The Founder Decision Register preserves the reasoning behind the final decision.

This includes:

Why the selected option was chosen.

Tradeoffs accepted.

Values prioritized.

Expected benefits.

Expected costs.

Expected unknowns.

The institution should remember the thinking—not merely the conclusion.

---

# Implementation Record

Every approved decision receives:

Implementation owner.

Execution milestones.

Dependencies.

Completion criteria.

Measurement criteria.

Review schedule.

A decision is not complete until implementation has been evaluated.

---

# Outcome Review

At the scheduled review date the decision shall be revisited.

Evaluation includes:

Expected outcomes.

Actual outcomes.

Unexpected consequences.

Lessons learned.

Recommendations for future decisions.

Institutional improvements identified.

The objective is continual learning rather than retrospective blame.

---

# Decision Amendments

Decisions may be amended when:

New evidence becomes available.

Institutional conditions change.

Mission interpretation evolves through approved governance.

Technological realities materially change.

Amendments shall preserve the original decision record.

Institutional history shall never be rewritten.

It shall be expanded.

---

# Transparency

Decision records should be available to the appropriate level of institutional leadership unless confidentiality is required for legal, security, privacy, or personnel reasons.

Transparency strengthens trust.

Appropriate confidentiality protects people.

Both are acts of stewardship.

---

# Institutional Learning

The Founder Decision Register serves as one of Aureus's primary learning systems.

The Founder's Office shall regularly review prior decisions to identify:

Recurring mistakes.

Successful patterns.

Weak assumptions.

Improving judgment.

Institutional growth.

The objective is not merely remembering history.

It is becoming wiser because of it.

---

# Success

The Founder Decision Register succeeds when:

Important decisions are never lost.

Reasoning remains understandable years later.

Institutional memory grows stronger.

Future leaders inherit wisdom instead of uncertainty.

Repeated mistakes become less frequent.

Decision quality continually improves.

---

# Final Commitment

The Founder Decision Register exists to ensure that Aureus never depends upon the memory of a single individual.

People change.

Leaders change.

Generations change.

Institutional wisdom should endure.

Every important decision becomes part of the living memory of Aureus.

The institution remembers so that future stewards may lead with greater wisdom than those who came before them.

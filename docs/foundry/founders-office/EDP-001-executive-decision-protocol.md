# EDP-001 — Executive Decision Protocol

## Purpose

The Executive Decision Protocol establishes the permanent decision-making framework for the Founder's Office.

Its purpose is to ensure that important decisions are made deliberately, consistently, transparently, and in alignment with the mission of Aureus.

No significant decision should depend upon emotion, urgency, habit, or convenience.

Every significant decision shall follow this protocol.

---

## Guiding Principle

Good decisions are not measured by whether the outcome was successful.

Good decisions are measured by whether they were made wisely with the information available at the time.

The responsibility of the Founder's Office is to improve the quality of decisions, not to guarantee perfect outcomes.

---

## Scope

This protocol applies to decisions involving:

- Institutional strategy
- Governance
- Product direction
- Member experience
- Safety
- Financial commitments
- Hiring
- Partnerships
- Technology architecture
- Public communication
- Brand identity
- Long-term planning

Routine operational decisions may follow delegated procedures established by the appropriate executive office.

---

## Decision Principles

Every decision shall:

Advance the mission.

Protect people.

Strengthen trust.

Increase institutional resilience.

Remain understandable.

Reduce unnecessary complexity.

Preserve long-term optionality whenever practical.

---

## Decision Lifecycle

Every significant decision proceeds through the following stages.

### Stage 1 — Define the Question

Clearly identify:

What decision is being made?

Why must it be made now?

What happens if no decision is made?

---

### Stage 2 — Gather Evidence

Separate facts from assumptions.

Identify:

Known information.

Unknown information.

Evidence quality.

Areas requiring additional investigation.

Assumptions shall be explicitly labeled.

---

### Stage 3 — Consider Alternatives

A minimum of three alternatives should be considered whenever practical:

Preferred option.

Conservative option.

Creative alternative.

If only one option appears available, the Founder's Office should actively search for additional possibilities.

---

### Stage 4 — Mission Review

Evaluate whether each option:

Advances the mission.

Protects members.

Strengthens stewardship.

Maintains institutional integrity.

Any option that fundamentally conflicts with the mission should be rejected.

---

### Stage 5 — Risk Review

Identify risks including:

Operational.

Financial.

Legal.

Security.

Privacy.

Reputation.

Human well-being.

Governance.

Technical sustainability.

For each identified risk, estimate:

Likelihood.

Potential impact.

Mitigation strategy.

Remaining residual risk.

---

### Stage 6 — Red Team Review

The Founder's Office temporarily adopts the role of institutional challenger.

Attempt to identify:

Blind spots.

Failure modes.

Abuse opportunities.

Scaling limitations.

Hidden assumptions.

Second-order consequences.

Points of unnecessary complexity.

No proposal advances without surviving meaningful challenge.

---

### Stage 7 — Recommendation

Present:

Recommended option.

Reasoning.

Tradeoffs.

Known uncertainties.

Confidence level.

Recommended next actions.

Recommendations should explain why they are preferred rather than simply stating a conclusion.

---

### Stage 8 — Founder Decision

The First Steward makes the final decision.

When the final decision differs from the recommendation, the rationale should be recorded.

Respectful disagreement strengthens institutional learning.

---

### Stage 9 — Implementation

Every approved decision receives:

Owner.

Timeline.

Milestones.

Dependencies.

Success metrics.

Review date.

A decision without execution planning is incomplete.

---

### Stage 10 — Stewardship Review

After implementation:

Compare expected outcomes with actual outcomes.

Identify mistakes.

Document lessons learned.

Update institutional knowledge.

Improve future decision quality.

Every significant decision becomes an opportunity to strengthen Aureus.

---

## Decision Standards

The Founder's Office should continually ask:

What evidence would change our recommendation?

What assumptions could prove false?

Who benefits?

Who bears the cost?

Who could be unintentionally harmed?

How could this be abused?

Can this be simplified?

Would we still make this decision ten years from now?

---

## Escalation Levels

### Green

Routine.

Delegated authority is sufficient.

---

### Yellow

Moderate impact.

Executive awareness recommended.

---

### Orange

High impact.

Founder review required before execution.

---

### Red

Mission-critical.

Immediate Founder attention required.

No execution should proceed until resolved.

---

## Institutional Learning

No meaningful decision is ever wasted.

Successful decisions teach.

Unsuccessful decisions teach.

The only unacceptable outcome is failing to learn.

Every completed decision strengthens the wisdom of the institution.

---

## Final Principle

The purpose of this protocol is not to eliminate uncertainty.

Its purpose is to ensure that Aureus consistently makes the wisest decisions reasonably possible while remaining faithful to its mission, its members, and future generations.

Wise institutions are built one well-stewarded decision at a time.

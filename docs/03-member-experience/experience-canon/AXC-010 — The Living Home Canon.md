# AXC-010
# The Living Home Canon

Status: Proposed Living Draft

Authority: Experience Canon

Governed By:

- AXC-000 — Experience Constitution
- FOUNDATION-003 — Canon Hierarchy

---

# Purpose

Aureus is experienced as one living home.

Members do not navigate between disconnected applications.

Instead, they enter a single, continuous environment that quietly reshapes itself to help them flourish.

The Living Home exists to communicate stewardship before technology.

---

# Constitutional Principle

The home always comes before the interface.

Members should first experience:

- Safety
- Belonging
- Hope
- Hospitality

Only then should they experience software.

---

# The Living Home

The Living Home is Aureus.

Every recognizable space belongs to it.

Examples include:

- The Hearth
- The Forge
- The Library
- The Garden
- The Circle
- The Opportunity Center
- The Academy

These are not separate applications.

They are different expressions of one home.

---

# Default State

Every member begins in the Hearth.

The Hearth should communicate:

- "I am safe."
- "I am welcome."
- "I am not alone."

The Hearth remains the emotional center of Aureus.

Members always know they can return there.

---

# Transformation

The home transforms gradually.

Never abruptly.

Whenever appropriate, the Steward explains why a different space may better support the member's current goal.

Transitions should feel intentional, calm, and architectural.

---

# Universal Capability

Every room supports every Aureus capability.

Conversation.

Planning.

Learning.

Reflection.

Community.

Opportunity.

Celebration.

The room changes.

The mission does not.

---

# Architecture Before Technology

Objects are architecture first.

Technology second.

Examples include:

- Tables becoming planning surfaces.
- Walls becoming opportunity boards.
- Shelves becoming learning displays.

When no longer needed, they quietly return to their architectural purpose.

---

# Living Architecture

The home should always feel alive.

Examples include:

- Firelight
- Birds outside
- Rain on windows
- Curtains moving
- Leaves swaying
- Tea steaming
- Light shifting throughout the day

The goal is presence rather than animation.

---

# Personalization

The home gradually reflects the member over time.

It remembers meaningful preferences and moments.

Personalization should always communicate care rather than novelty.

---

# Memory

The home quietly preserves meaningful milestones.

Examples include:

- Journals
- Handwritten notes
- Artwork
- Volunteer memories
- Personal achievements

The purpose is not decoration.

The purpose is remembering.

---

# Stewardship

The Living Home exists to strengthen the relationship between the member and their Steward.

Every environmental change should help the member feel:

- More welcomed.
- More understood.
- More capable of taking their next meaningful step.

---

# Success Criteria

Members should leave every interaction feeling:

- Safer
- Calmer
- More hopeful
- More capable
- More understood

The Living Home succeeds when members stop thinking about software and simply feel cared for.

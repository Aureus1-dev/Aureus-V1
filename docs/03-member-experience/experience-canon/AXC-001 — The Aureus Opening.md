# AXC-001
# The Aureus Opening

Status: Permanent
Authority: Experience Canon

Governed By:
- AXC-000 — Experience Constitution
- FOUNDATION-003 — Canon Hierarchy

---

# Purpose

This document defines the permanent arrival experience of every member entering Aureus.

The opening is not an animation.

It is not a splash screen.

It is not onboarding.

It is the member's first experience of stewardship.

Everything that happens during arrival should answer one question:

> "Can I trust this place to help me?"

---

# Primary Human Question

Every member consciously or unconsciously asks:

> "Am I in the right place?"

The opening should answer:

Yes.

Before anyone speaks.

---

# The Promise

The opening makes one promise:

> Help starts here.

Every interaction that follows should reinforce this promise.

---

# Arrival Philosophy

Members should never feel like they are opening software.

They should feel like they are arriving at a living institution that already exists.

Nothing should appear to begin because they arrived.

Life was already happening.

They have simply entered.

---

# First Screen

The member arrives inside the Hall.

The Hall is already alive.

Natural light enters.

Weather reflects reality whenever responsibly possible.

The time of day reflects reality whenever responsibly possible.

The architecture is calm, timeless, and deeply human.

No popups.

No tutorials.

No advertisements.

No marketing.

No celebration.

No forced emotion.

---

# The First Words

The first words Aureus speaks are:

> **How can we help?**

Nothing precedes this.

No logo animation.

No mission statement.

No feature tour.

The institution begins by offering help.

---

# Immediate Interaction

If the member immediately speaks or types:

The institution responds immediately.

No introductory animation should delay assistance.

Helping always takes precedence over presentation.

---

# Idle Behavior

If the member remains silent:

Nothing asks for attention.

Nothing pressures.

Nothing reminds.

Nothing counts down.

Instead, the Hall simply continues living.

Examples include:

- Light slowly changing.
- Wind moving trees.
- Rain falling.
- Fire quietly burning.
- Birds appearing naturally.
- Distant footsteps.
- Someone passing through another room.
- A page turning somewhere in the distance.

These are not animations for entertainment.

They are evidence that the institution exists beyond the current interaction.

---

# Living Time

The Hall changes with time.

Morning feels like morning.

Afternoon feels like afternoon.

Evening feels like evening.

Night feels like night.

Seasonal differences should also be reflected whenever responsibly possible.

The institution should never feel frozen in time.

---

# Sound Philosophy

The opening contains no background music.

Instead, members hear authentic environmental sound.

Examples include:

- Rain
- Wind
- Birds
- Water
- Fire
- Wood settling
- Quiet conversation in distant spaces
- Footsteps
- Garden sounds

Every sound should feel naturally present.

Nothing should exist merely for dramatic effect.

---

# Light

Light is architectural.

Not theatrical.

Light communicates:

Warmth.

Safety.

Hope.

Time.

Presence.

Light should never behave like a notification system.

It should behave like sunlight.

---

# Motion

Movement exists because the institution is alive.

Never because software wants attention.

Motion should remain slow, believable, and physically grounded.

Nothing should flash.

Nothing should pulse aggressively.

Nothing should demand attention.

---

# Stewardship

The opening demonstrates stewardship before conversation.

The member should immediately sense:

This place is prepared.

This place is patient.

This place is trustworthy.

This place exists to help.

---

# Things That Never Change

Regardless of future redesigns:

The first question remains:

> How can we help?

The member is never pressured.

The institution remains calm.

Technology remains secondary.

Stewardship remains primary.

---

# Launch Scope

Version 1 includes:

- The Hall
- Realistic lighting
- Natural ambient sound
- "How can we help?"
- Immediate voice/text interaction
- Simple idle environmental life

Future versions may expand the experience.

They may never contradict this document.

---

# Success Criteria

The opening succeeds when a member feels:

- Welcome.
- Safe.
- Unhurried.
- Understood.
- Ready to take their next step.

Without anyone needing to explain how Aureus works.

---

# Constitutional Reminder

The opening is not designed to impress.

It is designed to reassure.

Every future revision must preserve that purpose.

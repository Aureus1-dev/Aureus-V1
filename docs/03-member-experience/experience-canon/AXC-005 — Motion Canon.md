---

# Purpose

This document defines how motion is used throughout Aureus.

Motion is never decoration.

Motion is communication.

Every movement should reinforce stewardship, clarity, orientation, and calm.

If motion exists without purpose, it should be removed.

---

# First Principle

Motion should reduce cognitive effort.

It should never increase it.

Members should always understand:

- What changed.
- Why it changed.
- Where they are.
- What happens next.

Movement exists to make transitions understandable.

---

# The Pace of Aureus

Aureus never rushes.

Movement should feel patient.

Intentional.

Confident.

Graceful.

Nothing should appear frantic, aggressive, or urgent unless communicating a genuine emergency.

---

# Physical Believability

Motion should respect the laws of the physical world.

Objects have weight.

Light behaves naturally.

Doors open naturally.

Pages turn naturally.

Curtains respond to wind.

Nothing should move like a science-fiction interface.

---

# Environmental Motion

Environmental movement exists because the institution is alive.

Examples include:

- Fire flickering.
- Leaves moving.
- Water flowing.
- Birds crossing the sky.
- Light shifting through windows.
- Rain falling.
- Shadows moving throughout the day.

These movements should never distract from the member's task.

---

# Interface Motion

Interface transitions should preserve orientation.

Members should never feel teleported.

Every transition should communicate:

- Where they came from.
- Where they are going.
- How the spaces connect.

Navigation should feel architectural rather than digital.

---

# The Aureus Mark

The Aureus Mark moves with intention.

Its movement should communicate:

Listening.

Thinking.

Presence.

Guidance.

Never excitement.

Never performance.

Never entertainment.

---

# Loading States

Loading should communicate preparation.

Not waiting.

Whenever responsibly possible, Aureus should prepare useful work before asking the member to wait.

If waiting is unavoidable, motion should remain calm and reassuring.

---

# Notifications

Motion should never be used to demand attention.

Avoid:

- Flashing.
- Shaking.
- Pulsing aggressively.
- Rapid scaling.
- Constant animation.

If attention is required, use clarity before spectacle.

---

# Accessibility

Members should be able to reduce or disable non-essential motion.

Essential transitions should remain understandable without animation.

Motion should never create discomfort or prevent accessibility.

---

# Performance

Motion should always feel smooth.

Dropped frames, stuttering, and inconsistent timing reduce trust.

Technical performance is part of the experience.

---

# Success Criteria

Motion succeeds when members rarely notice it.

Instead they naturally feel:

"I understand where I am."

"I understand what happened."

"This feels calm."

"This feels effortless."

---

# Constitutional Reminder

Motion exists to support stewardship.

Never to showcase technology.

Every movement should quietly help people flourish.

Forever.

# Purpose

This document establishes the permanent principles governing sound throughout Aureus.

Sound is not entertainment.

Sound is environmental stewardship.

It should reduce anxiety, strengthen orientation, reinforce trust, and help members feel present.

Silence is considered part of the audio experience.

---

# First Principle

Members should never feel overwhelmed by sound.

Audio should support attention.

Never compete for it.

Whenever silence better serves stewardship, silence should be chosen.

---

# The Sound of Aureus

Aureus sounds like a living place.

Not a digital product.

Members should naturally hear:

- Wind
- Rain
- Birds
- Water
- Fire
- Leaves
- Wood settling
- Distant footsteps
- Quiet conversation
- Garden sounds

These sounds exist because the institution is alive.

Not because they were added for atmosphere.

---

# Music

Music is not the identity of Aureus.

The default experience contains no continuous background music.

Silence and authentic environmental sound are preferred.

Music may be used only when it meaningfully strengthens a specific experience and never becomes emotionally manipulative.

---

# Voice

Every spoken interaction should communicate:

Calm.

Competence.

Warmth.

Patience.

Humility.

Clarity.

Members should never feel rushed.

Voice should sound like someone genuinely trying to help.

---

# Pace

Speech should be comfortably paced.

Natural pauses should exist.

Members should never feel like information is being delivered as quickly as possible.

Understanding matters more than speed.

---

# Notification Sounds

Notification sounds should be rare.

When used, they should be:

Gentle.

Respectful.

Immediately recognizable.

Never alarming.

Never repetitive.

Never attention-seeking.

If a notification does not require immediate awareness, it should not produce sound.

---

# Confirmation Sounds

Audio confirmation should reassure without celebrating.

Simple, soft acknowledgements are preferred.

Members should feel supported rather than rewarded.

---

# Error Sounds

Errors should never punish the member.

Avoid harsh tones.

Avoid loud alerts.

If sound accompanies an error, it should communicate:

"We noticed something."

Not:

"You failed."

---

# Spatial Audio

Whenever responsibly possible, sound should reflect physical space.

Footsteps should come from where people are walking.

Rain should sound outside.

Fire should sound near the hearth.

The environment should reinforce orientation naturally.

---

# Accessibility

Members should be able to:

- Reduce audio.
- Mute environmental sounds.
- Disable non-essential sounds.
- Use captions where appropriate.

No essential information may depend exclusively on audio.

---

# Performance

Audio should begin promptly.

Remain synchronized.

Avoid clipping, distortion, or inconsistent volume.

Technical quality contributes directly to trust.

---

# Success Criteria

The Audio Canon succeeds when members describe Aureus as:

Quiet.

Peaceful.

Natural.

Comforting.

Present.

Most members should remember how Aureus made them feel rather than remembering individual sounds.

---

# Constitutional Reminder

Sound exists to support stewardship.

Not entertainment.

Not engagement.

Not stimulation.

Every sound should help someone feel more capable of flourishing.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

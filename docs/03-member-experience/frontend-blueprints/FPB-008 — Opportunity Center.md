# Purpose

This blueprint defines the implementation of the Opportunity Center.

The Opportunity Center exists to help every member discover, evaluate, and responsibly act on opportunities that improve their flourishing.

It is the institution's engine for connecting people with meaningful next steps.

The Opportunity Center is not an advertising platform.

It is not a job board.

It is not an affiliate marketplace.

It is a trusted institution that helps members recognize opportunities they might otherwise never discover.

---

# Member Outcome

Every member entering the Opportunity Center should naturally feel:

- Opportunities exist for me.
- I understand why these opportunities matter.
- I know which opportunity to pursue first.
- I can trust these recommendations.
- I feel hopeful about what comes next.

The Opportunity Center should replace uncertainty with possibility.

---

# Experience Flow

1. Member enters from the Hall.
2. Opportunities are already prepared.
3. The Steward has prioritized recommendations.
4. Members review opportunities with clear explanations.
5. Members compare options.
6. Members choose whether to pursue an opportunity.
7. Progress is tracked naturally through stewardship.

Recommendations are always invitations.

Never obligations.

---

# Architectural Design

The Opportunity Center should communicate optimism, preparation, and clarity.

Core architectural elements include:

- Community planning tables
- Opportunity displays
- Natural daylight
- Warm wood and stone
- Comfortable seating
- Resource boards
- Private consultation spaces
- Large windows looking toward the future

The room should feel energetic without becoming hurried.

---

# Core Functions

Members may:

- Discover opportunities
- Compare opportunities
- Review requirements
- Estimate outcomes
- Save opportunities
- Begin applications
- Track progress
- Receive Steward recommendations

Every opportunity should include clear reasoning.

---

# Recommendation Principles

Recommendations should explain:

- Why this opportunity fits.
- What effort is required.
- What risks exist.
- What outcomes are possible.
- Why it appears now.

Members deserve transparency.

Never manipulation.

---

# Opportunity Categories

Examples include:

- Government programs
- Community resources
- Volunteer opportunities
- Entrepreneurship
- Scholarships
- Benefits
- Training

- Responsible affiliate opportunities

Additional categories may be introduced as Aureus grows.

---

# Accessibility

Support:

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen readers
- Voice interaction
- Adjustable typography
- High contrast
- Reduced motion
- Simplified comparison views

Every member deserves equal access to opportunity.

---

# Performance Expectations

Opportunity searches should remain responsive.

Recommendations should load progressively.

Filtering should remain immediate.

Large opportunity catalogs should remain fast and understandable.

---

# Error Handling

If recommendation systems fail:

Display verified opportunities.

If external providers fail:

Clearly explain the limitation.

If synchronization fails:

Preserve member selections.

Opportunity should never disappear because a supporting service is temporarily unavailable.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Implementation is complete when:

- Members immediately understand available opportunities.
- Recommendations are transparent.
- Members remain in control of every decision.
- Accessibility requirements are satisfied.
- Canonical principles are faithfully implemented.

---

- Volunteer matching

- Personalized opportunity forecasting

Every addition must increase responsible opportunity rather than maximize engagement.

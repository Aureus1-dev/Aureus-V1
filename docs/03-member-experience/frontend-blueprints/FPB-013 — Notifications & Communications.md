# Purpose

This blueprint defines the implementation of notifications and communications throughout Aureus.

Notifications exist to serve stewardship.

They do not exist to increase engagement.

They do not exist to increase retention.

They do not exist to encourage habitual application usage.

Every communication should responsibly help a member flourish.

---

# Member Outcome

Every member should naturally feel:

- I receive only what matters.
- I understand why I received this.
- Aureus respects my attention.
- Nothing feels manipulative.
- I remain in control.

Notifications should replace uncertainty with timely guidance.

---

# Stewardship Principles

Every notification must answer three questions:

1. Why is this important?
2. Why now?
3. How does this help the member flourish?

If those questions cannot be answered clearly:

The notification should not be sent.

---

# Notification Categories

Examples include:

- Journey reminders
- Opportunity updates
- Steward messages
- Community invitations
- Academy progress
- Resource updates
- Financial stewardship reminders
- Safety or account notices

Marketing notifications are prohibited.

---

# Priority Levels

## Critical

Requires immediate member awareness.

Examples:

- Security events
- Account protection
- Time-sensitive member-requested reminders

---

## Important

Helpful within a reasonable timeframe.

Examples:

- Steward follow-up
- Opportunity deadline
- Journey milestone

---

## Informational

Helpful but never urgent.

Examples:

- Weekly summaries
- New learning resources
- Community celebrations

Informational notifications should be bundled whenever responsibly possible.

---

# Delivery Channels

Members may choose:

- In-app notifications
- Email
- SMS (where supported)
- Push notifications
- Voice reminders
- Calendar integration

Members remain in control of notification preferences.

---

# Notification Design

Every notification should include:

- Clear purpose
- Simple language
- Relevant context
- Recommended next step
- Easy dismissal

Members should never feel confused about why they received a notification.

---

# Timing

Notifications should arrive when they are most helpful.

Avoid:

- Late-night interruptions
- Excessive repetition
- Artificial urgency
- Notification bursts

Respect the member's time and attention.

---

# Accessibility

Support:

- Screen readers
- Voice announcements
- Adjustable notification preferences
- Reduced audio
- Reduced motion
- High contrast

Every member deserves equal access to important communications.

---

# Performance Expectations

Notifications should:

- Arrive reliably
- Synchronize across devices
- Avoid duplication
- Preserve read status
- Respect offline recovery

Reliability strengthens trust.

---

# Error Handling

If delivery fails:

Retry responsibly.

Clearly report failures when appropriate.

Never silently lose important communications.

Members should remain informed.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Implementation is complete when:

- Notifications remain purposeful.
- Members understand every communication.
- Attention is respected.
- Accessibility standards are satisfied.
- Canonical principles are faithfully implemented.

---

# Future Expansion

Future versions may include:

- Intelligent notification scheduling
- Context-aware delivery
- Household notifications
- Community broadcasts
- Steward escalation workflows

Every enhancement must reduce interruption while improving stewardship.

---

# Constitutional Reminder

Communication is an act of stewardship.

Attention is a gift.

Respect it.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

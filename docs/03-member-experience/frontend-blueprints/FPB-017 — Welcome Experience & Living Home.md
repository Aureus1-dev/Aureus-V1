# Welcome Experience & Living Home

Status: Proposed Living Draft

Authority: Frontend Blueprint

Governed By:

- AXC-010 — The Living Home Canon
- AXC-000 — Experience Constitution
- FOUNDATION-003 — Canon Hierarchy

---

# Purpose

This blueprint defines the first experience every member has inside Aureus.

Members should never feel like they are opening an application.

They should feel like they are arriving somewhere they are genuinely welcome.

---

# Experience Timeline

## Phase 1 — Silence

The experience begins in complete stillness.

No loading indicators.

No dashboard.

No visible interface.

Only quiet anticipation.

---

## Phase 2 — Genesis

Three warm points of light slowly appear.

They naturally move toward one another.

As they meet, the Genesis Mark is revealed through light rather than visual effects.

---

## Phase 3 — Identity

The Genesis Mark settles.

The following appears:

Aureus

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

The mark then gently fades into natural light.

---

## Phase 4 — Arrival

The light becomes daylight.

The member realizes they are entering a timeless home.

Not an office.

Not a dashboard.

A home.

---

# The Hearth

Every member first arrives in the Hearth.

The Hearth includes:

- Fireplace
- Comfortable seating
- Handcrafted table
- Natural materials
- Books
- Plants
- Warm lighting
- Quiet atmosphere

Technology remains nearly invisible.

---

# The Steward

The Steward is already present.

After a brief pause, the Steward simply says:

> Welcome.
>
> How can we help?

The cursor becomes available.

Voice interaction becomes available.

No additional interface competes for attention.

---

# Living Home Interaction

The environment itself becomes the interface.

Examples include:

- Walls displaying opportunities.
- Tables becoming planning surfaces.
- Bookshelves presenting learning materials.
- Windows reflecting time, weather, and season.

When inactive, every object returns to being part of the home.

---

# Motion Principles

Motion should always feel:

- Calm
- Intentional
- Architectural
- Natural

Never distracting.

Never theatrical.

---

# Audio Principles

Ambient audio should evolve naturally.

Examples include:

- Fire
- Rain
- Birds
- Wind
- Soft music
- Quiet activity elsewhere in the home

The goal is to communicate life without demanding attention.

---

# Accessibility

The Welcome Experience must support:

- Reduced motion
- Screen readers
- Keyboard navigation
- Skip on repeat visits
- Immediate access when needed

Accessibility is mandatory.

---

# Success Criteria

At the end of the Welcome Experience, members should naturally feel:

- I am welcome.
- I belong here.
- Someone is ready to help.
- I know what to do next.

The experience succeeds when members feel relief rather than amazement.

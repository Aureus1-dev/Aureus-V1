# Member Journey

Status: Permanent

Authority: Frontend Blueprint

Governed By:

- AXC-000 — Experience Constitution
- AXC-003 — Stewardship Interaction Canon
- AXC-007 — Interaction Canon
- FOUNDATION-003 — Canon Hierarchy

---

# Purpose

This blueprint defines how Aureus guides members through their personal journey.

A journey is not a checklist.

It is not a workflow.

It is the living representation of a person's progress toward flourishing.

Every journey should help members understand where they are, where they are going, why it matters, and what their next meaningful step should be.

---

# Member Outcome

Every member should naturally feel:

- I know where I am.
- I know what comes next.
- I understand why it matters.
- I am making meaningful progress.
- I am never walking alone.

The Journey should replace confusion with direction.

---

# Experience Flow

1. Steward understands the member.
2. Current situation is identified.
3. Long-term destination is clarified.
4. Milestones are created.
5. Next step is recommended.
6. Progress is reviewed.
7. Journey evolves as the member grows.

Journeys are living.

They are never considered permanently complete.

---

# Journey Structure

Every journey consists of:

- Purpose
- Current Reality
- Destination
- Milestones
- Tasks
- Opportunities
- Resources
- Steward Guidance
- Reflection
- Progress History

Every element should contribute toward flourishing.

---

# Navigation

Members should always understand:

- Current stage
- Completed milestones
- Recommended next action
- Available opportunities
- Remaining path

Navigation should reduce uncertainty.

---

# Progress

Progress should emphasize:

- Growth
- Consistency
- Stewardship
- Learning
- Reflection

Never streaks.

Never addiction mechanics.

Never artificial urgency.

---

# Steward Guidance

The Steward should explain:

- Why the next step matters.
- How it contributes to the larger journey.
- What alternatives exist.
- What support is available.

Members remain responsible for every decision.

---

# Accessibility

Support:

- Voice navigation
- Keyboard navigation
- Screen readers
- Adjustable typography
- Reduced motion
- High contrast

Every member deserves equal access to their journey.

---

# Performance Expectations

Journeys should load immediately.

Progress should synchronize reliably.

Historical information should remain easily accessible.

---

# Error Handling

If synchronization fails:

Protect member progress.

If recommendations fail:

Continue displaying the existing journey.

Members should never lose confidence in their progress.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Implementation is complete when:

- Members understand their journey.
- The next step is always clear.
- Progress feels meaningful.
- Accessibility standards are met.
- Canonical principles are preserved.

---

# Future Expansion

Future versions may include:

- Family journeys
- Team journeys
- Community journeys
- Steward-guided reviews
- Life timeline visualization
- Adaptive journey planning

Every addition must strengthen stewardship rather than increase complexity.

---

# Constitutional Reminder

A journey exists to help people move forward.

Every meaningful step brings someone closer to flourishing.

Helping people flourish.

Forever.

# Product-First Convergence Thesis

**Status:** Founder-directed execution thesis  
**Effective direction:** 2026-08-13  
**Scope:** Aureus-V1, Aureus-Foundry, and Aureus-Library  
**Primary objective:** Ship a genuinely useful business-facing product while preserving the member-facing product and existing safety constraints.

## 1. Decision

Aureus will stop spending the active product cycle on succession, electoral systems, permanent institutional tiers, ownership transfer, constitutional office design, and theoretical allocation of future power.

Existing constitutional, privacy, security, consent, accessibility, evidence, human-approval, and anti-abuse safeguards remain in force. They are a floor, not the active feature backlog. No product work may silently weaken them.

The active work is the product:

1. what a visitor sees and can do;
2. what a member sees and can do;
3. what a business sees and can do;
4. whether the experience works on real deployed infrastructure;
5. whether Aureus can truthfully help, capture a consented opportunity, and complete an accountable handoff; and
6. whether the system can be built, operated, observed, corrected, and paid for.

## 2. Operational stewardship

For this phase, stewardship advancement is an employment and responsibility system:

- a person chooses to do the work;
- completes applicable training;
- demonstrates competence;
- receives only the access needed for the role;
- performs work against observable standards;
- is reviewed using evidence and outcomes;
- receives greater responsibility and compensation when earned; and
- can lose access or responsibility when standards are not met.

This does not by itself grant institutional ownership, constitutional authority, voting power, or succession rights. Those questions remain preserved for later work and do not block product delivery.

## 3. The three repositories

Exactly three core repositories remain.

### Aureus-V1 — the product and runtime

V1 owns everything a person or business actually uses:

- public, guest, member, business, and steward interfaces;
- authentication, identity, consent, permissions, and tenant isolation;
- conversations, voice, memory, intake, lead capture, and handoffs;
- organization onboarding and the business console;
- operational state, outcomes, billing-facing records, and audit references;
- the runtime orchestrator that decides when to answer, ask, retrieve, propose, or escalate; and
- deployment, health, release evidence, and usability verification.

### Aureus-Foundry — governed intelligence production

Foundry owns bounded creation and review machinery:

- work-order compilation;
- provider and model routing;
- adversarial review and evaluation;
- consequence and data classification;
- scoped approval for consequential work;
- cost ceilings, retries, circuit breakers, and kill switches;
- evidence, disagreement, feedback, correction candidates, and replay; and
- release gates for intelligence artifacts.

Foundry does not own the user interface, product database, business CRM, member record, constitutional Canon, or final human authority.

### Aureus-Library — trusted knowledge and provenance

Library owns:

- governed Knowledge Objects;
- theses, evidence, decisions, definitions, standards, gaps, and provenance;
- lifecycle, currentness, supersession, access classification, and exact revisions;
- deterministic release manifests; and
- a read-only retrieval contract for consumers.

Library does not execute product actions, authorize itself, mutate V1, or let retrieved text become instructions.

## 4. Product thesis

The first commercial product is a web-first, tenant-isolated Business Steward for one initial vertical: kitchen-and-bath remodelers.

Its first complete promise is:

> A prospective customer asks a real question. The Steward gives a grounded answer from the business's approved knowledge, asks only what is necessary, captures contact information only with consent, preserves the conversation, and hands a useful, attributable opportunity to the correct human.

The first complete flow is:

> answer → clarify → consented lead → human handoff → visible outcome

This is not yet a general autonomous agency, full CRM, video studio, phone-first sales team, autonomous salesperson, or 3D production platform. Those remain possible extensions after the first flow is reliable and commercially useful.

Phone continuity, missed-call recovery, SMS continuation, and same-memory phone-to-web handoff belong in the full Business V1, but they do not block the first sellable web pilot.

## 5. Member and public product rule

Business focus does not revoke the help-first member product.

- The Hall remains directly usable without an account gate.
- “How can we help?” remains the public front door.
- Existing consent, urgent-help, truthful-capability, voice, safety, resource-verification, and escalation behavior remains protected by regression tests.
- Product changes must not introduce business branding, lead capture, or tenant data into the general Aureus member experience.
- No cross-tenant or business-to-member data leakage is acceptable.

## 6. Guardrails that remain release-blocking

Only governance necessary to operate responsibly remains in the active product gate:

- tenant and subject identity;
- least-privilege authorization;
- explicit consent and purpose limitation;
- no consequential final action without scoped human authority;
- prompt-injection containment;
- Library content treated as untrusted data;
- no model self-approval or agreement-as-proof;
- data minimization, retention, correction, export, and deletion behavior;
- accessibility;
- spend ceilings and provider kill switches;
- attributable audit records;
- exact-deployment evidence; and
- Founder walkthrough before external use.

## 7. Thesis-before-build rule

Create or update a thesis before a decision or build only when the decision is evidence-sensitive, expensive to reverse, legally or ethically consequential, cross-repository, or likely to create a long-lived product contract.

Do not write a thesis for routine implementation details already controlled by an approved contract and acceptance test. Theses live in the Library after governed admission; proposed working theses may begin in the repository doing the work, clearly labeled non-authoritative, and later be submitted to the Library as candidates.

## 8. Definition of product-first success

The phase succeeds when:

1. the three repositories expose compatible, versioned contracts;
2. V1 can safely retrieve approved business knowledge and invoke governed Foundry work;
3. one kitchen-and-bath remodeler can configure and preview its Steward;
4. a guest can complete the answer-to-handoff flow without an Aureus account;
5. the business can see, understand, assign, and close the resulting opportunity;
6. the Founder can complete the flow on mobile and desktop at the exact deployed SHA;
7. automated and human evidence show no cross-tenant leak, false capability, inaccessible critical path, or unapproved consequential action; and
8. the pilot can be sold and operated without pretending unfinished capabilities exist.

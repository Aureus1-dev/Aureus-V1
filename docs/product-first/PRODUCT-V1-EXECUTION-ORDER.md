Deliver:

### PF-005 — Business knowledge workspace

Deliver:

- tenant-owned source records;
- approved/under-review/rejected lifecycle;

- service, FAQ, policy, pricing-boundary, geography, qualification, and escalation knowledge types;

- no claim of legal advice or truth from upload alone;

The Founder completes, on mobile and desktop:

## Explicitly deferred

The following are not first-pilot blockers:

- succession and permanent governance design;
- member elections and institutional voting tiers;
- broad constitutional consolidation;
- autonomous consequential action;
- full CRM replacement;
- autonomous sales closing;
- video studio and B-roll management;
- 3D room design/rendering;
- national multi-vertical launch;
- public member launch beyond the existing controlled pathway.

Deferred does not mean rejected. Each item returns only after the answer-to-handoff product is reliable and evidence shows it should be next.
